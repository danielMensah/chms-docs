**Rhema** lets you ask about your church's records in your own words. You type a question, Rhema works out which report answers it and which filters to use, and Bethel ChMS runs that report for you, the same way the [report builder](/guides/reports) does. The answer shows how Rhema read your question, how many people match, and the first few rows. You can change any filter, ask a follow-up, or open the full report.

Rhema uses AI, a computer service that reads plain language, to understand your question. The AI never sees your records and never works out the numbers. Every number in an answer comes from your own reports.

Rhema only reads. It never edits members, changes tags or sends messages.

<Note>
Rhema is included in your plan at no extra cost, and the Bethel ChMS team switches it on for each church on request. If you're an Org Owner or Org Admin and you don't see **Rhema** in your sidebar, contact [hello@bethelware.app](mailto:hello@bethelware.app). See [Feature availability by plan](/guides/feature-availability-by-plan#rhema).
</Note>

<Info>
**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin. Branch Admins, Editors, Treasurers and Care Managers don't see Rhema and can't open it.
</Info>

## Open Rhema

- **On a computer:** **Rhema** is the first item in the sidebar, above **Dashboard**, with a small **AI** label beside it.
- **On a tablet held upright:** **Rhema** is the first shortcut in the rail.
- **On a phone:** **Rhema** is the middle tab in the tab bar, between **Members** and **Events**. While Rhema is switched on, **Visitors** moves into **More**.

See [Find your way around](/getting-started/find-your-way-around) for more on the sidebar, rail and tab bar.

If Rhema isn't switched on for your church and someone opens a link to it, they see **Rhema isn't available right now**, with **Go back** and **Contact us** buttons.

## Ask a question

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start from the Rhema page">
    A new chat opens on **What would you like to know?**, with an example of how Rhema turns a question into filters and four suggested questions you can tap, such as "Who missed the most Sunday services in September?".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your question">
    Type into the box at the bottom, which reads **Ask about your church**. Ask the way you would ask a colleague, for example "Visitors from last month with no follow-up yet". Press **Shift** and **Enter** together for a new line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Press **Enter**, or click the arrow button. While Rhema works, you see **Working on it** with three steps: **Reading your question**, **Choosing a report** and **Running the report**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your question stays at the top of the answer. The words Rhema used are highlighted, and any words it couldn't use have a wavy underline.

## Read the answer

An answer is a card with these parts:

- **The report name and one sentence** saying what the answer shows, for example "Members ranked by how many Sunday services they missed in September."
- **Filter chips**: one small label for each filter, grouping and sort order Rhema used, for example **1–30 Sep 2026**, **Sunday Service** and **Most absences first**. Point at a chip to light up the words in your question it came from.
- **A note about words Rhema couldn't use**, if any. For example: Rhema couldn't use "men" with the Attendance Frequency report, so the answer isn't filtered by it.
- **The number of matches**, for example **42 members**.
- **The first 8 rows**, with the person's name in the first column. On a small screen, swipe the table sideways to see more columns. If nobody matches, the table reads **Nobody matches these filters.**
- **Buttons** to open, save or download the answer, and a line such as **Showing 8 of 42**, **Showing all 5** or **Showing the only result**. See [Open, save or download an answer](#open-save-or-download-an-answer).

### Solid and dashed chips

A solid chip came from words in your question. A dashed chip is something Rhema chose because your question didn't say, for example a sort order. Point at a dashed chip to see **Rhema chose this because your question didn't say**. Once you change a dashed chip yourself, it turns solid.

### Events with the same name in several branches

If more than one branch has an event with the same name, such as Sunday Service, and your question doesn't name a branch, Rhema answers for one branch's event and says which under the chips, for example "London Central's Sunday Service only". To choose the branch, name it in your question or change the **Event** chip.

## Ask a follow-up

Within a chat, Rhema builds on the last answer. Ask "Now just the youth" after "Who missed the most Sunday services in September?", and Rhema keeps September, Sunday Service and the sort order, and adds your youth age group.

Follow-ups can add, change or remove filters, for example:

- "Same for August" changes only the dates.
- "Only the ones who came twice or less" adds a filter.
- "Sort by lowest attendance" changes only the order.
- "All events" or "all branches" removes that filter.

In the new answer, the chips that are new or changed are highlighted, and the count moves from the old total to the new one. If you ask a new, complete question instead, Rhema starts again from that question. Rhema builds on the most recent answer in the chat, skipping any cards that say it couldn't answer.

## Attendance over time

Questions about how attendance has changed, such as "How has Sunday attendance changed since Easter?", get a bar chart instead of a list. There is one bar for each week, or for each month if you ask by month. Weeks start on a Sunday.

Above the chart are the **Highest week**, the **Lowest week** and the average for the most recent weeks, for example **Last 4 weeks, average**. The highest bar is a darker colour. A note under the chart says the figures come from your Attendance Summary report, not from the AI.

- Click **Show as table** to see each week's total as a list, and **Show as chart** to go back.
- A chart shows up to 52 weeks or 12 months. For a longer period, it shows the most recent ones and says so, for example **Showing the last 52 weeks.**
- If your question doesn't give dates, the chart covers the last 52 weeks.
- If all the dates fall in one bar, for example a single Sunday, you get one total instead of a chart.
- Attendance totals include members and visitors. Ask for "members only" or "visitors" to count one of them.

A chart's **Group by** chip can switch between **By Week** and **By Month**. To turn a chart into a list, or a list into a chart, ask it as a new question.

## What Rhema can answer

Rhema answers from five of your reports:

| Report | Questions like |
| --- | --- |
| **Member Directory** | "Members from Ghana who joined this year", "Female members in the 13-19 age group", "Baptised members" |
| **Attendance Frequency** | "Who missed the most Sunday services in September?", "Members who came fewer than 2 times last month", "Who has missed the last 3 Sundays in a row?" |
| **Visitor Follow-up List** | "Visitors from last month with no follow-up yet" |
| **Ministry Roster** | "Who is in the choir?" |
| **Attendance Summary** | "How has Sunday attendance changed since Easter?" (a chart) |

Rhema can filter by:

- **Dates.** Dates can be fixed, such as "September" or "1 to 15 March", or move with time, such as "last month", "this year" or "the last 6 weeks". "September" means the most recent September, and "since Easter" runs from the most recent Easter Sunday to today.
- **Your church's own names** for branches, events, age groups, ministries and tags. Age words such as "youth" or "children" pick the age group that fits best.
- **Member status** (Newcomer, Active or Inactive), **gender** and **nationality**.
- **How often someone came or was absent**, including absences in a row, such as "missed the last 3 Sundays in a row". These use the **Attended**, **Absent** and **Absent in a row** filters.
- **Custom fields**, the extra fields your church adds to members and visitors, such as "baptised". These work on the Member Directory and Visitor Follow-up List. See [Custom fields](/guides/organisation-settings#custom-fields).
- **Follow-up status** from [Care](/guides/care) and **visit counts**, for visitors.

A few things to know about absences:

- Questions about who missed services count newcomers and active members. To include inactive members, say so, for example "including inactive members".
- Absences only count from the day a member was added to Bethel ChMS, so a new member doesn't show missed services from before they joined.

## When Rhema can't answer

When a question needs something your reports don't hold, Rhema says why in plain words. Where it helps, it suggests a question to try (tap **Try:** to ask it) or a page to go to.

| What you asked | What Rhema says | Where to go instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| To do something, such as send a message or change a tag | **Rhema only reads your records** | Open a report and use its bulk actions |
| Giving by person | **Rhema can't answer this yet**. Giving is recorded as a total for each category, so there is no list of givers. | **See giving by category** |
| Why something happened | **Rhema can show what changed, not why** | A question about what changed |
| Pastoral or confidential care | **Rhema can't see this**. Care records are never shared with Rhema. | **Go to Care** |
| Who stopped coming, comparing two periods for each person | **Rhema can't compare two periods for each person yet** | **Open Member care** |
| Anything else your reports don't hold, such as birthdays or addresses | **Rhema can't find that in your records** | A question your reports can answer |

If Rhema can tell your question is about your records but not which report fits, it says **Rhema isn't sure which report answers that** and offers three questions to pick from. Naming members, attendance, visitors or ministries in your question helps.

## Change or remove a filter

You can change an answer without asking again.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click a chip">
    Click the filter chip you want to change. A small panel opens with the choices for that filter, with the current one selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a new value">
    - **Date range** offers **This week**, **Last week**, **This month**, **Last month**, **Last 3 months**, **This quarter**, **This year**, **Last year** and **Last 30 days**, each with the dates it covers today. Choose **Custom range** to pick a **From** and **To** date.
    - Number filters, such as **Absent** or **Absent in a row**, have **At least** and **At most** boxes. Whole numbers only.
    - Filters that can hold several values, such as tags, change to the one value you pick.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply or remove">
    Click **Apply** to run the report again with your change, or **Remove** to drop the filter. The chip you changed is highlighted, and the count updates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Sort** and **Group by** chips can be changed but not removed, because a list always has an order. If a chip's panel says **Rhema can't change this here. Remove it, or change it in the full report.**, you can remove it or use **Open full report**. The custom fields chip works this way.

After a change, the sentence at the top of the card lists the report and its filters. If Rhema can't use a change, you see **Rhema couldn't use that filter. Try another.** and the answer stays as it was.

## Open, save or download an answer

Each answer has these buttons:

- **Open full report** opens the report builder with the answer's report, columns and filters already set, so you can see every row, add columns, export a PDF and use bulk actions. It opens in the same tab. To come back, open **Rhema** and pick the chat from **Chats**.
- **Save as template** opens the usual **Save Template** dialog, with a name and description filled in from the answer. Change them if you like, then click **Save**. You'll see a message such as **Saved "Attendance Frequency: September, Sunday Service" to your templates**, with **View** to open your templates. The template appears under **My Templates** on the **Templates** tab of [Reports](/guides/reports#use-templates), with a **Rhema** badge.
- **Download CSV** downloads every row of a list answer, not only the first 8, the same as exporting from the report builder. Chart answers don't have this button. Use **Open full report** to export them.

### Dates in a saved template

If the answer's dates move with time, such as "last month", the **Save Template** dialog asks how the template should treat them under **Dates**:

- **Move with time: last month** is chosen to begin with. Each time you open the template, it shows last month as of that day.
- **Keep these dates** shows the dates the answer covered, for example **Keep these dates: 1–30 Sep 2026**. The template always shows the same period.

If your question named fixed dates, such as "September", the template keeps those dates and the dialog doesn't ask.

## Chats

Rhema keeps your questions and answers as **chats**, so you can come back to them. Each chat is named after its first question.

- **On a wide screen,** your chats are listed in a column on the left of the Rhema page.
- **On a smaller screen,** click **Chats** at the top of the page to open the list.

Chats are grouped into **Today**, **This week** and **Earlier**, newest first. Click a chat to open it. Bethel ChMS runs each report in it again, so the numbers are always up to date. You can carry on asking follow-ups in a reopened chat.

To start again, click **New chat**.

### Delete a chat

Point at a chat in the list and click the bin icon (on a touch screen it's always shown). The chat is deleted straight away, and you see **Chat deleted** with **Undo** for 5 seconds. Templates you saved from a chat stay in Reports.

To delete all your chats at once, use **Delete all my chats** in the Rhema settings. See [Rhema settings](#rhema-settings).

### Who can see your chats

Your chats are private to you. Nobody else in your church can see them, including other Org Owners and Org Admins.

Each chat is deleted 90 days after the last question asked in it. The bottom of the list reminds you: **Chats are deleted after 90 days. Templates you save stay in Reports.**

## Your privacy

Rhema uses AI from an outside company to read your question. The line under the box where you type names the company your church's questions go to, and says what it sees: your question and the names of your reports, events and age groups, never member records.

**What is sent to the AI company:**

- Your question. If you type a person's name in your question, the name is sent as part of it.
- The names of Bethel ChMS's own reports, columns and filters.
- Your church's own names for branches, events, age groups and ministries.
- Your tag names and custom field names, including the choices in your custom fields, unless you switch this off. See [Rhema settings](#rhema-settings).
- Today's date and your church's time zone, so it can work out dates like "last month".
- For a follow-up, the filters of the answer it builds on.

**What is never sent:** member and visitor records, people's names from your records, counts or rows, and care records. Bethel ChMS runs the report itself, with your own access, after the AI has replied.

### Unanswered questions

When Rhema can't answer a question, Bethel ChMS keeps a copy of the question for 90 days, with names, email addresses and phone numbers removed, so the Bethel ChMS team can see which questions to support next. It isn't linked to you or to your chat. Cards that can't answer remind you: **Rhema keeps unanswered questions, with names removed, to decide what to add next.**

## Rhema settings

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin.

While Rhema is switched on, a **Rhema** card with an **On** badge appears in [Organisation settings](/guides/organisation-settings#rhema). It has four rows:

- **Who can use Rhema** says that Org Owners and Org Admins can use it, and that Rhema only answers from reports the person can already see.
- **Send tag and custom field names to Rhema** is on to begin with, so Rhema understands questions like "members tagged Choir" or "baptised members". Switch it off if any tag, custom field or custom field choice is sensitive, for example a support group. With it off, a question that mentions a tag shows **Tag names aren't shared with Rhema for your church.**, and words about custom fields are listed as words Rhema couldn't use. Rhema never sees who has a tag or what anyone's custom fields say, whichever way it's set. The switch applies to your whole church.
- **Chat history** has **Delete all my chats**, which deletes your own chats and nobody else's. You see **All chats deleted** with **Undo**. If you have none, you see **You have no chats to delete.**
- **Switching Rhema off** explains that Rhema is switched off by the Bethel ChMS team. Click **contact support** to send an email. Your saved reports are kept.

## Limits and messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Rhema needs a short break**: You've asked a lot of questions in a short time. Try again in a minute. | Each person can ask up to 30 questions a minute. |
| **Rhema needs a short break**: Your church has asked Rhema as many questions as it can today. Try again tomorrow. | Your church has reached its daily limit, which is 500 questions a day. |
| **Ask a question in one or two sentences.** | Questions can be up to 500 characters. Your question is put back in the box so you can shorten it. |
| **Rhema can't reach its AI service** | The AI company didn't reply. Your question is still there. Click **Try again** in a moment. |
| **Rhema couldn't run the report** | Rhema read your question but the report didn't run. Click **Try again** in a moment. |
| **Rhema isn't set up on this environment.** | Rhema can't answer questions here yet. Your reports still work in **Reports**. Contact support if you see this. |

When an answer card turns amber, it has **Try again**, which asks the same question again, and **Go to Reports**, so you can build the report yourself in the [report builder](/guides/reports).
