Bethel ChMS has one plan, called **Standard**. Every feature in the plan is included for every organisation, and the only limit is the number of branches you can create. Your **organisation** is your church network as a whole, and a **branch** is one of its churches or sites.

Porter, the phone app for your service teams, is the one exception. It is not part of the plan, and the Bethel ChMS team switches it on for your church on request. See [Porter](#porter) below.

Being included in the plan does not mean everyone sees a feature. Bethel ChMS still hides features from roles that are not allowed to use them. For example, **Branch Admin** and **Editor** users do not see **Gifting & Donations** or **Update Campaigns**. See [Roles and permissions](/guides/roles-and-permissions).

## Branch limit

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin.

Branches are the only usage limit in the Standard plan. New organisations start with a limit of 1 branch, and the Bethel ChMS support team can raise it for you.

You can check your usage in two places:

- The **Branches** page shows a meter such as **1 of 2 branches used**, with a **Standard** badge and a line telling you how many more branches are available.
- The **Plan & Usage** card in **Organisation Settings** shows **Branches** as used against the limit, for example `1 / 2`.

When you reach the limit, the meter on the **Branches** page turns amber and reads **You've reached your plan's branch limit. Contact us to create another branch.**, with a **Contact us** button.

If you click **Create Branch** at the limit, Bethel ChMS shows **Branch limit reached** before the form opens. The message tells you how many branches your Standard plan includes and how many your church is using. It has two buttons:

- **Dismiss** closes the message.
- **Contact us** opens an email to `hello@bethelware.app` with the subject **Increase branches limit on Standard plan**, ready for you to send.

No branch is created until your limit is raised.

<Note>
You pay for each branch. See [Billing and subscription](/guides/billing-and-subscription) for pricing and how adding or removing branches affects your bill.
</Note>

## Porter

[Porter](/guides/porter) is the part of Bethel ChMS for church workers and volunteers, used on a phone. Workers check in when they arrive, take out and return equipment, and report faults.

Porter is switched off for every organisation to begin with. It is not part of the Standard plan, so you cannot switch it on yourself. To use it, contact `hello@bethelware.app` and ask the Bethel ChMS team to switch Porter on for your organisation.

Until Porter is switched on:

- The **Users** page shows no Porter options.
- There is no **Porter** item in the sidebar.
- If someone opens Porter, they are told **Porter isn't switched on for your church**.

Once Porter is on, an **Org Owner** or **Org Admin** can give members Porter access from the **Users** page. See [User management](/guides/user-management) and [Porter](/guides/porter).

## Feature unavailable page

Bethel ChMS has a page for features that are not available to your organisation. Because every plan feature is included, you should rarely see it.

If you do, the page names the feature, explains that it is included in your Standard plan but isn't available right now, and offers to help you get set up. It has two buttons:

- **Go back** returns you to the page you came from.
- **Contact us** opens your email app, addressed to `hello@bethelware.app`.

## What is not self-serve

Some changes are made by the Bethel ChMS support team rather than in the app.

Contact support when you need to:

- Increase your branch limit.
- Switch Porter on for your organisation.
- Have **Find by name** switched on for your church, if you do not see the **Quick Find** setting on your branch. See [Find by name (Quick Find)](/guides/quick-find).
- Resolve a feature unavailable message.
- Update billing details for an account that is billed manually.

Support emails go to `hello@bethelware.app`.
