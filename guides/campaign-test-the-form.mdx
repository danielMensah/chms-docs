**Test the form** lets you try an [update campaign](/guides/update-campaigns) before you launch it. An update campaign asks members to check and update their own details through a short form. Test the form opens a blank copy of that form, with the same questions and controls members will see, so you can check that the right questions appear and that conditions reveal follow-up questions at the right moment. Nothing you enter in the preview is saved.

<Info>
**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin. Test the form is part of Update Campaigns.
</Info>

## Open the preview

You can open the preview from three places:

- **In the campaign builder.** Once you have saved the draft at least once, **Test the form** appears in the bar at the bottom of the builder, next to **Save draft**. On a phone it is labelled **Test**. You can test as you build, before the rest of the setup is finished. On smaller screens, the button is also at the bottom of the **Email preview** sheet.
- **On a draft's campaign page.** Click the **Test the form** tile (**Test form** on a phone), or open the **⋯** menu (**More campaign actions**) and choose **Test the form**.
- **On the Campaigns page.** Open the **⋯** menu on a draft campaign's row and choose **Test the form**.

<Note>
If you do not see **Test the form** in the builder, click **Save draft** first. The button appears once the draft has been saved.
</Note>

## The preview page

The top of the page shows **Test the form** with the campaign name underneath. You also see:

- **Back to campaign**, which returns you to the campaign page.
- The campaign's status, such as **Draft**, on tablets and larger screens.
- **Edit campaign**, which opens the campaign builder so you can make changes. On a phone it shows as a pencil icon.

A banner stays pinned to the top of the page the whole time, reminding you that nothing you enter is saved and that the preview is there to check your questions and conditions.

## What the preview shows

The preview uses the same form members receive, with the same controls: **Day**, **Month** and **Year** boxes for dates, buttons to tap for choices, a searchable list for **Nationality**, and the **Save my details** button at the bottom of the screen.

Your chosen standard fields, custom fields and tags appear in the order you set in the builder. Conditions reveal follow-up questions exactly as you configured them, and required fields are checked in the same way, so you see the same messages a member would if they left something out.

Every field starts blank, because the preview is not tied to a real member, so there are no existing details to fill in. Every tag starts at **No, not me**.

<Tip>
The preview shows the campaign as it was last saved. Click **Save draft** before you open the preview from the builder. Opening the preview takes you out of the builder, and changes to the name, message, dates or switches that you have not saved are lost.
</Tip>

## Try the form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fill in the fields">
    Enter answers as a member would. Change your answers to the trigger questions and watch follow-up questions appear and disappear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the preview">
    Click **Save my details**. If a required field is empty, the form tells you, just as it would for a member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the thank-you screen">
    A badge says you are in test mode and nothing was saved. Below it is the same thank-you screen members see, with a **What you updated** list built from what you typed. If the campaign requires review, the screen also says that a staff member will review the changes shortly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run through it again">
    Click **Test again** to clear the form back to blank. Before you submit, you can also click **Reset preview** below the form to start over.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Screen readers announce that the preview was submitted and nothing was saved, and announce again when the form is reset.

## Nothing is saved

The preview cannot save anything. Submitting it never creates a submission, never changes a member record, and never affects the campaign's response counts or review queue, however many times you submit.

Because of that, you can test with realistic details, hand the preview to a colleague, or run through the form as many times as you like. There is nothing to clean up afterwards.

<Tip>
If a condition does not reveal its follow-up questions when you expect, click **Edit campaign**, find the condition's card in the **Conditions** section and check its trigger answers. Then test the form again. See [Show follow-up questions with conditions](/guides/update-campaigns#show-follow-up-questions-with-conditions).
</Tip>
