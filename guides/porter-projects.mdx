A **project** is anything with a date and a list of jobs: the Carol Service, a youth weekend, the hall clean. Each **task** on it is either given to **a team** (anyone on it can claim it) or to **named people**. Projects live in Porter under **Tasks → Projects**; everyone's own tasks are under **Tasks → My tasks**.

<Note>
Projects and tasks are part of Porter. Someone can be given a task only if they have Porter access, and a team task can be claimed only by people on that team in Bethel ChMS.
</Note>

## Who can do what

| Role | Create and plan projects | Edit, close or delete a project | Work on tasks | See ratings, retro answers and the AI summary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Porter admin | Every branch | Any project | Yes | Yes |
| Team leader or assistant leader with Porter access | Branches where they lead a team; their own teams' tasks | Projects they created | Yes | Projects they created |
| Porter worker | No | No | Their own and their teams' tasks | Their own rating only |

Every Porter user can read the projects of their own branch, and any project they have a task on.

## Plan a project

1. Go to **Tasks → Projects** and press **New project**, or open a service on the **Rota** and press **Plan this service**. A service's project follows the service if it moves.
2. Press **Add tasks**, at the top of the project. It opens over the page, as a sheet from the bottom on a phone.
3. Choose the **Due** date for all of them (it starts at the project's first day; tasks are due by 18:00 on that day) and where the rest go (**Everything else goes to**: a team, or **No team**). Porter picks the project's only team, or the last team you used on this project.
4. Type one task per line, and press **Enter** for the next. Put notes after the title with a colon and a space; a spaced dash ( - ) or a pasted tab starts the notes too. To give one task to a team or a person, type **@**: Porter suggests the project's teams and the people you can give tasks to as you type; pick one with the arrow keys and **Enter** or **Tab**, or tap it. Put any **@** before the notes: an **@** after them is part of the note. For example:

   ```text
   Set out 200 chairs: rows of 20, aisle down the middle
   Livestream check @Media
   ```

   makes two tasks: *Set out 200 chairs* with the note *rows of 20, aisle down the middle* for the team the rest go to, and *Livestream check* for Media. A colon without a space after it stays in the title (*Doors open 10:30*). A note is one line; for longer notes, or one task for several named people, switch to **One task with notes**.
5. Check the list beside the lines (on a phone, tap **Check them**): it shows every task with its note and who it goes to. Until you pick where everything else goes, a line without an **@** shows **pick a team**; someone who already has five open tasks on the project shows **5 tasks already** with **Assign anyway**.
6. Press **Add N tasks** (or **⌘ Enter** on a Mac, **Ctrl Enter** elsewhere). Up to 50 lines go in at once, all together or none. The due date and the team stay set for the next batch.
7. When the plan is ready, press **Notify N people**. Nobody hears about a task before you do, so you can build the plan quietly.

**Your lines are kept.** Until you add them, the lines are saved on this device as a draft for 14 days, even if the page reloads or you close it. **Add tasks** then shows **Draft, N lines**; open it and choose **Restore** or **Discard**. Closing with lines still in the box asks first: **Keep as draft**, **Discard** or **Keep editing**. Signing out deletes drafts from the device, so a shared church phone never shows your lines to the next person.

The project's facts sit at the top of the page: its dates, any **unclaimed**, **overdue** or **not told yet** counts, the progress bar, **Remind everyone outstanding**, and the **···** menu with **Edit**, **Copy project**, **Close project** and **Delete**. Finished tasks fold away under **Done, N tasks**, with the latest one (*Kwame finished Hang the banners 20 min ago*).

**Copy project** (in the **···** menu) starts next year's (or next week's) project from this one: the tasks come along with their due dates moved, and the people too if you keep them.

The **Board** (on a tablet or computer) shows the tasks as cards in columns: **Unclaimed** (nobody has it yet), your church's own columns, then **Done**. Switch between **List** and **Board** at the top of the page. Drag a card by its handle, or use its menu, to move it. Moving a card out of **Unclaimed** opens the task so you can name who has it; once it has people, it goes to the column you chose. When a project has cancelled tasks, tick **Show N cancelled** to see them in a dashed column at the end.

Porter remembers **List** or **Board**, and **Show N cancelled**, on each device: open any project and it looks the way you left it. A phone always shows the list.

Until your church sets its own, there is one column between Unclaimed and Done: **To do**. A Porter admin sets the columns in **Setup → Tasks → Board columns**, up to 8 (for example *To do*, *In progress*, *Ready*): tap one to rename or delete it, and use the arrows to change the order. Deleting a column moves its tasks to the one before it.

## Remind and chase

- **Remind** on a task, or **Remind everyone outstanding** on the project, sends a reminder to people who were told about their tasks, at most once an hour.
- Each task gets an automatic reminder before it's due (24 hours by default, set in **Setup → Tasks**) and one overdue notice the morning after.
- You hear when a task is finished, straight away or once a day (**Tell me when a task is done: Straight away | Once a day**, beside the tabs on the Projects page), unless you mute the project.
- Everything Porter sends you about tasks (new tasks, reminders, comments, tags and finished tasks) is also listed in **Notifications**. See [The Notifications tab](/guides/porter-alerts#the-notifications-tab).

## Keyboard shortcuts

On a computer or a tablet with a keyboard, press **?** anywhere in Porter (or the **?** button at the top of the page) to see every shortcut. In **Add tasks**, the keys are listed along the bottom of the dialog, and **All shortcuts** (or **Shortcuts** on a touch screen) opens the full list; buttons with a key show it beside their name.

| Where | Key | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Add tasks | **Enter** | A new line: one task per line |
| Add tasks | **⌘ Enter** on a Mac, **Ctrl Enter** elsewhere | Adds all the lines, from any field |
| Add tasks | **@** | Suggests teams and people; **↑ ↓** to choose, **Enter** or **Tab** to put the name in |
| Add tasks | **:** | Notes after the title |
| Add tasks | **Esc** | Closes; lines you haven't added are kept as a draft |
| Project page | **A** | Opens Add tasks |
| Project page | **B** | Switches between List and Board |

**A** and **B** never fire while you're typing in a field, and not on a touch screen. To turn them off on a device, press **?** and switch off **Single-letter keys on this device**.

## Work on a task

Tasks show on Porter's Home (under **Needs you** and **Coming up**) and under **My tasks**, by when they're due. Open one to **Claim** it (a team task), mark it **Done** (with an optional note and up to three photos), **Hand back** with a reason, or ask a question in its comments. On a computer the task's notes and comments are on the left and its due date, project, team, people and buttons on the right; on a phone **Hand back** and **Done** stay at the bottom of the screen.

When a project asks for it, finishing a task asks **How did it go?**: a rating from 1 to 5 and an optional comment. Only the project's planner and the Porter admins see ratings.

### Tag someone in a comment

To bring someone into a task's conversation, type **@** in the comment box and start their name. Porter suggests the people who can open the task (never yourself). Use **↑** and **↓** to choose, then **Enter** or **Tab**, or tap the name, to put it in your comment. **Esc** closes the list and keeps what you typed. When there's someone you can tag, the box says **Type @ to tag someone. They're told straight away.**

When you post, everyone you tagged (up to ten people) is told straight away, by push notification, or by email if notifications aren't on. The notice reads, for example, *Grace Mensah tagged you on Set up the stage*, with the start of your comment, and it's listed in their **Notifications**.

- Someone you tag hears about the tag instead of the usual comment notice, never both.
- Being tagged lets them reply on that task, even if it isn't theirs, until the comment that tagged them is deleted.
- Tagged names show in blue in the comments.
- You can tag people, not teams.

## Close a project and ask how it went

When the project is over, choose **Close project** from its **···** menu. **Close and ask** sends everyone who worked on it two questions: *What went well?* and *What should we do differently?* They have 14 days to answer, from the notice or from **My tasks**. **Close without asking** just closes it. Closing stops reminders, claims and comments; the project stays readable. You can **Reopen** it for 30 days; answers are kept and nobody is asked twice. A project that has been asked, rated or answered can't be deleted, even reopened: close it instead.

The project's **Retro** shows the numbers (tasks done, done on time, overdue and unclaimed at close, handed back, the average rating), every answer and comment grouped by team, and who is still to answer.

## The AI summary

On the Retro, **Summarise** turns the answers and comments into a short summary: what went well, what to do differently, three suggestions for next time, and the themes. It needs a closed project and at least three answers or three comments. **Summarise again** appears only when someone has answered, or left or changed a comment, since the last summary.

This summary uses AI from an outside company, named on the card, for example "This summary uses AI from Anthropic. It sees task titles, counts and what people wrote with names removed, never member records."

What is sent: the project's name, dates and branch, the team names, the task titles and whether each was done, late or handed back, the counts, and the answers and comments, with your church's member and visitor names, email addresses and phone numbers taken out of every one of them. What is **never** sent: member records or who wrote what. The summary is seen by the planner and Porter admins only, is never put in a notice, and is deleted with the project.

To turn the AI summary off for your church, go to **Setup → Tasks** and switch off **AI retro summaries**.

## Privacy

Ratings, comments left when finishing a task and retro answers are seen only by Porter admins and the person who created the project. They are deleted 24 months after the project closes, along with completion notes and photos; the project, its tasks and when they were done stay as your church's history.
