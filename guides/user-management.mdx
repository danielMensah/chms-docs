Use the **Users** page to manage everyone who signs in to your organisation's apps. A **user** is someone with their own sign-in, such as a pastor, office administrator or finance volunteer. Your **organisation** is your church network, with all its branches, members and settings.

From the **Users** page you can add users, resend invitations, edit names, change roles, move users between branches, and deactivate or reactivate users. If your church uses **Porter**, the part of Bethel ChMS that workers and volunteers use on their phones, Org Owners and Org Admins also give, change and remove Porter access here.

<Info>
**Who can do this:** **Org Owner** and **Org Admin** can manage users across the organisation. **Branch Admins** can manage **Branch Admin** and **Editor** users in their own branch. Only **Org Owners** and **Org Admins** can give Porter access. **Editors**, **Treasurers** and **Care Managers** cannot open the **Users** page.
</Info>

## Open the Users page

In the sidebar, open **Organisation** and click **Users**. On a phone, tap **More** and find **Users** under **Organisation**.

Users are organisation-wide, so the list shows everyone in your organisation. Each user's role decides which branches they see. The list shows:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The person's name, with their email underneath. Your own row has a **You** badge. |
| **Role** | Their role, with their branch underneath, or **All branches** for Org Owners and Org Admins. |
| **Apps** | Which apps they use: **ChMS** for Bethel ChMS, and **Porter · Worker** or **Porter · Admin** when they have Porter. |
| **Member record** | For people with Porter, the member record their check-ins and equipment loans go on. |
| **Status** | Whether they are active, invited or deactivated. |

The **Apps** and **Member record** columns appear on wider screens. Click anywhere on a row to open that user's page. On a tablet or computer, click the **⋯** button at the end of a row for actions. On a phone, rows have no **⋯** button, so tap the row and use the user's page instead. Deactivated users appear dimmed.

<Note>
Branch Admins see everyone in the organisation, but can only make changes to **Branch Admin** and **Editor** users in their own branch.
</Note>

### Search and filter

- **Search:** type in **Search by name or email…** to find someone.
- **Status:** use the chips **All**, **Active**, **Invites** and **Deactivated**. Each shows a count.
- **Filters:** click **Filters** to filter by **Role**, and by **Branch** if you're an Org Owner or Org Admin. The button at the bottom shows how many users match, for example **Show 12 users**. Click it to see them, or click **Clear all** to start again.
- **Branch:** Org Owners and Org Admins on a tablet or computer also have an **All Branches** dropdown next to the search box.

Filters you've applied appear as chips next to the status chips. Click one to remove that filter.

## User statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Invite pending** | The person has been invited but has not accepted the invitation yet. |
| **Active** | The person has accepted the invitation and can sign in. |
| **Deactivated** | The person cannot sign in until you reactivate them. |
| **Invite expired** | Porter invitations only. The emailed link has expired. Resend the invitation to send a new link. |

## Add a user

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    On the **Users** page, click **Add user**. On a phone, the button shows just an icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their email">
    Enter the person's **Email**. This is the address their invitation goes to, and the email they'll sign in with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the apps (when Porter is switched on)">
    If your church uses Porter and you're an Org Owner or Org Admin, choose under **Apps**:

    - **Bethel ChMS**: members, events and attendance on the web.
    - **Porter**: worker check-in and equipment loans on the phone.
    - **Both apps**: Bethel ChMS on the web and Porter on the phone.

    One sign-in works for both apps, so someone with access to both uses the same email and password. If you don't see **Apps**, the form is for Bethel ChMS only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their Bethel ChMS details (when adding Bethel ChMS)">
    If you chose **Bethel ChMS** or **Both apps**, or the form has no **Apps** choice, enter a **Name** (at least 2 characters) and choose a **Role**:

    - Org Owners and Org Admins can choose **Org Admin**, **Branch Admin**, **Editor**, **Treasurer** or **Care Manager**.
    - Branch Admins can choose **Branch Admin** or **Editor**.

    Choose a **Branch** for **Branch Admin**, **Editor**, **Treasurer** or **Care Manager**. **Org Admin** users cover all branches and do not need one. If you're a Branch Admin, the **Branch** field is locked to your own branch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their Porter details (when adding Porter)">
    If you chose **Porter** or **Both apps**, choose a **Porter role**, **Worker** or **Admin**, then use **Link to member record** to pick the person's member record. Every Porter user must be linked to a member record, so their check-ins and equipment loans go on their own record rather than a duplicate. See [Porter](/guides/porter) for the full flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Click **Create user**. Bethel ChMS emails the person an invitation with an **Accept Invitation** button. They open it, set a password and they're in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When you choose **Treasurer** or **Care Manager**, the form shows a short reminder of what that role can access. For what each role can do, see [Roles and permissions](/guides/roles-and-permissions).

If the email is already in use, you'll see a message such as "A user with this email already exists" or "This user already exists in the organisation". To give Porter to someone who already uses Bethel ChMS, [edit their user](#edit-a-user) instead of adding them again.

For what the invited person sees, see [Invite your team](/getting-started/invite-users).

## Manage invitations

Click the **Invites** chip to see every pending invitation, for both Bethel ChMS and Porter. Porter invitations appear here only for Org Owners and Org Admins. Each row shows the **Invited person**, the **Status** with the date it was sent, and a **Resend** button.

### Resend an invitation

Do one of the following:

- In the **Invites** view, click **Resend** on the person's row.
- In the main list, click **⋯** on a pending user and choose **Resend invitation**.
- On the user's page, click **⋮** and choose **Resend invitation**.

Bethel ChMS sends a fresh email with a new link and confirms with "Invitation resent to" followed by their email address. An expired Porter invitation needs to be resent before it can be accepted.

Invitation links expire after a while. A Bethel ChMS invitation keeps showing **Invite pending** even after its link has expired, so if someone tells you their link no longer works, resend it.

Once someone has opened their Bethel ChMS invitation link, they show as **Active** and you can no longer resend it. If they never finished setting a password, ask them to use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. See [Sign in](/getting-started/sign-in).

### Cancel a Porter invitation

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin

In the **Invites** view, click **⋯** on a Porter invitation and choose **Cancel invite**. The emailed link stops working. You can invite the person again later.

## Give or remove Porter access

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, once Porter is switched on for your church

Porter access sits on the person's member record and covers the whole organisation. The **Users** page shows it in two ways:

- **Bethel ChMS users with Porter** have a **Porter · Worker** or **Porter · Admin** chip in the **Apps** column.
- **People with Porter only** appear as their own rows after the Bethel ChMS users. Their **Role** column shows **No ChMS role** with **Porter only** underneath, and clicking the row opens their member record. Filtering by any role hides these rows.

To give Porter to someone new, [add a user](#add-a-user) and choose **Porter** or **Both apps**.

To give Porter back to someone whose Porter access you removed earlier, [add a user](#add-a-user), choose **Porter**, link their member record and enter the email address they already sign in with. Their existing sign-in is linked as soon as you click **Create user**, so they keep their password.

To give Porter to an active Bethel ChMS user, open **Edit user**, tick **Porter** under **Apps**, choose a **Porter role** and link their member record, then click **Save changes**. Because their email is already confirmed, Porter applies as soon as you save. If their sign-in is already linked to a member record, that link is shown and can't be changed.

To remove Porter:

- **For a Porter-only person:** on a tablet or computer, click **⋯** on their row and choose **Remove Porter**, then confirm with **Remove Porter**. Their member record, past check-ins and sign-in stay, and you can give Porter back later.
- **For a Bethel ChMS user:** open **Edit user**, untick **Porter** and click **Save changes**. This also cancels a pending Porter invitation.

For what Porter users can do, see [Porter access levels](/guides/roles-and-permissions#porter-access-levels) and [Porter](/guides/porter).

## Edit a user

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin (for Branch Admin and Editor users in their own branch)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the edit form">
    Click **⋯** on an active user's row and choose **Edit**. Or open the user's page and click **Edit** (a pencil icon on a phone), or click **⋮** and choose **Edit role, branch and apps**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    In the **Edit user** sheet, update their **Name**, **Role** or **Branch**. Org Owners and Org Admins also see **Apps** when Porter is switched on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save changes**. Bethel ChMS confirms with "User updated successfully".
  </Step>
</Steps>

The following restrictions apply:

- You can edit active users. **Edit** does not appear for pending or deactivated users.
- You cannot change your own role or branch, but you can change your own name.
- Nobody can change the **Org Owner**'s role, and only the Org Owner can change their own name. To hand over the organisation, use [ownership transfer](/guides/roles-and-permissions#transferring-ownership).
- **Branch Admins** can edit **Branch Admin** and **Editor** users in their own branch only, and cannot move them to another branch.
- **Branch Admins** cannot assign the **Org Admin**, **Treasurer** or **Care Manager** roles.
- **Branch Admin**, **Editor**, **Treasurer** and **Care Manager** users must each belong to one branch.
- Under **Apps**, **Bethel ChMS** is always ticked for a Bethel ChMS user. To remove their Bethel ChMS access, [deactivate them](#deactivate-or-reactivate-a-user).

For when a role change reaches the person, see [How role changes take effect](/guides/roles-and-permissions#how-role-changes-take-effect).

## Deactivate or reactivate a user

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin (for Branch Admin and Editor users in their own branch)

Deactivate a user when they should no longer have access. Deactivation keeps their user record but blocks their sign-in. Because one sign-in opens both apps, a deactivated user can't sign in to Porter either.

To deactivate someone, click **⋯** on their row, or **⋮** on their user page, and choose **Deactivate**. Check the details in **Deactivate User** and click **Deactivate**. Bethel ChMS confirms with "User deactivated".

You cannot deactivate yourself. You also cannot deactivate the **Org Owner**.

To restore access, click the **Deactivated** chip, click **⋯** on the user (or **⋮** on their user page) and choose **Reactivate**. Confirm in **Reactivate User** by clicking **Reactivate**. The user regains access with their current role and branch, and Bethel ChMS confirms with "User reactivated".

## Row actions

On a tablet or computer, the **⋯** menu on each row shows the actions available for that person:

| Action | When it appears |
| --- | --- |
| **Open user** | Bethel ChMS users. Opens their user page. |
| **Edit** | Active Bethel ChMS users. |
| **Resend invitation** | Bethel ChMS users who haven't accepted their invitation yet. |
| **Open member record** | When the person is linked to a member record. |
| **Email** | When the person has an email address. Opens your email app. |
| **Deactivate** | Active users, except yourself and the Org Owner. |
| **Reactivate** | Deactivated users. |
| **Resend** and **Cancel invite** | Porter-only people who haven't accepted their invitation yet. |
| **Remove Porter** | Porter-only people whose Porter access is active. |

Porter actions appear only for Org Owners and Org Admins.

## View a user's details and change history

Click anywhere on a user's row to open their page. The top of the page shows their role and status, their email address, and an **Email** button. A **Member** button also appears when they're linked to a member record.

The **Details** tab has two cards:

- **Account** shows their **Name**, **Email**, **Role**, **Scope** (organisation or branch), **Branch**, **Status** and **Member record**.
- **App access** shows each app they can use. For Bethel ChMS, it shows their role and branch. For Porter, it shows their Porter access level, whose member record their check-ins and loans go on, and whether the Porter invitation is pending, expired or active.

The **Change History** tab shows field-level changes to the user record. See [Change history](/guides/change-history).
