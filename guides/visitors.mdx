A **visitor** is someone who has attended your church but has not become a member yet. Bethel ChMS keeps visitors in their own list, separate from [members](/guides/managing-members), so your team can see who is new, who keeps coming back, and who is ready to be welcomed as a member.

Like members, each visitor belongs to one **branch** (one of your church's locations or congregations).

<Info>
**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin and Editor can open **Visitors**, and add, edit and convert visitors. Org Owner, Org Admin and Branch Admin can delete visitors. Editors cannot delete visitors. Care Managers and Treasurers do not have access to **Visitors**. See [Roles and permissions](/guides/roles-and-permissions).
</Info>

## How visitors get into Bethel ChMS

Visitors are added in three ways:

- **They register themselves at check-in.** When someone who isn't in Bethel ChMS checks in with an event's QR code or at a kiosk, they can register as a visitor. See [Recording attendance](/guides/recording-attendance) and [Kiosk mode](/guides/kiosk-mode).
- **Staff add them while taking attendance.** The **Record Attendance** sheet has **Add visitor** for first-time visitors. See [Recording attendance](/guides/recording-attendance).
- **Staff add them on the Visitors page.** See [Add a visitor](#add-a-visitor).

When a new visitor has an email address, Bethel ChMS emails them a welcome message with their personal check-in QR code, so they can check in quickly next time.

## Open the visitor list

In the sidebar, open **People** and click **Visitors**. On a phone or tablet, tap **Visitors** in the tab bar or rail.

If you are an Org Owner or Org Admin, the list shows the branch chosen in the branch selector. Switch branch to see another branch's visitors. See [Find your way around](/getting-started/find-your-way-around).

### Visitor summary cards

Four cards at the top of the page give you a quick picture:

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Visitors** | How many visitors your branch has, and the change since last month |
| **New This Month** | Visitors whose first visit was this month, compared with last month |
| **Returning Visitors** | Visitors who have come more than once, and what share of all visitors that is |
| **Ready to Convert** | Visitors with 2 or more visits. Click **Show them** to filter the list to these visitors. When there are none, the card reads "No visitors ready yet". |

### What the list shows

On a phone, each row shows the visitor's name, mobile number and tags, with a badge for how many visits they have made and the date of their last visit. The visits badge turns green from 2 visits. Tablets and computers add the email address under the name and columns for **Mobile**, **Visits** and **Last Visit**. Wide screens also show **Nationality**, **First Visit** and a column for each visitor custom field.

The list shows 50 visitors at a time. When there are more, click **Show 50 more** at the bottom to load the next set. Near the end of the list, the button shows how many are left instead.

## Find visitors

- **Search:** type in **Search by name, email, or mobile…**.
- **Quick chips:** under the search box, tap **All** or **Ready to convert**. Each chip shows a count.
- **Filters:** click **Filters** to open one sheet with:
  - **Sort by**: **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email**, **Nationality**, **First Visit Date**, **Last Visit Date**, **Visit Count**, **Created At**, or a visitor custom field. The list starts sorted by first visit date, newest first.
  - **Visits**: **Any**, or **2+ visits (ready to convert)**.
  - **Tags**: one or more visitor tags. The list shows visitors with any of the chosen tags.
  - **Nationality**: **All nationalities**, or one nationality from your branch.
  - **Custom fields**: a filter for each visitor custom field.

Click **Show N visitors** to close the sheet, or **Clear all** to remove every filter. On tablets and computers, you can also sort with the menu next to the search box.

## Add a visitor

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin, Editor

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the visitor list">
    In the sidebar, open **People** and click **Visitors**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Visitor">
    Click **Add Visitor** in the top bar. On a phone, tap the **Add Visitor** button that floats above the tab bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the visitor's details">
    **First Name** and **Last Name** are required. You can also fill in **Gender**, **Nationality**, **Email**, **Mobile Number**, **Tags**, and any visitor custom fields your church has set up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the visitor">
    Click **Create Visitor**. You see "Visitor created".
  </Step>
</Steps>

When you leave the **Email** or **Mobile Number** field, Bethel ChMS checks whether another visitor already uses that email address or mobile number. If one does, the form shows a warning, for example "A visitor with this email already exists: View visitor". Click **View visitor** to open that visitor's details in a new tab. Two visitors in the same branch can't share an email address or mobile number, so check the existing visitor first.

Visitor tags are created in [Tags](/guides/tags). Visitor forms only show tags that apply to visitors.

## Visit tracking

Bethel ChMS keeps three details up to date for every visitor:

- **Visit Count** goes up by one each time the visitor is checked in to an event. A visitor who registers at check-in has 1 visit after that first check-in. A visitor you add on the Visitors page starts at 1, counting the day you met them.
- **First Visit** is the date the visitor was added. It never changes.
- **Last Visit** is the date of their most recent check-in, or the date they were added if they haven't been checked in yet.

You don't edit these yourself. They change when the visitor is checked in, for example through public check-in, a kiosk or the **Record Attendance** sheet.

## View a visitor's details

Visitors don't have a page of their own. Tap a visitor in the list, or choose **View details** from the row's **⋯** menu, to open their details.

The details show:

- The visitor's name, their number of visits and, from 2 visits, "ready to convert".
- Their Visitor care status, if your team is following them up. See [Follow up with visitors](#follow-up-with-visitors).
- **Mobile**, **Nationality**, **Email**, **Gender**, **Visit Count**, **First Visit**, **Last Visit**, custom fields and **Tags**. Tap the mobile number to call, or the email address to write to them.
- **Visit history**: their 20 most recent check-ins, with the event and date. A visitor who hasn't checked in yet shows "No check-ins recorded yet."

At the bottom are the actions you can take: **Add to visitor care** (or **View care case**), **Edit**, **Convert to Member**, **Add a care case** and **Delete**. You only see the actions your role allows.

On tablets and computers, each row's **⋯** menu has **View details**, **Call** (when the visitor has a mobile number), **Edit**, **Convert to Member**, **Add to visitor care** (or **View care case**) and **Delete**.

## Edit a visitor

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin, Editor

1. Open the visitor's details, or the row's **⋯** menu, and choose **Edit**.
2. Update the details in the **Edit Visitor** panel. It has the same fields as **Add Visitor**.
3. Click **Save Changes**. You see "Visitor updated".

## Follow up with visitors

Following up with visitors is part of **Care**, Bethel ChMS's area for keeping track of the people who need a call, a visit or a welcome. In Care, a **care case** is one person's follow-up: who is doing it, its status, when it is due and notes along the way.

From the Visitors page you can:

- **See where follow-up stands.** If a visitor has a Visitor care case, their details show its status, who it is assigned to, and an **Open in Visitor care** link.
- **Start follow-up by hand.** Choose **Add to visitor care** from the visitor's details or the row's **⋯** menu. If the visitor already has an open case, the button reads **View care case** instead. Org Owners, Org Admins, Branch Admins and Editors can do this.
- **Record a pastoral need.** Click **Add a care case** in the visitor's details, for example for a prayer request.

Your church can also have Bethel ChMS open a Visitor care case automatically for every new visitor. To set that up, and to learn how assigning, statuses and reminders work, see [Care](/guides/care).

<Tip>
To get a list of visitors with their care status, assignee and due date, use the **Visitor Follow-up List** report. See [Reports](/guides/reports).
</Tip>

## Convert a visitor to a member

When a visitor is ready to join your church, convert their visitor record into a member record. You don't need to type their details again.

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin, Editor

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the conversion">
    Open the visitor's details and click **Convert to Member**, or choose **Convert to Member** from the row's **⋯** menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the details">
    The panel is titled with the visitor's name, for example "Convert Grace Owusu to Member". **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Gender**, **Nationality**, **Email** and **Mobile Number** are filled in from the visitor record. Correct anything that has changed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add member details if you have them">
    Under **Additional member details (optional)**, you can add **Date of Birth** (which sets their age group automatically), **Address**, **Post Code** and **Tags**. Tags from the visitor record that also apply to members are carried over.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the conversion">
    Click **Convert to Member**. You see "Visitor converted to member". The new member starts with the **Newcomer** status, and the visitor no longer appears in the visitor list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/guides/visitors-convert-to-member.png" alt="The Convert Grace Owusu to Member panel with the visitor's name, gender, nationality, email and mobile filled in, and an empty Additional member details (optional) section with Date of Birth, Address, Post Code and Tags" width="480" />
</Frame>

If the email address or mobile number already belongs to a member, the panel warns you, for example "A member with this email already exists: View member". Check that member's record first so you don't create a duplicate.

Care Managers can't open **Visitors**, but they can convert a visitor from that visitor's Visitor care case. See [Care](/guides/care).

### What carries over to the new member

- **Attendance:** the member's **Attendance History** tab includes the check-ins from before they became a member.
- **Tags:** only tags that apply to members carry over. Visitor-only tags stay behind.
- **Custom fields:** visitor custom field values are not copied to the member record.
- **Care:** an open Visitor care case closes with the status **Became a member**. Any open pastoral care cases stay open and move to the new member. The member's **Care** tab shows their visitor follow-up under **Visitor care history**.

<Note>
Bethel ChMS can also convert visitors automatically once they reach a number of visits, using the **Visitor Conversion** automation rule. See [Automation rules](/guides/automation-rules).
</Note>

## Export visitors

Click **Export CSV** in the toolbar. On smaller screens, the button shows only a download icon, next to **Filters**. The file includes every visitor that matches your current search and filters, so set your filters first.

The file has the columns **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email**, **Mobile**, **Gender**, **Nationality**, **Visit Count**, **First Visit**, **Last Visit** and **Tags**, followed by a column for each visitor custom field. Custom fields that have since been removed are marked "(removed)".

On most phones and tablets, the share sheet opens so you can save the file or send it on. Otherwise, the file downloads.

## Delete a visitor

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin

1. Open the visitor's details, or the row's **⋯** menu, and choose **Delete**.
2. In **Delete Visitor**, click **Delete** to confirm. You see "Visitor deleted".

Deleting a visitor removes them from your lists, but the record is not removed straight away. Any open Visitor care or pastoral care cases for that visitor close with the status **No follow-up needed**.
