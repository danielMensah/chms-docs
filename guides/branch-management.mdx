A **branch** is an individual church or campus within your organisation, and your **organisation** is the whole church network. Each branch owns its own members, visitors, events, locations and ministries.

The **Branches** page is where organisation-level administrators set up and look after branches. From here you can create new branches, duplicate existing ones, copy resources between branches, and change each branch's settings.

<Info>
**Who can do this:** Org Owner and Org Admin can create, duplicate, edit and copy resources between branches. Only the Org Owner can delete a branch. Branch Admins and Editors don't see **Branches** in the sidebar, so if a Branch Admin needs their branch's details or Quick Find setting changed, an Org Owner or Org Admin can do it from **Branches**.
</Info>

## Open the Branches page

In the sidebar, open the **Organisation** group and click **Branches**. On a tablet or phone, tap **More**, then **Branches** under **Organisation**. See [Find your way around](/getting-started/find-your-way-around).

The page shows:

- A usage card, for example **2 of 3 branches used**, with your plan and a meter. See [Branch limit](#branch-limit).
- Your branches, sorted by name, with their **Name**, **Email** (or **No email**) and **Created Date**.
- A **⋯** menu on each branch with **View**, **Duplicate Branch** and **Copy Resources**.

Click a branch to open its page, where you can change its details, Quick Find and categories.

## Branch limit

Your plan includes a set number of branches. The usage card on the **Branches** page shows how many you've used and how many more are available.

When you reach the limit, the card turns amber and reads **You've reached your plan's branch limit. Contact us to create another branch.** Click **Contact us** to email the BethelWare Software Ltd team about raising your limit. Until it's raised:

- Clicking **Create Branch** shows **Branch limit reached** instead of opening the form.
- Duplicating stops at your limit, because each duplicate counts as a new branch.

## Create a branch

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new branch">
    On the **Branches** page, click **Create Branch**. On a phone, the **Create Branch** button sits near the bottom of the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the branch details">
    Under **Branch Details**, enter the **Branch Name**. It must be at least 2 characters and different from your other branch names. You can also add a **Branch Email (optional)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the primary location">
    Under **Primary Location**, enter the **Location Name**, for example "Main Hall". You can also add an **Address (optional)** and **Capacity (optional)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up the Sunday Service">
    Under **Sunday Service**, check the **Start Time** and **End Time**. They start at 10:00 and 12:00, so change them if your branch meets at a different time. The end time must be after the start time. The **Description (optional)** fills in from the branch name, and you can change it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the branch">
    Click **Create Branch**. Bethel ChMS confirms with **Branch created successfully**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If your organisation pays annually, Bethel ChMS may show **Confirm branch addition** with the **Estimated charge:** for the extra branch before it creates it. Click **Confirm and add branch** to go ahead. If your subscription payment is still being processed, you can't add branches until it's confirmed.
</Note>

### What a new branch comes with

Everything is set up in one step, so you don't need to create the location or Sunday Service separately. A new branch gets:

- A **primary location**, the main place where the branch meets. See [Locations](/guides/locations).
- A weekly **Sunday Service** event at that location, with 52 weekly occurrences. An **occurrence** is one date of a repeating event.

The Sunday Service is set to repeat for one year from the first service. To keep it going after that, open the event, move its **Recurrence End Date** later, and save. Bethel ChMS then adds the extra Sundays for you. See [Managing events](/guides/managing-events).

## Duplicate a branch

Duplicating creates up to 10 new branches at once, using an existing branch as a template. This is useful when you're launching several new locations with a similar setup.

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the wizard">
    On the **Branches** page, open the **⋯** menu on the branch you want to copy and choose **Duplicate Branch**. The wizard has four steps: **Source**, **Details**, **Events** and **Review**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Source: choose how many copies">
    The **Source Branch** is shown for reference. Enter the **Number of Duplicates**, from 1 to 10.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Details: set up each new branch">
    Each new branch has its own card. Open a card and fill in the **Branch Name**, **Location Name** and **Address**. You can also add a **Post Code (optional)** and **Capacity (optional)**. Every new branch needs a different name. Under **Sunday Service**, the **Start Time** and **End Time** start at 10:00 and 12:00 and the description fills in from the branch name. Change them if the new branch meets at a different time.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/guides/branches-duplicate-details-step.png" alt="The Duplicate Branch sheet on step 2 of 4, Details, with the first new branch card open showing Branch Name, Location Name, Address, Post Code, Capacity and Sunday Service times, and a second branch card collapsed" width="480" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Events: choose events to clone">
    Under **Select Events to Clone**, tick any other events from the source branch that you'd like each new branch to have. You don't need to pick the Sunday Service here, because every new branch gets its own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and duplicate">
    Check the summary for each new branch, showing its location, Sunday Service, events and ministries, then click **Duplicate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the results">
    **Duplication Results** shows **Created** or an error for each new branch. If any failed, click **Retry Failed**. Click **Done** to finish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### What gets duplicated

**Included:** a primary location with the details you entered, a weekly Sunday Service at the times you set, any other events you selected, with their occurrences, and all active ministries (name, description and status only). Cloned events take place at the new branch's primary location.

**Not included:** the branch email, members, visitors, attendance records, user accounts and ministry member assignments.

<Info>
Duplicated branches start with no people in them. You'll need to add members, invite users and assign ministry rosters separately.
</Info>

## Copy resources between branches

Copy individual events, locations or ministries from one branch into one or more other branches, without creating a new branch.

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Copy Resources">
    On the **Branches** page, open the **⋯** menu on the branch you want to copy from and choose **Copy Resources**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to copy">
    Tick the items you want under **Events** (each marked **Recurring** or **One-time**), **Locations** and **Ministries**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the target branches">
    Under **Target Branches**, tick each branch you want to copy into.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy">
    Click **Copy**. A results table shows each target branch, whether the copy worked, and how many events, locations and ministries were copied. Click **Done**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
- A copied event keeps its location only if you copy that location in the same go. Otherwise the copied event has no location, so open it afterwards and choose one.
- Copied locations are never set as the target branch's primary location.
- Copied ministries include their name, description and status only, with no members. Only active ministries can be copied.
</Note>

## The branch page

To open a branch's page, click the branch on the **Branches** page, or choose **View** from its **⋯** menu.

At the top you'll see the branch name, a badge showing whether the branch's Quick Find switch is on, buttons for the branch's **Dashboard** and **Email** (if it has one), and the date it was created. The **More actions** menu (the three dots at the top right) has **Edit branch details**, **Branch dashboard** and, for the Org Owner, **Delete Branch**.

Below that are these cards:

| Card | What it's for | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Branch Details** | The branch name, email and what the branch calls its fellowship groups. | Everyone who opens the page |
| **Quick Find** | Accepting the Quick Find disclosure and switching Quick Find on or off for this branch. | Org Owner, Org Admin |
| **Gifting Categories** | Which gifting categories this branch uses. | Org Owner, Org Admin |
| **Activity Types** | Which activity types this branch tracks. | Org Owner, Org Admin |
| **Delete Branch** | Deleting the branch. | Org Owner |

## Edit branch details

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the edit panel">
    On the branch page, click **Edit** on the **Branch Details** card, or choose **Edit branch details** from the **More actions** menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Update the **Branch Name** (at least 2 characters, and different from your other branches) or **Email (optional)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your fellowship groups">
    Under **What do you call your fellowship groups?**, enter the name your branch uses, up to 50 characters, or tap a suggestion: **Homecell**, **Connect Group**, **Life Group** or **Group**. Bethel ChMS uses this name wherever it mentions groups at this branch. If you leave it blank, it uses "Group".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**. Bethel ChMS confirms with **Branch updated successfully**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Groups](/guides/groups) for more about fellowship groups.

## Quick Find

Quick Find lets people check in at a kiosk by finding their name, as well as by email, mobile or QR code. At the kiosk it appears as **Find by name**. There are two parts to switching it on: one acceptance for the whole organisation, then a switch on each branch.

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a branch page">
    On the **Branches** page, click any branch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read and accept the disclosure">
    If nobody has accepted it yet, the **Quick Find** card shows a safeguarding disclosure. Read it, tick **I have read and accept this on behalf of my organisation**, and click **Accept and enable Quick Find**. You only need to do this once: it covers every branch in your organisation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch Quick Find on or off for each branch">
    Once the disclosure is accepted, each branch's **Quick Find** card has a **Quick Find** switch, with a note of who accepted the disclosure and when. The switch starts on. Turn it off for any branch that shouldn't offer Find by name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can see every branch's Quick Find status at once on the **Quick Find** card in [Organisation settings](/guides/organisation-settings#quick-find-status). See [Find by name (Quick Find)](/guides/quick-find) for how it works at the kiosk.

<Note>
If you do not see the Quick Find setting on your branch, contact support at hello@bethelware.app to have Find by name switched on for your church.
</Note>

## Gifting categories and activity types

Gifting categories and activity types are created once for the whole organisation. On each branch page you choose which ones that branch uses.

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin

- On the **Gifting Categories** card, turn each category's switch on or off.
- On the **Activity Types** card, turn each activity type's switch on or off.

Changes apply immediately. See [Gifting and donations](/guides/gifting-and-donations) and [Activity log](/guides/activity-log).

## Delete a branch

**Who can do this:** Org Owner

<Warning>
Deleting a branch deletes all of its data, including its users, members, visitors, events, locations and ministries.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the deletion">
    On the branch page, click **Delete Branch** on the **Delete Branch** card, or choose **Delete Branch** from the **More actions** menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the branch name">
    Type the branch's name exactly as shown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Delete Branch**. Bethel ChMS confirms with **Branch deleted successfully** and takes you back to the **Branches** page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Deleted branch data can be recovered within 30 days. Your billing is updated automatically to reflect the new number of branches.

You can't delete your organisation's only branch. Create another branch first if you need to remove the original one.
