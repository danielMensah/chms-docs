[Porter](/guides/porter) lets your workers and volunteers check in when they arrive for a service. A worker scans the event's check-in QR code in Porter, and their phone confirms they're on site. The check-in then shows up as attendance in Bethel ChMS, just like a kiosk check-in.

A few terms used on this page:

- An **occurrence** is one date and time of an event, such as this Sunday's morning service.
- A **check-in area** is the circle around a location where check-in works. You set its centre and its size, called the **radius**, in metres.
- A **Porter admin** is someone with **Porter · Admin** access. A **worker** has **Porter · Worker**. See [Porter access levels](/guides/porter#porter-access-levels).

## Before your first check-in

Check these before your service team starts checking in:

1. **The location exists in Bethel ChMS.** Porter uses the [locations](/guides/locations) you already have. If Porter's **Setup** says **Your church has no locations yet. Add one in Bethel ChMS first.**, add your building as a location first.
2. **The event has a location.** Porter checks the worker's phone against the occurrence's own location, or the event's location if the occurrence doesn't have one. See [Managing events](/guides/managing-events).
3. **Public Check-in is on for the event.** Workers scan the event's check-in QR code, and Bethel ChMS only shows that code while **Public Check-in** is on. See [Porter uses the same QR code](/guides/managing-events#porter-uses-the-same-qr-code).
4. **The location has a check-in area.** A Porter admin sets this in Porter. See [Set a check-in area](#set-a-check-in-area).
5. **The QR code is on display where workers arrive.** Print it from the event's **Public Check-in QR** card with **Print QR**. See [Share the event QR code](/guides/recording-attendance#share-the-event-qr-code).

<Note>
Members and visitors can also scan this same code to check themselves in, so keep it where you're happy for anyone to use it. The event's **Check-in Window** only applies to members and visitors. Workers can check in with Porter at any time on the day of the occurrence.
</Note>

<Warning>
If you use **Regenerate Token** on the event's **Public Check-in QR** card, the old code stops working in Porter straight away. Workers who scan it see **That isn't today's check-in code**. Print and display the new code before your next service.
</Warning>

## Set a check-in area

**Who can do this:** Porter · Admin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Setup">
    In Porter, tap the **Setup** tab. Every location in your organisation is listed under its branch, with a badge showing **Check-in area set** and its radius, or **Not set**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the location">
    Tap the location you want to set up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the centre of the area">
    Stand at the building and tap **Use my current location**. Porter fills in the **Latitude** and **Longitude** and tells you roughly how accurate your phone's position is, in metres. You can also type the **Latitude** and **Longitude** yourself.

    If Porter says **Couldn't get your location. Turn location on, allow it and try again.**, turn on location for your browser or the app, then tap the button again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the radius">
    Enter the **Radius (m)**, between 25 m and 2000 m. It starts at 150 m, which suits most buildings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Save**. Porter confirms with **Check-in area saved for** and the location's name. Tap **Cancel** to close without saving.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  <img src="/images/guides/porter-setup-check-in-area.png" alt="The London Central locations in Porter Setup, with Main Auditorium open showing Use my current location, Latitude, Longitude and Radius (m), and Community Hall marked Not set" width="320" />
</Frame>

<Tip>
Phones often struggle to get an accurate position indoors. If workers keep seeing **Couldn't confirm your location**, make that location's radius bigger.
</Tip>

## Check in when you arrive

**Who can do this:** Porter · Worker, Porter · Admin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Check in">
    On Porter's **Home** tab, tap **Check in**. The camera opens, with the words **Scan the event's check-in code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the event's QR code">
    Point your camera at the QR code for today's event, usually shown at the entrance. To stop, tap the **X** at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share your location">
    If your phone asks, allow Porter to use your location. Porter shows **Confirming your location…** while it checks that you're on site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're checked in">
    Porter shows **You're checked in!** with the time, the event and location, and the date. Tap **Done** to go back to **Home**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You only need to check in once for each occurrence. If you scan again, Porter shows **You're already checked in** with the event and location you checked in to.

Once you're checked in, you can [loan and return equipment](/guides/porter-equipment#loan-equipment).

<Tip>
Use the **Check in** button in Porter to scan the code. Your phone's own camera app opens the self check-in page for members and visitors instead, which doesn't check you in to Porter, so you couldn't loan equipment afterwards.
</Tip>

### Which occurrence your check-in counts for

Porter works out which occurrence of the event you're checking in to:

- **Today** means today in the event's time zone. Cancelled occurrences never count.
- If an occurrence is in progress, your check-in goes to that one.
- If not, it goes to the next occurrence later today.
- If there are none left today, it goes to the most recent one earlier today.

You can check in to any event in your organisation, not just events in your own branch.

## If check-in doesn't work

Each problem screen starts with **You're not checked in.** and offers **Try again** and **Back to home**. If your phone isn't working, ask a Porter admin to [check you in by hand](#check-workers-in-by-hand).

| Screen | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Location is turned off** | Porter couldn't use your phone's location. | Turn on location and allow Porter. On iPhone, go to **Settings**, **Privacy & Security**, **Location Services**. On Android, go to **Settings**, **Location**. Then scan again. |
| **You're outside the check-in area** | Your phone is further from the location than its check-in area allows. | Move closer to the building and scan again. |
| **Couldn't confirm your location** | Your phone's position isn't accurate enough yet, or it took too long to get one. | Step outside or stand near a window, wait a few seconds and scan again. |
| **That isn't today's check-in code** | The code isn't a check-in code for one of your church's events, or it has been replaced. | Scan the QR code for today's event. |
| **There's no event to check in to right now** | The event has nothing on today. | Check you're scanning the right event's code. |
| **This location isn't set up yet** | The location has no check-in area. | Ask a Porter admin to [set the check-in area](#set-a-check-in-area). |
| **No connection** | Your phone is offline. | Check-in needs mobile data or Wi-Fi. Reconnect and scan again. |
| **Check-in didn't work** | Something else went wrong. | Tap **Try again**. If it keeps happening, ask a Porter admin to check you in by hand. |

## Check workers in by hand

**Who can do this:** Porter · Admin

Use **Manual check-in** for workers who can't scan, for example when their phone is dead. It doesn't need a check-in area or the QR code. You can open it from **Home**, **Setup** or **History**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manual check-in">
    Tap **Manual check-in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the event">
    Under **1. Event**, pick one of today's occurrences. Each shows the event, its start and end time, and its location. If nothing is on, Porter shows **No events today**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the workers">
    Under **2. Workers**, tick everyone you want to check in. The list shows everyone in your organisation with Porter access who hasn't checked in to that occurrence yet. Use **Select all** or **Deselect all** to save time. If the list says **Everyone is checked in.**, there's nobody left to add.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a reason">
    Under **3. Reason (optional)**, add a note such as "Phone dead", up to 200 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check them in">
    Tap the **Check in** button, which shows how many workers you've chosen. Porter confirms how many were checked in, and how many were already checked in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The worker's **Home** then shows **Checked in by an admin**, and they can loan and return equipment as usual.

## See check-in history

Open the **History** tab. It shows the past two weeks to begin with. Use the date range picker to choose other dates.

**History** has these tabs:

- **Check-ins**: who checked in, to which event, when and where. Workers see only their own check-ins. Porter admins see everyone's, and can filter by **All workers** or one worker. Anyone can filter by **All events** or one event. Manual check-ins have a **manual** badge with who added them, and the reason if one was given.
- **Equipment**: loans, returns, faults and status changes. See [Equipment history](/guides/porter-equipment#equipment-history).
- **Rejected attempts**, for Porter admins only: check-ins that were refused.

Lists show 25 rows at a time, with buttons to move between pages.

### Rejected attempts

**Who can do this:** Porter · Admin

Each refused check-in shows the worker, the time, the event and location, and a reason:

| Reason | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| **Wrong code** | The code wasn't a current check-in code for one of your events. |
| **No event today** | The event had nothing on that day. |
| **No location set** | The location had no check-in area. |
| **Poor accuracy** | The phone's position wasn't accurate enough. |
| **Outside the area** | The phone was outside the check-in area. |

Where Porter knows them, the row also shows the distance from the location and the phone's accuracy, for example **Distance 240 m · Accuracy ±18 m**. This helps you decide whether a location's radius needs to be bigger.

Some problems are caught on the worker's phone before anything is sent, so they don't appear here: location being turned off, no connection, a QR code that isn't a check-in code at all, and a phone that takes too long to find its position.

## How Porter check-ins appear in Bethel ChMS

Every Porter check-in, including a manual one, also records attendance for that member on that event occurrence in Bethel ChMS. On the member's **Attendance History** tab, the check-in method shows as **Porter**.

If the member already has attendance for that occurrence, for example from the kiosk, Bethel ChMS keeps the existing record. See [Record attendance](/guides/recording-attendance).
