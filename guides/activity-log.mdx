---
title: "Activity log"
description: "Record counts of pastoral and outreach activity for each branch, manage activity types and open the activity log report in Bethel ChMS."
---

The **Activity Log** records how much pastoral and outreach work happens at each branch, as numbers. For example, you might log that 3 people gave their lives to Christ at Sunday's service, or that your team made 12 home visits this week. Over time, the counts show your leaders how ministry is growing.

A few terms you'll see on this page:

- An **activity type** is what you are counting, such as **Souls Won** or **Home Visits**. Your organisation shares one list of types, and each branch chooses which ones it tracks.
- An **entry** is one count for one activity type at one branch, with a date, an optional time and optional notes.
- A **branch** is an individual church or campus within your organisation.

<Info>
**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin, Editor. Treasurers and Care Managers do not have access to the Activity Log.
</Info>

## Who can do what

| | Org Owner, Org Admin | Branch Admin | Editor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Branches | Any branch, chosen with the branch selector | Their own branch | Their own branch |
| Entry dates | Any date | Up to 90 days in the past | Up to 90 days in the past |
| Log and edit entries | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Delete, see and restore deleted entries | Yes | Yes | No |
| Filter entries by **Logged by** | Yes | No | No |
| Manage activity types | Yes | No | No |
| Activity log report | Any branch or several branches | Their own branch | Their own branch, without deleted entries |

Branch Admins and Editors work only with their own branch's entries, because each branch owns its own records. Org Owners and Org Admins look after the whole organisation, so they can log and review activity for any branch.

## Open the Activity Log

In the sidebar, open **Church life** and click **Activity Log**. On a phone, or a tablet held upright, tap **More**, then **Activity Log**.

See [Find your way around](/getting-started/find-your-way-around) for how the sidebar, rail and tab bar work.

### Choose a branch

The Activity Log shows one branch at a time, and new entries are saved to that branch.

- **Org Owner** and **Org Admin** users choose the branch with the branch selector: at the top of the sidebar on a computer, or the branch chip in the top bar on a tablet or phone. If you have never picked a branch, Bethel ChMS uses the first branch in alphabetical order.
- **Branch Admin** and **Editor** users always work in their own branch.

If your organisation has no branches yet, the page shows "Select a branch above to start logging entries." See [Branch management](/guides/branch-management).

### What's on the page

- **The date range.** It starts on the last 30 days and controls both the summary tiles and **Recent entries**. It works the same way as on the Gifting page. See [Change the dates](/guides/gifting-and-donations#change-the-dates).
- **Activity report**, next to the dates (**Report** on a phone). See [Activity log report](#activity-log-report).
- **Three summary tiles:**
  - **Total entries**: how many entries fall in the date range, with the range shown underneath.
  - **Total count**: all the counts in the range added together.
  - **Top activity type**: the type with the highest total count in the range. Until there are entries, it says "Log an entry to populate".
- **The log form**, for recording counts. See [Log a single entry](#log-a-single-entry) and [Log several types at once](#log-several-types-at-once).
- **Recent entries**, the list of entries for the branch and dates. See [Review recent entries](#review-recent-entries).

The top bar has an **Activity Types** button for Org Owners and Org Admins on computers and tablets, and a **⋮** menu (**More actions**) with **Log multiple entries**, **Activity Types** (Org Owner and Org Admin only) and **Activity report**.

The tiles update every time you save or restore an entry.

## Log a single entry

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin, Editor

Where you find the form depends on your screen:

- **On a computer or tablet:** the log card sits beside **Recent entries**, or above it on narrower screens. It is titled **Log a single entry** and names the branch it saves to. A switch at the top moves between **Single entry** and **Log multiple**.
- **On a phone:** tap the **Log entry** button at the bottom right of the screen. A sheet slides up with the branch and today's date at the top, and the same **Single entry** and **Log multiple** switch.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the activity type">
    Tap a type under **Activity type**. Only the types your branch tracks appear. Bethel ChMS selects the last type you used for this branch on this device, and tells you so, for example "Home Visits was your last type here, so it is pre-selected." Org Owners and Org Admins also see a **Manage activity types** link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the count">
    Type the **Count**, or use the minus and plus buttons. The count is a whole number, 0 or more. A count of 0 is saved, so you can record that something didn't happen, for example no baptisms this month.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the date">
    The **Date** starts on today. Change it if you are recording an earlier day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a time">
    Add a **Time (optional)** if it helps, for example to tell a morning service from an evening one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add notes">
    Add **Notes (optional)** of up to 500 characters. A counter shows how many characters you have used.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the entry">
    Click **Log entry**. On a phone, the button shows the count, for example **Log entry · 6**. With a keyboard, you can also press **Cmd+S** on a Mac or **Ctrl+S** on Windows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A message confirms the count, the type, the date and your name. On a computer or tablet, the type and date stay set, and the count, time and notes clear, so you can log the next entry straight away. On a phone, the sheet closes. The next time you tap **Log entry**, your last type is selected again and the date is back to today.

The new entry appears in **Recent entries** with a green **New** badge. The badge stays while you remain on the page.

If no types appear, the page says "No active activity types for this branch." Ask an Org Owner or Org Admin to [switch types on for the branch](#choose-activity-types-for-each-branch).

### Rules for entry dates

- **Branch Admins** and **Editors** can log entries dated up to 90 days in the past. The same limit applies when they edit an entry, so they can't save changes to an entry dated more than 90 days ago. An older date shows "Entry date is too far in the past. Only org admins can log entries older than 90 days."
- **Org Owners** and **Org Admins** can use any date, so they can record older activity if needed.

## Log several types at once

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin, Editor

Use **Log multiple** to record counts for several activity types on the same date, such as everything from one Sunday service. Each type you count becomes its own entry.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch to Log multiple">
    In the log card, click **Log multiple**. You can also choose **Log multiple entries** from the **⋮** menu. On a phone, this opens the log sheet ready for several types.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the date and time">
    Set the **Date** and, if you want, the **Time (optional)**. They apply to every entry you save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the counts">
    Each type your branch tracks has its own row with a **Count**. Enter a count for each type you want to record. Leave a count blank to skip that type. A count of 0 is saved as 0.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add notes">
    The **Notes** box below the rows applies to every entry. To give one type its own note, click **Note** under the type's name and type the note. A type's own note replaces the shared notes for that entry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The bar at the bottom shows how many entries will be saved, their total count, and how many blank types will be skipped. Click **Save all**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A message confirms how many entries were logged and for which date. On a computer or tablet, the date and time stay set, and the counts and notes clear. On a phone, the sheet closes.

If every count is blank, you see "Please enter a count for at least one activity type before saving."

## Review recent entries

The **Recent entries** list shows the branch's entries for the chosen dates, newest first, with the total number of entries at the top. When you filter by type or by person, the total says **filtered**.

On a computer or tablet, the list has the columns **Type · Notes**, **Date** (with the time underneath), **Logged by**, **Status** and **Count**. On a phone, each row shows the type, notes, the date, time and who logged it, and the count.

**Status** shows:

- **New** for entries you logged during this visit to the page.
- **Edited** when someone has changed the entry, with how many times and who made the last change.
- **Deleted** when the entry has been deleted. Deleted entries only appear when **Show deleted** is on.

The list shows 50 entries at a time. Use **Previous** and **Next** to see more. If nothing matches, you see "No entries found."

Click a row to [open the entry](#open-an-entry).

### Filter the list

Click **Filters** to open the filter sheet:

- **Activity types:** pick one or more types.
- **Logged by:** choose **All users** or one person. Org Owner and Org Admin only.
- **Deleted entries:** turn on **Show deleted** to include deleted entries. Deleted entries are not counted in the tiles. Org Owner, Org Admin and Branch Admin only.

Click **Show** followed by the number of entries to close the sheet, or **Clear all** to remove every filter. Org Owners, Org Admins and Branch Admins also have a **Show deleted** chip next to **Filters**, to turn deleted entries on and off in one click.

### Restore an entry from the list

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin

Turn on **Show deleted**, then click **Restore** on the deleted entry. You see "Entry restored", and the entry counts towards the tiles again.

## Open an entry

Click an entry in **Recent entries** to open its page. The page title is the count and type, for example **6 Home Visits**. Click **Back to Activity Log** to return to the list.

The entry page shows:

- **A summary** with the type, the count, the date and time, who logged the entry and when, and who last updated it. An **Edited** badge shows how many edits there have been, and a **Deleted** badge shows when the entry is deleted.
- **Entry Information**, with **Date**, **Time**, **Type**, **Count**, **Created by**, **Last updated by** and **Notes**.
- **Audit History**, newest first. Each line says whether the entry was created, updated, deleted or restored, by whom and when. Edits list what changed, for example "changed count from 5 to 6".

While the entry is not deleted, the **⋮** menu (**More actions**) has **Edit entry**, **Open activity report** and **Delete entry**, depending on your role.

### Edit an entry

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin, Editor. Deleted entries cannot be edited.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the entry">
    Click the entry in **Recent entries**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start editing">
    Click **Edit entry** in the top bar, or choose it from the **⋮** menu. On a phone, tap the pencil button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    In **Edit entry**, change the **Activity type**, **Count**, **Date**, **Time** or **Notes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save changes**. You see "Entry updated".
  </Step>
</Steps>

Every change is recorded in **Audit History**, with the old and new values. Branch Admins and Editors can only change entries for their own branch.

### Delete an entry

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the entry">
    Click the entry in **Recent entries**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete it">
    Open the **⋮** menu and choose **Delete entry**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The confirmation shows the count, type and date of the entry. Click **Delete entry**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You see "Entry deleted" and stay on the entry page. It now shows who deleted the entry and when, and that it is no longer counted in the Activity Log tiles. Reports leave it out unless **Include deleted entries** is switched on.

### Restore a deleted entry

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin

On the deleted entry's page, click **Restore** in the top bar. You can also restore it from the list. See [Restore an entry from the list](#restore-an-entry-from-the-list).

## Manage activity types

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin

### The types every organisation starts with

Every organisation starts with six **system types**:

- Souls Won
- Communion Participation
- Water Baptisms
- First-time Visitors
- Home Visits
- Prayer Requests

You can rename or archive a system type, but you cannot delete it. Types you add yourself can be deleted while no entries use them.

### Open Activity Types

On the **Activity Log**, click **Activity Types** in the top bar, or choose **Activity Types** from the **⋮** menu. You can also click **Manage activity types** in the log form. Click **Back to Activity Log** to return.

The **Activity Types** page covers all branches. A line above the list shows how many types you have and how many are active and archived. On a computer or tablet, the list has these columns:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The type name and description. |
| **Status** | **Active** or **Archived**. |
| **Type** | A **System** badge for the types every organisation starts with. On narrower screens, the badge sits next to the name. |
| **Entries** | How many entries use the type. |
| **Branch Activation** | How many branches track the type, out of all your branches. You change this on each branch's page, not here. See [Choose activity types for each branch](#choose-activity-types-for-each-branch). |

Each row has a **⋯** button (**Actions for** the type name) with **Rename / Edit**, **Archive** or **Unarchive**, **Move up**, **Move down** and **Delete**.

### Add an activity type

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    On **Activity Types**, click **Add Activity Type**. On a phone, tap the **+** button in the top bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the type">
    Enter a **Name** of up to 100 characters, for example "Hospital Visits". Each type needs its own name. If the name is taken, you see "An activity type with this name already exists".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a description">
    Add a **Description (optional)** of up to 500 characters if it helps your team count consistently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create Activity Type**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A new type is switched on for every branch straight away.

### Rename or edit an activity type

Open the row's **⋯** menu and choose **Rename / Edit**. In **Edit Activity Type**, change the **Name** or **Description (optional)** and click **Save Changes**.

The sheet shows how many entries use the type and how many branches track it. Renaming a type updates every past entry and report, so old entries show the new name.

### Archive an activity type

Archive a type when you no longer want people to log it. Open the row's **⋯** menu and choose **Archive**. Archived types stay on past entries but no longer appear in the log forms. To bring one back, choose **Unarchive**.

### Delete an activity type

You can only delete a type you added that no entries use. When you can't, **Delete** is greyed out in the row's menu and the reason is written underneath:

- **System types:** "System types can be archived but not deleted"
- **Types with entries:** the number of entries that use it, with a reminder to archive the type instead.

To delete a type, open the row's **⋯** menu, choose **Delete**, then click **Delete** to confirm. This cannot be undone.

### Change the order of activity types

The order on **Activity Types** is the order of the type picker in **Log a single entry** and the rows in **Log multiple**.

- **With a mouse:** drag a type by its handle.
- **On any screen:** click **Reorder** (in the top bar on a computer or tablet, or next to the summary line on a phone). Use the **Move up** and **Move down** arrows to move types. The top of the list comes first. Each move is saved straight away. Click **Done** when you have finished. **Reorder** needs at least two types.

You can also choose **Move up** or **Move down** from a type's **⋯** menu.

### Choose activity types for each branch

Each branch chooses which of your organisation's activity types it tracks. New branches start with every type that isn't archived switched on.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the branch">
    In the sidebar, open **Organisation** and click **Branches**. Open the branch you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Activity Types">
    On the branch page, find the **Activity Types** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn types on or off">
    Use the switch next to each type. Changes apply immediately, and you see "Type activated" or "Type deactivated".
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only types that are switched on for the branch, and not archived, appear in that branch's log forms. When you edit an existing entry, you can keep its type even if it has since been switched off or archived. See [Branch management](/guides/branch-management).

## Activity log report

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin, Editor

The **Activity Log Report** adds up your entries over a period, compares them with the period before and breaks them down by type, over time and by branch.

### Open the report

- On the **Activity Log**, click **Activity report** next to the dates (**Report** on a phone), or choose **Activity report** from the **⋮** menu.
- On an entry's page, choose **Open activity report** from the **⋮** menu.
- Org Owners, Org Admins and Branch Admins can also open **Reports** and click **Activity Log** under **Dashboards**.

Editors don't have **Reports** in the sidebar, so they open the report from the Activity Log.

### Choose what the report covers

The report starts on the last 30 days, grouped by month, without deleted entries. Use the date range and the **Filters** button at the top to change:

- **Date range**, with quick chips for common periods.
- **Branch**: pick one or more branches. If you pick none, the report covers all branches.
- **Activity Type**: pick one or more types.
- **Granularity**: group the chart by **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly** or **Yearly** periods.
- **Include deleted entries**, under **Options**.

Branch Admins and Editors always see their own branch. Editors don't see **Include deleted entries**. See [Reports](/guides/reports) for more on these filters.

### What the report shows

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Entries** | How many entries fall in the period. |
| **Total Count** | All the counts in the period added together. |
| **vs Prior Period** | The change in total count compared with the period of the same length just before. |
| **Top Activity Type** | The type with the highest total count, and its share of the total. |
| **Activity Type Breakdown** | For each type: **# Entries**, **Total Count**, **Avg per Entry** and the change on the prior period. Sorted by total count, highest first. |
| **Activity Count Over Time** | A chart of the total count for each day, week, month or year. Switch between **Chart** and **Table**. |
| **Branch Comparison** | Each branch's activity side by side. Org Owners and Org Admins see it when they pick two or more branches. Choose a layout: **Flat**, **Rows = Branches** or **Rows = Types**. |
| **Entries** | Every entry in the report, newest first. Tap an entry to open it. |

### Export the report

Click **Export CSV** to download the entries that match your filters as a spreadsheet file, named with the report dates. On a phone or tablet that can share files, your device's share options open so you can save or send the file. The file has one row per entry, with the date, time, activity type, count, notes, branch, who logged it and when, who last updated it and when, and who deleted it and when.

When the file is ready, Bethel ChMS shows a message with **Download**. If your device can share files, it also offers **Share**.
