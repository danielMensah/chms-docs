A **member** is someone who belongs to your congregation. Each member record lives in one **branch** (one of your church's locations or congregations), and holds their contact details, demographics, tags, attendance, ministry roles, household and group.

Use **Members** for your member directory: searching and filtering, adding and editing people, member QR codes for check-in, and exports. People who have attended but not joined yet are **visitors**, and have their own list. See [Visitors](/guides/visitors).

<Info>
**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin and Editor can add and edit members. Care Manager (a role for people who follow up members in [Care](/guides/care)) can edit members but cannot add them. Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin and Care Manager can delete members. Editors cannot delete members. See [Roles and permissions](/guides/roles-and-permissions).
</Info>

## Open the member list

In the sidebar, open **People** and click **Members**. On a phone or tablet, tap **Members** in the tab bar or rail.

The page shows your branch's members. If you are an Org Owner or Org Admin, the list shows the branch chosen in the branch selector. Switch branch to see another branch's members. See [Find your way around](/getting-started/find-your-way-around) for where the branch selector sits on each device.

On a phone, each row shows the member's name, mobile number, tags and status. Tablets and computers add the email address under the name and columns such as **Mobile**, **Age** and **Status**, and wide screens also show **Nationality** and **Created**. A shield icon after a name means the member **Has app sign-in**, for example because they use [Porter](/guides/porter), the Bethel ChMS app for service teams.

On wide screens, two cards above the list show **Age Group Distribution** and **Gender Distribution** for your Newcomer and Active members. Click a slice to filter the list to that age group or gender.

## Find members

### Search

Type in **Search by name, email, or mobile…** to match a member's first name, last name, email address or mobile number.

### Filter by status

Under the search box, the status chips **All**, **Newcomer**, **Active** and **Inactive** each show a count. Tap a chip to show only members with that status.

Gender, age group, tag and nationality filters you set in the Filters sheet also appear as chips, for example "Gender: Female". Tap the chip's remove button to clear that filter.

### Use the Filters sheet

Click **Filters** to open one sheet with every other filter. A badge on the button shows how many filters are on. Filters apply as you choose them.

| Filter | What you can choose |
| --- | --- |
| **Sort by** | The field to sort by, and **A → Z** or **Z → A** |
| **Gender** | **Male**, **Female**, **Other**, **Prefer not to say** or **Not set** |
| **Age group** | One or more age groups, or **Not set** |
| **Tags** | One or more member tags. The list shows members who have any of the chosen tags. |
| **Nationality** | **All nationalities**, or one nationality from your branch |
| **Custom fields** | A filter for each of your member custom fields |

Click **Show N members** to close the sheet, or **Clear all** to remove every filter.

<Accordion title="How custom field filters work">
Each custom field opens as its own row, with a filter that suits the field type:

- **Text** fields have a search box.
- **Number** fields have **Min** and **Max**.
- **Date** fields have a from and to date range.
- **Yes/No** fields have **Any**, **Yes** and **No**.
- **Single Select** fields have a checkbox for each option.

Once you set a custom field filter, members with no value in that field are left out.
</Accordion>

### Sort the list

You can sort by **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email**, **Status**, **Gender**, **Age Group**, **Nationality**, **Date of Birth**, **Created At**, or any active member custom field. Use **Sort by** in the Filters sheet. On tablets and computers you can also click a column heading or use the sort menu next to the search box.

The line above the list shows what you are looking at, for example "48 of 120 members · sorted by last name".

### Move between pages

The list shows 50 members per page by default. Use **Rows per page** to show 25, 50 or 100, and **Previous** and **Next** to move between pages. Changing a filter or the sort order takes you back to the first page.

## Add a member

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin, Editor

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the member list">
    In the sidebar, open **People** and click **Members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Member">
    Click **Add Member** in the top bar. On a phone, tap the **Add Member** button that floats above the tab bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the member's details">
    **First Name** and **Last Name** are required. You can also fill in **Date of Birth**, **Age Group**, **Gender**, **Nationality**, **Email**, **Mobile Number**, **Address**, **Post Code**, **Tags**, and any member custom fields your church has set up. Required custom fields are marked with an asterisk.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the member">
    Click **Create Member**. You see "Member created", and the new member starts with the **Newcomer** status. If you entered an email address, Bethel ChMS emails the member their personal check-in QR code.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  <img src="/images/guides/members-add-member-sheet.png" alt="The Add Member panel with First Name and Last Name filled in, a date of birth entered, the Age Group locked and marked Derived from date of birth, and the Create Member button at the bottom" width="480" />
</Frame>

On phones, the form opens as a sheet from the bottom of the screen. On tablets and computers, it opens as a panel from the right.

### Nationality

**Nationality** is a searchable list. Click **Select nationality**, start typing in **Search nationality…**, and pick a match. Use the clear button to remove a nationality you chose by mistake.

### Age groups on member records

Age groups help you filter the member list and report on demographics. Your organisation's age groups are set up in [Age groups](/guides/age-groups).

- If you leave **Date of Birth** empty, you can choose an **Age Group** yourself. This is useful when someone prefers not to share their date of birth.
- Once you enter a **Date of Birth**, Bethel ChMS works out the age group for you. The **Age Group** field locks and shows "Derived from date of birth". Any age group you chose by hand is replaced.

### Tags on member records

Tags are labels you attach to people, such as **Student**, **Deacon** or **Choir**, so you can filter and report on them. In the **Tags** field, click **Add tag**, search for the tags you want and tick as many as you need. Member forms only show tags that apply to members.

Tags are created and managed in [Tags](/guides/tags).

### Duplicate email and mobile warning

When you leave the **Email** or **Mobile Number** field, Bethel ChMS checks whether another member already uses that email address or mobile number. If one does, the form shows a warning, for example "A member with this email already exists: View member". Click **View member** to open that record in a new tab.

Two members in the same branch can't share an email address or mobile number, so you can't save a second one. Check the existing record first. If the two records are the same person, see [Duplicates and merging](/guides/duplicates-and-merging).

## The member page

Click a member in the list to open their page. The page title is the member's full name, with **Back to Members** to return to the list.

### Summary and quick actions

The summary card at the top shows:

- The member's name and status.
- Their age group, either worked out from their date of birth (with their age) or marked "Manually set".
- **Has app sign-in**, if the member has a sign-in.
- All of their tags.

Below it are quick actions: **Call**, **Message**, **Email** and **QR code**. **Call** and **Message** use the member's mobile number, and **Email** opens your email app. These buttons are greyed out when the member has no mobile number or email address. On wide screens, the summary stays on the left with a contact card showing **Mobile**, **Email** and **Address**.

In the top bar, tablets and computers show **QR Code** and **Edit**. On phones, tap the pencil to edit. The **More actions** menu (⋮) has **Show QR code** and, for roles that can delete members, **Delete member**.

### Tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Details** | Every field on the record, including **Status**, **Created** and a **Custom Fields** section. Members who use Porter also have an **App access** card here. |
| **Attendance History** | The member's attendance at each event, occurrence by occurrence, marked **Attended** or **Absent**. You can check the member in by hand or remove a check-in made in error. See [The Attendance History tab](#the-attendance-history-tab). |
| **Ministry Assignments** | Each ministry and role, with when they joined and left. Current roles show an **Active** badge. |
| **Household** | The member's household, whether they are the head, and the other people in it. Use **Assign to Household** or **Remove from Household** to change it. Care Managers can see this tab but can't change households. See [Households](/guides/households). |
| Your group name | The member's group. This tab uses your branch's own name for groups, for example **Group** or **Cell**. Care Managers can see this tab but can't change the member's group. See [Groups](/guides/groups). |
| **Care** | Care cases and notes for this member. See [The Care tab](#the-care-tab). |
| **Change History** | A timeline of changes to the member's core details, such as name, date of birth, gender, contact details, address and status. See [Change history](/guides/change-history). |

Removing someone from a household does not change their attendance history.

### The Attendance History tab

The **Attendance History** tab shows, event by event, which occurrences the member attended and which they missed. An **occurrence** is one date and time of an event, for example this Sunday's service.

- **The date range.** It starts on the last 90 days. It works the same way as on the Gifting page. See [Change the dates](/guides/gifting-and-donations#change-the-dates).
- **The event chips.** Choose **All events** or one event. The tab opens on your **Sunday Service** event if it has occurrences in the date range, otherwise on the first event that does.

A line at the top sums up what you're looking at, for example "12 occurrences · attended 9". Below it, each event has its own row showing how many occurrences there were, how many the member attended and missed, when they last attended, and their attendance rate as a percentage. Click an event to open or close its list of occurrences.

Each occurrence shows its date and time and is marked **Attended**, **Absent** or **Cancelled**. For check-ins, a badge shows how the member was checked in, for example **Manual**, **QR Code**, **Kiosk** or **Porter**.

#### Correct a member's attendance

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin, Editor, Care Manager

- **Check a member in:** on an occurrence marked **Absent**, click **Check in**.
- **Remove a check-in made in error:** on an occurrence marked **Attended**, click **Remove**, then **Remove** again to confirm. This cannot be undone.

These are manual corrections, so they work whether or not the occurrence's check-in window is open. You can't check anyone in to a cancelled occurrence. For a member who was once a visitor, check-ins from before they became a member are listed as **Recorded as a visitor** and can't be removed here.

### The Care tab

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin, Editor, Care Manager

The **Care** tab brings together the follow-up your team is doing for this person. It shows:

- **Member care**: their open follow-up case if they have been missing services, with its status, who it is assigned to and when it is due. Click **Open in Member care** to work the case.
- **Pastoral care**: pastoral cases such as a hospital visit or a bereavement. Click **Add a care case** to open a new one. On wider screens, **Add a care case** is also in the page's top bar.
- **Notes**: notes about the member, shared with Member care.
- **Visitor care history**: for someone who started as a visitor, a read-only record of how they were followed up before they became a member.

Confidential pastoral cases only appear for the people allowed to see them. To learn how cases, statuses, due dates and confidentiality work, see [Care](/guides/care).

### App access

If a member has been given access to Porter, the **Details** tab shows an **App access** card. It shows their Porter access level, **Porter · Worker** or **Porter · Admin**, their **Sign-in email**, and a **Status** of "Email confirmed" or "Invite pending". Their Porter check-ins and equipment loans are recorded against this member record. See [Porter](/guides/porter).

## Edit a member

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin, Editor, Care Manager

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the edit panel">
    On the member page, click **Edit** in the top bar, or tap the pencil on a phone. On tablets and computers, you can also open the **⋯** menu on the member's row in the list and choose **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the details">
    The **Edit member** panel has the same fields as **Add Member**, plus **Status**, where you choose **Newcomer**, **Active** or **Inactive**. If you change the email address or mobile number, the duplicate check runs again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save Changes**. You see "Member updated", and the panel switches to a read-only view. Click **Edit details** if you need to change something else.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Clicking a member in a dashboard widget or a report opens the same **Edit member** panel, so you can update them without leaving the page.

## Lifecycle statuses

Every member has one of three lifecycle statuses:

- **Newcomer**: set automatically when a member is first added.
- **Active**: the member attends regularly.
- **Inactive**: the member has stopped attending.

You can change a member's status yourself in the **Edit member** panel.

<Accordion title="How automation rules can change statuses">
Bethel ChMS can update lifecycle statuses for you based on attendance. With the default settings:

- **Newcomer to Active** promotes a member to Active after 4 attendances within 90 days.
- **Active to Inactive** marks a member Inactive after 30 days with no attendance.

These rules are off until an Org Owner turns them on. Thresholds are set for the whole organisation, and each branch can override them. In the sidebar, open **Organisation** and click **Automation**. See [Automation rules](/guides/automation-rules).
</Accordion>

## Member QR codes

Each member has a personal QR code they can scan to check in at a kiosk. See [Recording attendance](/guides/recording-attendance) and [Kiosk mode](/guides/kiosk-mode).

### Show or email one member's code

On the member page, click **QR Code** in the top bar, tap the **QR code** quick action, or open the **More actions** menu (⋮) and choose **Show QR code**. The dialog shows the member's name and code, with "Scan this code to record attendance".

- Click **Full screen** to show the code edge to edge, so it is easy to scan from your screen. Click **Done** to go back.
- Click **Email code** to send the code to the member's email address. Only Org Owners and Org Admins see this button, and it is greyed out if the member has no email address.

A member can only be emailed their code once an hour. If you try again sooner, you see "A QR code was emailed to this member in the last hour."

### Email codes to many members

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Email QR codes">
    On the member list, open the **⋯** menu next to **Filters** and choose **Email QR codes**. To email only some members, select them first and click **Email QR codes** in the selection bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who should receive a code">
    Choose **Selected members**, **All members** (members across your whole organisation, not only the branch you are viewing), or **Filter** to narrow by status, age group and group. The dialog shows how many members will be considered. Click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review before sending">
    Check who will receive an email, and who will be skipped because they have no email address or were sent a code in the last hour. You can download each skipped list as a CSV file to follow up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the emails">
    Click **Send now**. **Send complete** shows how many were sent, skipped and failed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Select members

To work with several members at once, select them in the list. On tablets and computers, tick the box on each row, or tick the box in the header row to select every member on the page. On a phone, tap **Select** first to show the checkboxes, and **Done** when you finish.

The selection bar shows how many members you have selected and offers:

- **Email QR codes**: email the selected members their codes (Org Owner and Org Admin only).
- **Export**: download a CSV file of just the selected members.
- **Merge**: merge two records for the same person (Org Owner, Org Admin and Branch Admin only). Select exactly two members from the same branch. See [Duplicates and merging](/guides/duplicates-and-merging).

On tablets and computers, each row also has its own **⋯** menu with **Open member**, **Edit**, **Call** (when the member has a mobile number) and, for roles that can delete members, **Delete**.

## Export members

Open the **⋯** menu next to **Filters** and choose **Export CSV**. The file includes every member that matches your current search and filters, so set your filters first. To export only certain members, select them and click **Export** in the selection bar instead.

The file has the columns **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email**, **Mobile**, **Date of Birth**, **Gender**, **Nationality**, **Address**, **Post Code**, **Status**, **Created At** and **Tags**, followed by a column for each member custom field. Custom fields that have since been removed still appear, marked "(removed)".

On most phones and tablets, the share sheet opens so you can save the file or send it on. Otherwise, the file downloads.

The same **⋯** menu also has **Possible duplicates** and, for Org Owners, Org Admins and Branch Admins, **Import members**. See [Importing members](/guides/importing-members).

## Delete a member

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin, Care Manager

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the delete">
    On the member page, open the **More actions** menu (⋮) and choose **Delete member**. On tablets and computers, you can also open the member's **⋯** menu in the member list and choose **Delete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check what will close">
    The **Delete Member** dialog lists any active ministry assignments that will be closed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Delete**. You see "Member deleted". If you started on the member page, you return to the member list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Deleting a member hides the record from your lists, but it is not removed straight away. Their past attendance records are kept.

## Duplicate members

If the same person has been added twice, you can merge the two records into one.

- When Bethel ChMS finds likely duplicates in your branch, the member list shows a banner with the number of possible duplicates waiting for review and a **Review pairs** button.
- You can open the queue at any time from the **⋯** menu next to **Filters** by choosing **Possible duplicates**.
- You can also select exactly two members in the list and click **Merge**.

See [Duplicates and merging](/guides/duplicates-and-merging) for the full steps and what a merge changes.
