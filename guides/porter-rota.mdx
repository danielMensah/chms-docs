The **service rota** lives in Porter, on the **Rota** tab. A team is a ministry you already have in Bethel ChMS (Ushering, Media, Worship). Each team has its own positions, such as *Door*, *Car park* or *Sound desk*, and you set how many people each service needs in each position. People are told about their slots in Porter, by push notification on their phone, or by email when notifications aren't turned on.

<Note>
The rota is part of Porter. Someone can be put on the rota only if they are on the team in Bethel ChMS **and** have Porter access. The team page lists any team members who don't have Porter access yet.
</Note>

## Who can do what

| Role | Set up positions and needs | Fill and change the rota | See their own rota, can't make it, swap, away dates | Rota reminder time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Porter admin | Every team | Every team | Yes | Yes |
| Team leader or assistant leader with Porter access | Their teams | Their teams | Yes | No |
| Porter worker on the team | No | No | Yes | No |

A leader sees only their own teams, on every screen and in every notice.

## Set up a team

1. In Porter, open **Rota → Teams and positions** and pick the team.
2. Add the team's **positions** (for example *Door*, *Car park*, *Welcome desk*). Tap **Reorder** to move them up or down; archiving a position keeps its history and removes its future slots (anyone already told is told it's off).
3. Under **Services**, set how many people each regular service needs in each position. `0` means the team isn't needed at that service.

To need a different number on one date (a baptism Sunday, say), change it on that date's rota page.

## Fill the rota

**Rota** opens the **Team rota** calendar for schedulers: a month (or week, on a phone) of services, each showing how many places are filled, and a red warning when a service in the next 48 hours is short (an amber mark when a gap is further out).

1. Open a service and tap **Add person** on a position.
2. Porter shows who is free and warns you before you pick someone who is **away** that day, **already serving** at that service, or **serving somewhere else at the same time**.
3. When the date looks right, tap **Notify**. Everyone you added since the last Notify gets one message listing their new slots. People are only told when you press Notify, so you can change your mind first.


**Copy from** fills this date from another date of the same service. **Repeat** copies this date onto up to 8 later dates. Porter skips anyone who is away, already serving, serving elsewhere at that time or no longer on the team, tells you who it skipped and why, and leaves the copies un-notified until you press Notify.

Removing someone who had been told sends them a short notice. Someone who leaves the team or loses Porter access shows as **No longer on the team** and their place counts as empty.

## What workers see

- **Home** lists their next serving dates under **Coming up**. On the day it opens on *You're serving at Sunday Service*, with their slot (for example *Door · Ushering*), the times and the place, and a **Check in** button; once they're checked in it offers **My rota**.
- **Rota → My rota** lists their slots by date, with a **Month** view that marks serving days and away days.
- **Can't make it** gives the slot back with an optional reason. The team's leaders, and whoever put them on the rota, are told (or every Porter admin, when there is nobody else).
- **Swap** asks a named teammate to take the slot. Porter greys out teammates who are already serving or busy elsewhere, and warns if they are away. The slot stays theirs until the teammate accepts; then both of them, and the leaders, are told.
- **Unavailable dates** lets them mark days they're away, with an optional note that only they see. If they're already on the rota on one of those days, Porter lists the slot and offers **Can't make it**.

## Reminders

Everyone on the rota gets a reminder before each service they serve at. The default is **24 hours before**. A Porter admin can change it in **Setup → Rota reminders** (2, 12, 24 or 48 hours before). The reminder says the time and place, for example *You're serving tomorrow at 10:00 · Door · Sunday Service · Main Hall*. Someone added after their reminder time doesn't get one.

## On the day

From 30 minutes before a service until it ends, schedulers see **Not here yet** first in Home's lists: everyone on their teams who is rostered but hasn't checked in, with a call button when Porter has their mobile number, and any position that is still empty, with **Open rota**. It goes away once everyone is checked in.

**Manual check-in** (Porter admins: **Setup → Check-in → Manual check-in**, also linked from Home) lists the people **On the rota tonight** for the chosen service first, with their position, then **Other workers**. Anyone already checked in shows **Already in**. See [Check workers in by hand](/guides/porter-check-in#check-workers-in-by-hand).

## When a service is cancelled or moved

If someone in Bethel ChMS cancels a service, changes its date or time, changes its location, or deletes the event, Porter tells everyone who had been told about a slot at it (by Notify or a reminder), and gives each team's leaders, and whoever put people on the rota, a summary. Porter waits 5 minutes after the last change so a few quick edits send one message. A rescheduled service gets a fresh reminder for its new time. People who hadn't been told yet hear nothing until the next Notify or reminder, which gives them the new time. Changes to a service that has already ended don't send anything.

## Add the rota to a calendar

**My rota → Add to my calendar** gives each person a private calendar link that works in Apple Calendar, Outlook and Google Calendar. It updates by itself when the rota changes.

- **My rota** shows only their own slots for the next 6 months.
- **Team calendars** (also on each team's page) show every service the team serves, with who is on each position and how many places are still empty, for example *Sunday Service · Ushering (4/5)*. Anyone on the team, or who schedules it, can add one.

A link is shown once. **Reset link** makes a new one and stops the old one straight away. Team calendars show teammates' names, so only share a team link with people on that team. A calendar stops updating when the person leaves the team, loses Porter access, or Porter is switched off for your church.

## Privacy

Unavailable dates are kept until 30 days after they end, and their notes are never shown to schedulers. Calendar links are stored only as a fingerprint, never the link itself. Service change records are deleted 30 days after the notices go out.
