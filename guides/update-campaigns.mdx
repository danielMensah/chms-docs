An **update campaign** asks a group of members to check the details your church holds for them and correct anything that is out of date. You choose who to ask and which fields and tags to include. Members get a personal link by email, or you can share one public link and QR code. They fill in a short form on their phone or computer, and you review their changes before they reach the member record, or let them apply automatically.

Use update campaigns for a yearly data refresh, an address check, collecting missing mobile numbers, or asking members to confirm tags such as a serving team.

<Info>
**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin. Update campaigns are managed at organisation level. Branch Admins, Editors, Treasurers and Care Managers do not see **Update Campaigns** and cannot open campaign pages.
</Info>

<Note>
Update campaigns are included in the Standard plan with no limit on the number of campaigns. See [Feature availability by plan](/guides/feature-availability-by-plan).
</Note>

## How campaigns work

Each campaign has one of three statuses:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | You are still setting the campaign up. You can change the name, message, audience, fields, conditions, dates and switches. |
| **Open** | The campaign has launched. The audience is frozen, links work and members can send updates. |
| **Closed** | The campaign was stopped or reached its close date. Links stop working and no more responses are accepted. |

A typical campaign runs like this:

1. Build the campaign in the campaign builder and save it as a draft.
2. [Test the form](/guides/campaign-test-the-form) to check your questions.
3. Launch the campaign. This freezes the audience and opens it for responses.
4. Share it: send the personalised emails, and use the public link and QR code if you like.
5. Track responses, send reminders and review the changes members send.
6. Stop the campaign, or let it close on its close date.

Launching freezes the audience. Members who join a matching group, ministry, age group or tag after launch are not added to the campaign.

<Warning>
Launching does not send the personalised emails. After you launch, send them yourself from the **Share** page or the campaign page, ideally straight away. See [Send personalised emails](#send-personalised-emails).
</Warning>

## Open Update Campaigns

In the sidebar, open **Giving & outreach** and click **Update Campaigns**. On a tablet held portrait, or on a phone, tap **More**, then **Update Campaigns**. If you are new to the menus, see [Find your way around](/getting-started/find-your-way-around).

The **Campaigns** page lists every campaign, newest first. Use **Search campaigns** to find one by name, and the chips at the top to show **All**, **Open**, **Draft**, **Closed** or **Pending review** campaigns. Each chip shows how many campaigns it holds.

For each campaign, the list shows:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The campaign name. |
| **Close date** | When the campaign closes or closed, or **No close date set**. |
| **Targeted** | How many members were in the audience at launch. On smaller screens, drafts show **Not launched yet**. |
| **Status** | **Draft**, **Open** or **Closed**. |
| **Submitted** | How many members have sent updates. |
| **Pending review** | How many members have changes waiting for you to approve or reject. |

On a phone, campaigns with changes waiting show a **to review** chip with the number of members waiting.

Click a campaign to open its campaign page. Each row also has a **⋯** menu with the actions that suit its status:

| Status | Menu actions |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | **Continue setup**, **Test the form**, **Open campaign**, **Delete campaign** |
| **Open** | **Open campaign**, **Review submissions** (when changes are waiting), **Share & send**, **Stop campaign** |
| **Closed** | **Open campaign**, **Review submissions** (when changes are waiting), **Delete campaign** |

## Create a campaign

Click **New campaign** to open the campaign builder. On a phone, the **New campaign** button floats above the tab bar. The builder has five numbered sections: **Details**, **Audience**, **Fields & tags**, **Conditions** and **Dates & behaviour**. A bar at the bottom of the screen holds **Save draft** and **Create & share**, plus **Test the form** once you have saved the draft.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the details">
    In **Details**, enter a **Name** for the campaign, up to 150 characters. Add an optional **Message to members**, up to 2,000 characters. The message appears in the email and at the top of the member form, so write it the way you would speak to your congregation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the audience">
    In **Audience**, choose **Org-wide** to ask everyone across every branch, or **Segment** to ask specific people. See [Choose the audience](#choose-the-audience).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the draft">
    Click **Save draft**. Saving once lets you see the audience preview, add conditions and test the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose fields and tags">
    In **Fields & tags**, click **Choose fields and tags** and pick what members should check. See [Choose fields and tags](#choose-fields-and-tags).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add conditions (optional)">
    In **Conditions**, add rules that show extra questions only when an earlier answer calls for them. See [Show follow-up questions with conditions](#show-follow-up-questions-with-conditions).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set dates and behaviour">
    In **Dates & behaviour**, choose the close date, whether changes need review, and whether to send automatic reminders. See [Set dates and behaviour](#set-dates-and-behaviour).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test, then launch">
    Click **Save draft**, then **Test the form** to try the member form yourself. When you are happy, click **Create & share** to launch. See [Launch a campaign](#launch-a-campaign).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Some parts of the builder save as you go and some do not. Once you have saved the draft once, the audience and the fields save automatically, and each condition saves when you click **Save condition**. The name, message, dates and the two switches save only when you click **Save draft**. If you leave the builder, for example to test the form, without clicking **Save draft**, changes to these are lost.
</Note>

### Choose the audience

| Option | Who it includes |
| --- | --- |
| **Org-wide** | Every member across every branch. This is the default. |
| **Segment** | Members you pick, plus everyone in the ministries, groups, age groups and tags you pick. |

For a segment, use the **Members**, **Ministries**, **Groups**, **Age groups** and **Tags** pickers. A member who matches any one of your choices is included, so picking a ministry and a tag includes everyone in the ministry as well as everyone with the tag. To find a member, search by their first name or their last name. Ministries and groups count their current members only. Until you pick at least one item, a segment has no audience.

The audience includes every matching member, whatever their membership status.

After you save the draft, the **Audience preview** shows how many members match, how many have an email address, and how many cannot be contacted. It updates as you change the audience.

### Choose fields and tags

Click **Choose fields and tags** to open the **Fields & tags** sheet. Tick the items you want members to check, grouped under **Standard fields**, **Custom fields** and **Tags**, then click **Done**. Members see only the items you choose.

The standard fields are **First name**, **Last name**, **Date of birth**, **Gender**, **Nationality**, **Email**, **Mobile number**, **Address** and **Post code**. On the member form, **Address** is labelled **Home address**. Custom fields are the extra member fields your organisation has set up. See [Custom fields](/guides/organisation-settings#custom-fields). Tags come from your organisation's member tags. If you have none yet, create them on the [Tags](/guides/tags) page first.

Once fields are selected:

- **Required or optional:** each selected standard field has a **Required** switch, which starts off. When it is on, members must fill the field in before they can save. **First name** and **Last name** are always required and show a lock. Custom fields follow the required setting they already have. Tags are never required.
- **Order:** when you have more than one standard field or more than one custom field, you can change their order. With a mouse, drag a row by its handle. On a touch screen, click **Reorder**, use the move up and move down buttons, then click **Done**. The member form follows this order, with standard fields first, then custom fields, then tags.
- **Remove an item:** click the **X** next to it, or untick it in the sheet.

If you remove a field or tag that a condition uses, Bethel ChMS asks you to confirm. Click **Remove all** to remove the field together with the conditions or follow-up questions that depend on it, or **Keep field** to leave everything as it is.

<Tip>
Select only the fields you need members to check. Shorter forms are quicker to complete and leave you less to review.
</Tip>

### Set dates and behaviour

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Open date** | The date you plan to start the campaign. Optional. |
| **Close date** | The last day members can respond. Links keep working until the end of that day. Optional. It must be on or after the open date. Leave it blank for a campaign that stays open until you stop it. |
| **Require review before applying** | On by default. See [Review mode](#review-mode). |
| **Nudge non-responders** | Off by default. When on, Bethel ChMS emails reminders to members who have not responded. See [Send reminders](#send-reminders). |

<Info>
A campaign opens for responses as soon as you launch it. Launch it on the day you want responses to start.
</Info>

### Check the email members will receive

On a large screen, a preview of the campaign email sits beside the builder. On a smaller screen, click the eye icon (**Preview**) at the top to open the **Email preview** sheet. The preview shows your message in place, so you can check it reads well before you launch.

## Show follow-up questions with conditions

A **condition** shows extra questions only when they are relevant. For example, ask for a new home address only when a member says they have moved, or ask about access needs only when a member picks a particular option.

Each condition is one rule: when a member answers a chosen question a certain way, the form reveals one or more follow-up questions.

You can add and change conditions only while the campaign is a draft, and only after you have saved the draft once.

### Add a condition

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the condition sheet">
    In the **Conditions** section, click **Add condition**. The sheet builds the rule as a sentence in three steps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the trigger question">
    Under **Trigger question**, choose the question whose answer reveals the follow-ups. Only some question types can be triggers. See [Which questions can be triggers](#which-questions-can-be-triggers).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the trigger answers">
    Under **Fires when the answer is…**, choose the answer that reveals the follow-ups. For **Gender** and **Single Select** custom fields, tick one or more answers. If you tick several, any of them reveals the follow-ups. For **Yes/No** custom fields and tags, pick either **Yes / assigned** or **No**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the follow-up questions">
    Under **Follow-up questions to show**, tick the questions to reveal. For each one, choose **Required when shown** (the default) or **Optional**. A required follow-up only has to be answered when the member can actually see it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the condition">
    Check the sentence at the bottom of the sheet, which starts with **Reads:**, then click **Save condition**. The button stays unavailable until you have chosen a trigger, at least one answer and at least one follow-up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Which questions can be triggers

Only these selected items can be trigger questions:

- The standard **Gender** field
- **Yes/No** custom fields
- **Single Select** custom fields
- Tags

Text, number and date fields, and the other standard fields, cannot be triggers. One question can trigger more than one condition. A question that is already a follow-up appears greyed out.

These cannot be follow-up questions:

- Tags
- **First name** and **Last name**
- Custom fields that are already marked as required
- The trigger question itself
- Any question already used as a trigger or follow-up in another condition

If nothing is left to choose, the sheet shows **No eligible follow-up questions yet**. Add more fields in **Fields & tags** first.

### Edit or remove a condition

Each saved condition appears as a card that reads as a sentence, for example **When Gender is Female or Other** followed by the number of questions it asks. For a tag, the card says whether the tag is **Assigned** or **Not assigned**. Under the sentence, each follow-up is listed as **Required when shown** or **Optional**.

- Click **Edit** to change the condition.
- Click **Remove**, then **Remove condition**, to delete it. Members are no longer asked its follow-up questions, but the fields stay selected in the campaign.

A few rules keep conditions simple:

- Conditions are one level deep. A follow-up question cannot reveal further follow-ups.
- Each question can be the follow-up of only one condition.
- Tags can trigger a condition but cannot be follow-ups.

## Test the form before you launch

Once you have saved the draft, click **Test the form** in the bar at the bottom of the builder (**Test** on a phone). The preview shows the form members will see, with every field blank, and nothing you enter is saved.

See [Test the form](/guides/campaign-test-the-form) for details.

## Launch a campaign

You can launch in two ways:

- In the builder, click **Create & share**. Bethel ChMS saves the draft, launches the campaign and takes you to the **Share** page.
- On a draft's campaign page, open the **⋯** menu (**More campaign actions**), choose **Launch campaign**, then confirm with **Launch campaign**. You then land on the **Share** page.

**Create & share** stays unavailable until the campaign is ready. The bar at the bottom of the builder lists what is missing, such as a campaign name, at least one field or tag, a saved draft, or an audience of at least one member. On larger screens it also shows **Ready to launch** or **Not ready to launch** and how many members are in the audience.

When you launch:

- The audience is frozen.
- Members with an email address or a mobile number can respond. Those with an email address receive their personal link when you [send the emails](#send-personalised-emails). Those with only a mobile number can use the public link or QR code.
- Members with neither are **Not contactable**. They get no personal link. On the public link and QR code, people confirm who they are with the email address or mobile number on their member record, so a member with neither cannot use those either.
- Personal links, the public link and the QR code work straight away.

After launch, a campaign can no longer be edited. **Continue setup** becomes **View setup**, which shows the builder read-only.

## Review mode

The **Require review before applying** switch decides what happens to changes members send through their personal link.

| Setting | Shown as | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On | **Requires review** | Changes wait in the review queue until you approve or reject them. |
| Off | **Auto-update** | Changes from personal links apply to the member record as soon as the member saves. |

Changes sent through the public link or the QR code are always reviewed, even when review is off. Anyone can open the public link, so a person on staff checks those changes before they reach a member record.

## Share a campaign

Open the campaign and click **Share & send**. The **Share** page has three sections, in this order: **QR code**, **Public link** and **Email**. It also shows a **Campaign launched** label, the close date and the review mode, and a **Review** button with the number of members whose changes are waiting.

| Section | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **QR code** | Display or print a code people scan with their phone camera, for example during a service or at the welcome desk. |
| **Public link** | Share one link in a bulletin, a WhatsApp group or a text message. |
| **Email** | Send every member with an email address their own personal link. This is the main way members respond. |

The QR code and the public link open the same page. Anyone who uses them must first confirm who they are, and their changes are always reviewed.

### Send personalised emails

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the email">
    In the **Email** section, look over the **Email preview** to make sure it says what you intend.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start sending">
    Click **Send personalised emails**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The confirmation shows the subject line and the review mode. Click **Send emails**. Once sending starts, it cannot be undone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The page shows progress while the emails go out. Each member gets their own link.

You can also send from the campaign page. While no emails have gone out, a banner at the top of an open campaign has its own **Send personalised emails** button.

Sending again later only reaches members who have not been emailed yet, or whose email failed. Nobody gets the first email twice. If some emails fail, the **Email** section says how many. Click **Retry** to try them again, or **Export the list** to download the failed addresses as a CSV file.

Members without an email address can still respond through the QR code or the public link, using their mobile number to confirm who they are.

### Use the QR code

In the **QR code** section:

- **Show full screen** displays the code large and bright, with a short instruction to scan it, so you can hold it up during a service or leave it on the welcome desk. Click the **X** in the corner when you are done.
- **Download PNG** saves the code as an image for slides or printed material.
- **Print** prints the QR code section on its own.

### Share the public link

In the **Public link** section, the full link is shown. Click **Copy link** to copy it, or **Share…** to open your device's share options, for example to send it to a WhatsApp group. Where sharing is not available, **Share…** copies the link instead.

### The email members receive

The email has the subject **Help us keep your details up to date**. It greets the member by first name, names your organisation, includes your **Message to members**, and has an **Update My Details** button that opens their personal form.

The **Email preview** in the **Email** section of the **Share** page shows the email. The preview uses "Friend" where each member will see their own first name. Reminders use the same email.

## What members see

Members open the form in one of two ways:

| How they arrive | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Personal link from the email | The form opens straight away, with a greeting by first name and their current details filled in. |
| Public link or QR code | In **Step 1 of 2**, the member enters their **Last name** and their **Email or mobile number**, using the switch to choose email or mobile, then taps **Continue**. In **Step 2 of 2**, their form opens with their current details filled in. A **Start again** bar lets someone who is not that person go back. |

The page shows the campaign name and your **Message to members** above the form. The form shows only the fields and tags you selected, in the order you set. Required fields are marked with an asterisk.

The form is built for phones as well as computers:

- Dates of birth and other dates use separate **Day**, **Month** and **Year** boxes.
- **Gender**, Yes/No fields and short option lists show as buttons to tap.
- **Nationality** opens a searchable list.
- Email and mobile fields bring up the matching keyboard.
- Tags appear under **Does this describe you?** (or **Do these describe you?** when there are several), each with a switch for **Yes, that's me** or **No, not me**. Switching a tag to **No, not me** asks for it to be removed from the member.
- The **Save my details** button stays at the bottom of the screen. If something needs fixing, a message there links to each field to check.

If the campaign has conditions, follow-up questions appear directly under the trigger question as soon as the member gives a matching answer. If the member changes their answer, the follow-ups hide again. Anything typed into a hidden follow-up is never sent or saved, so member records only reflect what the member actually saw and answered.

After saving, the member sees a thank-you message with a **What you updated** list. When review applies, it adds that a staff member will review and confirm the changes shortly. If nothing changed, the page thanks them for checking. On the public link, a **Done** button clears the page so the next person can use the same phone or tablet.

Each member can respond once per campaign, through either their personal link or the public link. If they submit again, their first set of changes is kept. If a member saves without changing anything, it does not count as a response, so they can come back later.

When a link has expired, or the campaign has been stopped, closed or deleted, the member sees a message that the link is no longer active and is asked to contact the church office.

<Info>
For privacy, every unsuccessful attempt on the public link shows the same message, asking the person to check their details or contact the church office. The page never reveals whether the name, email address or mobile number was the problem, whether the person is part of this campaign, or whether they have already responded. After too many attempts from the same device or network in a few minutes, successful or not, further attempts show the same message for a short while.
</Info>

## Track responses

Click a campaign on the **Campaigns** page to open its campaign page. At the top you see the campaign name, its status, its close date and review mode, and the number of recipients.

A row of shortcut tiles takes you to the main tasks: **Review submissions** (with the number of members whose changes are waiting), **Share & send**, **Test the form** (drafts only) and **Continue setup** or **View setup**. On a phone the tiles use shorter labels, such as **Review** and **Share**. The **⋯** menu (**More campaign actions**) has the actions that suit the campaign's status, including **Launch campaign** for a draft, **Stop campaign** for an open campaign, and **Delete campaign** for a draft or closed campaign.

The page has two tabs: **Overview** and **Recipients**.

### Overview tab

The **Delivery & response** card shows tiles that update as members respond:

| Group | Tiles |
| --- | --- |
| Journey | **Targeted** (frozen at launch), **Sent**, **Opened**, **Submitted** |
| Outcomes | **Review** (members with changes waiting, click to open the review queue), **Approved**, **Rejected** |
| No response | **No-email**, **No-reply**, **Expired**, **Not-contactable** |

**Opened** is an estimate. It counts the first time each member opened their personal link, not the email itself.

Below the tiles, a chart shows how many members reached each stage, from targeted to approved, with the same numbers in a table.

The **Submissions export** card has an **Export CSV** button. The file lists every change members submitted, with the member, the field, the old value (**Was**), the new value (**Now**), the review status, who reviewed it and when, and any review note.

### Recipients tab

The **Recipients** tab lists everyone in the audience, 25 at a time, sorted by name. Before launch it is empty.

- Use **Search recipients** to find someone by name.
- Use the chips to show **All**, **Pending review**, **Not responded**, **Approved**, **Rejected**, **Expired** or **Not contactable** recipients.
- Use the filters to narrow by response, by delivery (**Sent**, **Skipped (no email)**, **Send failed**, **Not sent**) or by whether the link was opened.
- Click a name to open the member's record.

The **Last activity** column shows when the member was sent the email and whether they opened their link.

Each recipient's response status works like this:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Not responded** | The member has not sent any changes yet. |
| **Pending review** | At least one of their changes is waiting for review. |
| **Approved** | At least one of their changes was approved. |
| **Rejected** | Every change they sent was rejected. |
| **Expired** | The campaign closed before they responded. |
| **Not contactable** | They had no email address or mobile number at launch. |

### Send reminders

**Manual reminders.** On the **Overview** tab, under the no-response tiles, or on the **Recipients** tab, click **Remind non-responders**. The button shows how many people will get a reminder. The confirmation shows the subject line. Click the send button, for example **Send 12 reminders**, to email each recipient who has not responded and has an email address. Members without an email address are skipped, and the result shows how many reminders were sent, skipped or failed. You can send manual reminders as often as you need.

To remind only some people, select their rows on the **Recipients** tab, click **Remind**, then **Send reminders**. Anyone in your selection who has already responded is skipped.

**Automatic reminders.** When **Nudge non-responders** is on, Bethel ChMS checks open campaigns every night and emails a reminder to members who have not responded and have an email address. Nobody gets an automatic reminder if they were reminded in the last seven days, whether automatically or by a manual reminder. Reminders stop when the member responds or the campaign closes.

## Review submissions

When changes need review, open the review queue from any of these:

- The **Review submissions** tile on the campaign page
- The **Review** tile on the **Overview** tab, when changes are waiting
- **Review submissions** in the campaign's **⋯** menu, or in the row menu on the **Campaigns** page
- The **Review** button on the **Share** page

The **Review** page shows one card per member. The card shows the member's name (click it to open their record), whether they used their personal email link or the public link, and when they submitted. Each change shows the field, the old value under **Was** and the new value under **Now**.

- Click **Approve** or **Reject** on a single change.
- Click **Approve all** or **Reject all** on a card to handle every change from that member.
- Tick changes or whole cards, then use **Approve** or **Reject** in the bar that appears to handle many at once. **Select all** ticks everything in the queue.

Approving writes the new value to the member's record straight away.

Rejecting keeps the member's record as it is and discards the submitted value. Every rejection asks you to confirm. You can add an optional reason: choose **Couldn't confirm**, **Looks like a typo** or **Not this member**, type a note of up to 500 characters, or both. The reason is saved with the review history and appears in the submissions export. The member is not told that a change was rejected.

You can keep reviewing after a campaign has closed.

## Stop, close or delete a campaign

**Stop a campaign.** To end an open campaign early, choose **Stop campaign** from the campaign's **⋯** menu or the row menu on the **Campaigns** page, then confirm with **Stop campaign**. Members who had not responded change to **Expired**, and personal links, the public link and the QR code stop working.

**Let it close.** A campaign with a close date stops accepting responses at the end of that day. Bethel ChMS then marks it **Closed** and changes anyone who had not responded to **Expired**. A campaign without a close date stays open until you stop it.

**Delete a campaign.** You can delete draft and closed campaigns. Choose **Delete campaign** from the **⋯** menu or the row menu, then confirm. The campaign is archived and removed from your list. To delete an open campaign, stop it first.

<Warning>
Once a campaign is closed, its personal links, public link and QR code no longer accept responses. If you printed the QR code, take it down.
</Warning>

## Data retention

Bethel ChMS keeps campaign response records for tracking and audit purposes. The old and new values members submitted are removed once the campaign has been closed for 12 months.
