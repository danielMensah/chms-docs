**What's New** keeps you informed when Bethel ChMS ships updates. Each release comes with a short release note listing what is new, what has improved and what has been fixed, so you can catch up without leaving your work.

Release notes are informational only. Reading or dismissing them does not change any records, settings, permissions or billing.

## Latest release: v0.12.0

**Porter: the new app for your service teams, plus check-in by name**

- **Porter** is a phone app for your workers and volunteers. The Bethel ChMS team switches it on for your church on request. You give a member Porter access from the **Users** page, and they sign in and scan the QR code on site to check in for the day. Their check-ins show up as attendance on the event in Bethel ChMS. See [Porter](/guides/porter) and [Check in with Porter](/guides/porter-check-in).
- **Equipment register**: list your church's equipment, print QR labels, group items into sets and tag them by department. Workers scan a label to take equipment out and scan it again to return it, marking anything faulty or missing on the way. See [Porter equipment](/guides/porter-equipment).
- **Fault reports and alerts**: workers can report a fault with photos at any time. Porter admins get an **Alerts** tab and a push notification for every fault or missing item, and can resolve faults or mark items found from the same screen. See [Porter alerts](/guides/porter-alerts).
- **Return reminders**: workers who took equipment that day get a reminder before the event ends, at a time you set for each event (for example, 10 minutes before Sunday service ends). See [Porter alerts](/guides/porter-alerts).
- **Manual check-in and history**: Porter admins can check a worker in by hand and see a full history of check-ins and equipment, including scans that were rejected and why. See [Check in with Porter](/guides/porter-check-in).
- **Find by name at the kiosk**: returning members can check themselves in by typing their name, with no email, mobile number or QR code needed. It is switched on per branch after an **Org Owner** or **Org Admin** accepts a safeguarding disclosure, and children never appear in the suggestions. See [Find by name (Quick Find)](/guides/quick-find).
- **Fixed**: tighter security checks, so records can only ever be edited or deleted from within their own organisation.

## Earlier releases

### v0.11.0

**Smarter campaign forms with follow-up questions and a safe preview**

- Campaign questions can have conditions, so follow-up questions only appear when they are relevant. For example, only ask for a mobile number when a member answers a certain way.
- Members filling in an update form see follow-up questions appear as they answer, so the form stays short.
- **Test the form** lets you fill in a campaign form exactly as a member would before you send it. Nothing is saved to anyone's record.
- Answers to questions hidden by a condition are never saved, so member records only reflect what the member actually saw and answered.

Update campaigns are managed at organisation level by **Org Owner** and **Org Admin** users. **Branch Admin** and **Editor** users do not see **Update Campaigns**. See [Update campaigns](/guides/update-campaigns) and [Test the form](/guides/campaign-test-the-form).

### v0.10.0

**Redesigned campaign insights and a dedicated follow-up role**

- A new role gives a team member access to just **Members** and **Care**, so you can delegate pastoral follow-up without granting full admin rights. The role was released as Backslider Manager and is now called **Care Manager**. See [Care](/guides/care) and [Roles and permissions](/guides/roles-and-permissions).
- Check-in plays a confirmation sound when a scan succeeds, so greeters know a member has been checked in without watching the screen. See [The check-in chime](/guides/kiosk-mode#the-check-in-chime).
- Campaign pages were redesigned with a clearer delivery and response overview and an easier-to-read recipient list.
- Filtering by date is smoother across reports and lists, with an improved date range picker.

### v0.9.0

**Let members update their own details**

- **Member update campaigns** ask members to review and update their own details, including custom fields and tags, through a personalised email link, a public link or a QR code. You review and approve what they submit, or apply it automatically, with reminders and a tracking dashboard. See [Update campaigns](/guides/update-campaigns).
- Fixed: a member you marked as **Welcomed back** could be flagged again the next day. Welcoming someone back now records the date they actually returned, so the flag stays cleared.
- Improved: marking a flagged member as **No concern** now shows a short explanation first. It clears the current flag, but the person can be flagged again later if they still meet the rule.

The **What's New** page in the app holds the full history, including releases before v0.9.0.

## Where to find updates

Bethel ChMS shows release notes in two places:

- The **What's New** popup, which can appear after you sign in or open the app.
- The **What's New** page, which lists every release note, newest first.

To open the **What's New** page:

- **On a computer**: click **What's New** in the sidebar footer, under your email address.
- **On a tablet**: tap **More** at the bottom of the rail, then tap **What's New** under your account details.
- **On a phone**: tap **More** in the tab bar, then tap the **What's New** tile under **Apps**, or the **What's New** link under your account details.
- **From the popup**: click **See all updates**.

See [Find your way around](/getting-started/find-your-way-around) for more on the sidebar, rail and tab bar.

<Note>
**Treasurers** and **Care Managers** see the popup when a new release comes out, but the full **What's New** page is outside the areas their roles can open. If they follow a **What's New** link or click **See all updates**, Bethel ChMS takes Treasurers to **Gifting & Donations** and Care Managers to **Care**.
</Note>

You need to be signed in to read release notes.

## What the popup shows

On a phone, the popup slides up from the bottom of the screen as a sheet. On a tablet or computer, it slides in as a panel from the right.

The popup includes:

- A **What's New** title, the release headline, a version badge such as `v0.12.0`, and a **Close** (X) button.
- The release items, grouped as **New**, **Improved** and **Fixed**.
- **See all updates** and **Got it** buttons at the bottom.

Longer release notes scroll inside the popup, while **See all updates** and **Got it** stay in view.

## When the popup appears

The popup appears only when there is a newer release note you have not seen yet.

Bethel ChMS does not show the popup when:

- You have already dismissed the latest release.
- The latest release was published before your account was created.
- You need to review updated terms first.
- No release notes have been published.

Only the latest release opens as a popup. If you miss several releases, Bethel ChMS shows the newest one rather than each missed release in turn. You can read the others on the **What's New** page.

<Info>
If updated terms are waiting for you, the terms take priority. Review and accept them first. **What's New** can then appear the next time you open the app, if a release note still applies to you.
</Info>

## Dismiss a release note

Any of these marks the release as seen:

- Clicking **Got it**.
- Clicking **See all updates**.
- Clicking the **Close** (X) button.
- Pressing **Escape**.
- Clicking or tapping outside the popup.

Bethel ChMS remembers the dismissal against your account, so the same release does not come back when you reload the page, switch browser or sign in on another device.

## Read all release notes

The **What's New** page is headed **What's New**, with the line "Everything we've shipped, newest first." Releases appear as a timeline, newest at the top.

Each release shows:

- Its version badge.
- The release headline.
- The date it was published.
- The **New**, **Improved** and **Fixed** groups that have items for that release.

If no releases have been published yet, the page shows **No releases yet. Check back soon.**

## If you do not see What's New

You may not see a popup every time Bethel ChMS changes. This usually means one of these is true:

- You already dismissed the latest release.
- Your account was created after the release was published.
- You need to review updated terms first.
- The change does not have a published release note yet.

You can always open the **What's New** page yourself: from the sidebar footer on a computer, or from **More** on a tablet or phone.
