**What's New** keeps you informed when Bethel ChMS ships updates. Each release comes with a short release note listing what is new, what has improved and what has been fixed, so you can catch up without leaving your work.

Release notes are informational only. Reading or dismissing them does not change any records, settings, permissions or billing.

## Latest release: v2.4.0

**Your church's logo and colour on wallet passes**

- **New**: wallet passes can now carry your church's own logo and colour. Org Owners and Org Admins upload, replace or remove the logo under **Wallet pass logo** on the Organisation page, and new Apple and Google passes show it. See [Wallet pass logo](/guides/organisation-settings#wallet-pass-logo).
- **Improved**: wallet passes are easier to read, with white text on your church's colour, and Apple and Google passes now share the same layout. See [Add the code to a phone wallet](/guides/member-qr-codes#add-the-code-to-a-phone-wallet).
- **Improved**: tighter security across sign-in, reports, household check-in and admin pages, so every action stays within your own church and branch.
- **Fixed**: a service that is still to come, under way or cancelled is no longer counted as an absence in **Member Frequency** and the Attendance Frequency report. See [Find members who have stopped attending](/guides/recording-attendance#find-members-who-have-stopped-attending).

## Earlier releases

**All-day check-in, and check-in codes in your phone's wallet**

- **All-day check-in**: check-in now stays open all day on the day of a service, at the kiosk, on check-in links and on the Dashboard. The **Check-in Window** setting is gone, and you don't need to change your events. The Dashboard keeps **Take attendance** and **QR & kiosk** on today's services until the day ends. See [How long check-in stays open](/guides/recording-attendance#how-long-check-in-stays-open) and [Dashboards](/guides/dashboards#upcoming-events).

- **Better QR code emails**: add a short message and a reply-to address, and emails are named after your church. You can see past sends and pick up a send that was interrupted. See [Email codes to many members](/guides/member-qr-codes#email-codes-to-many-members) and [Check what happened to a send](/guides/member-qr-codes#check-what-happened-to-a-send).
- **Add several people at once**: pick several people to add to a household or a ministry in one go. See [Households](/guides/households#add-members) and [Setting up ministries](/guides/setting-up-ministries#add-members).
- **Two-step verification**: everyone who uses Bethel ChMS now signs in with their password and a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on their phone. Porter admins enter a code before they use admin screens, and Porter workers are never asked. See [Two-step verification and devices](/guides/account-security).
- **Sign-in and security**: see the devices you're signed in on and sign out any you no longer use. Org Owners and Org Admins can reset someone's two-step verification or sign them out of all devices from **Users**. See [See and sign out your devices](/guides/account-security#see-and-sign-out-your-devices) and [User management](/guides/user-management#reset-two-step-verification-or-sign-someone-out).
- **Improved**: when a second service starts, the kiosk asks staff to switch to it, and a kiosk left open stays on the right service. If you keep a kiosk open all the time, reload it once after this update. See [Move the kiosk to the next service](/guides/kiosk-mode#move-the-kiosk-to-the-next-service).
- **Improved**: in Porter, rota calendars are named after your church and team, and workers can report or block someone in task comments, with Porter admins reviewing reports. See [Add the rota to a calendar](/guides/porter-rota#add-the-rota-to-a-calendar) and [Report a comment or a person](/guides/porter-projects#report-a-comment-or-a-person).
- **Improved**: if you use both Bethel ChMS and Porter, the app now opens on your Bethel ChMS starting page. See [Install the app](/getting-started/install-the-app#where-the-app-opens).
- **Fixed**: a QR code that failed to check in can be scanned again straight away, and the Members list shows members again for staff who look after one branch.
- **Fixed**: weekly care reminders no longer send several past weeks at once. See [Care](/guides/care).

### v2.2.0

**Service rotas and projects in Porter**

- **Service rota in Porter**: set up teams and positions, fill each service from a calendar, copy last week's rota or repeat it forward, and tell the team with one press. Workers see **My rota**, can say they can't make it, swap with a teammate, mark dates they're away and get reminders. Calendar links put the rota in their own calendar, and on the day **Not here yet** shows who hasn't checked in. See [Porter: service rota](/guides/porter-rota).
- **Projects and tasks in Porter**: plan a project, add tasks for a team or named people, and send notices and reminders. Workers claim, finish or hand back tasks from **My tasks**, and the planner follows progress on a board, with comments, overdue notices and daily round-ups. When a project closes, the team answers two short questions and you can get an AI summary with names removed. See [Porter: projects and tasks](/guides/porter-projects).
- **Notifications in Porter**: **Alerts** is now **Notifications**, and every worker has one, with a count of what's new. Type **@** in a task comment to tag someone, and they're told straight away. See [Porter notifications and return reminders](/guides/porter-alerts).
- **Improved**: Porter has a cleaner layout on phones, tablets and computers. **Home** opens on what's happening now, the task board uses the columns you set in **Setup**, you can add several tasks at once and keep unfinished ones as a draft, and **Setup** is split into clear sections. See [Porter](/guides/porter#find-your-way-around-porter).
- **Improved**: Rhema understands more questions. Filter by member status, gender, event or custom field, and ask for people who missed several services in a row. New members no longer show absences from before they joined. See [Rhema](/guides/rhema).
- **Improved**: the check-in kiosk now opens on the email and mobile search, and recurring events no longer need an end date. See [Kiosk mode](/guides/kiosk-mode) and [Managing events](/guides/managing-events).
- **Fixed**: attendance reports, dashboards and Rhema now include visitors in attendance totals.
- **Fixed**: a rescheduled or edited event keeps its check-ins and accepts check-in, and campaign reminders wait for the **Open date** and only go to people who were emailed.
- **Fixed**: lots of smaller fixes. Buttons are hidden from roles that can't use them, member merges are safer, imports read UK dates correctly, and many messages are clearer.

## Where to find updates

Bethel ChMS shows release notes in two places:

- The **What's New** popup, which can appear after you sign in or open the app.
- The **What's New** page, which lists every release note, newest first.

To open the **What's New** page:

- **On a computer**: click **What's New** at the bottom of the sidebar, above your email address. The label next to it shows the version you are using.
- **On a tablet**: tap **More** at the bottom of the rail, then tap **What's New** under your account details.
- **On a phone**: tap **More** in the tab bar, then tap the **What's New** tile under **Apps**, or the **What's New** link under your account details.
- **From the popup**: click **See all updates**.

See [Find your way around](/getting-started/find-your-way-around) for more on the sidebar, rail and tab bar.

<Note>
**Treasurers** and **Care Managers** see the popup when a new release comes out, but the full **What's New** page is outside the areas their roles can open. If they follow a **What's New** link or click **See all updates**, Bethel ChMS takes Treasurers to **Gifting & Donations** and Care Managers to **Care**.
</Note>

You need to be signed in to read release notes.

## What the popup shows

On a phone, the popup slides up from the bottom of the screen as a sheet. On a tablet or computer, it slides in as a panel from the right.

The popup includes:

- A **What's New** title, the release headline, a version badge such as `v2.0.0`, and a **Close** (X) button.
- The release items, grouped as **New**, **Improved** and **Fixed**.
- **See all updates** and **Got it** buttons at the bottom.

Longer release notes scroll inside the popup, while **See all updates** and **Got it** stay in view.

## When the popup appears

The popup appears only when there is a newer release note you have not seen yet.

Bethel ChMS does not show the popup when:

- You have already dismissed the latest release.
- The latest release was published before your account was created.
- You need to review updated terms first.
- No release notes have been published.

Only the latest release opens as a popup. If you miss several releases, Bethel ChMS shows the newest one rather than each missed release in turn. You can read the others on the **What's New** page.

<Info>
If updated terms are waiting for you, the terms take priority. Review and accept them first. **What's New** can then appear the next time you open the app, if a release note still applies to you.
</Info>

## Dismiss a release note

Any of these marks the release as seen:

- Clicking **Got it**.
- Clicking **See all updates**.
- Clicking the **Close** (X) button.
- Pressing **Escape**.
- Clicking or tapping outside the popup.

Bethel ChMS remembers the dismissal against your account, so the same release does not come back when you reload the page, switch browser or sign in on another device.

## Read all release notes

The **What's New** page is headed **What's New**, with the line "Everything we've shipped, newest first." Releases appear as a timeline, newest at the top.

Each release shows:

- Its version badge.
- The release headline.
- The date it was published.
- The **New**, **Improved** and **Fixed** groups that have items for that release.

If no releases have been published yet, the page shows **No releases yet. Check back soon.**

## If you do not see What's New

You may not see a popup every time Bethel ChMS changes. This usually means one of these is true:

- You already dismissed the latest release.
- Your account was created after the release was published.
- You need to review updated terms first.
- The change does not have a published release note yet.

You can always open the **What's New** page yourself: from the sidebar footer on a computer, or from **More** on a tablet or phone.
