Bethel ChMS records attendance against an **occurrence**, which is one date and time of an event. A weekly Sunday service is one event with a new occurrence every Sunday, so each service keeps its own attendance list.

Attendance can reach Bethel ChMS in several ways:

- **Staff check people in** from the occurrence's attendance page, by searching for them, scanning their QR code or entering a paper register in bulk. See [Take attendance as staff](#take-attendance-as-staff).
- **People check themselves in on their own phone** by scanning the event's QR code. See [Self check-in on a phone](#self-check-in-on-a-phone).
- **People check themselves in at a kiosk**, a tablet or laptop at your entrance. See [Kiosk mode](/guides/kiosk-mode) and [Find by name (Quick Find)](/guides/quick-find).
- **Service team workers check in with Porter**, the part of Bethel ChMS that workers and volunteers use on their phones. Their check-ins appear on the same attendance lists. See [Check in with Porter](/guides/porter-check-in).

<Info>
**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin, Editor. **Bulk Entry** is for Org Owner, Org Admin and Branch Admin only.
</Info>

Org Owners and Org Admins see one branch at a time. Choose the branch whose events you want to work with in the branch selector. See [Find your way around](/getting-started/find-your-way-around#choose-a-branch).

## Set up public check-in

**Public check-in** lets members and visitors check themselves in with the event's QR code, either on their own phone or at a kiosk. You switch it on for each event.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the event form">
    In the sidebar, open **Church life** and click **Events**. On a phone, tap **Events** in the tab bar. Click **Add Event** (the **+** button on a phone) to create an event, or open an existing event and click **Edit** (the pencil on a phone).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on public check-in">
    In the **Check-in** section, turn on **Public Check-in**. It is already on for new events.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the check-in window">
    Under **Check-in Window**, choose **30 minutes**, **1 hour**, **2 hours**, **3 hours**, **4 hours** or **8 hours**. New events start with **2 hours**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the event">
    Click **Create Event** for a new event, or **Save Changes** for an existing one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### How the check-in window works

The check-in window opens that length of time before each occurrence starts, and closes the same length of time after the start. With a **30 minutes** window and a service at 10:00, people can check themselves in from 09:30 until 10:30. If people often arrive late, or your service runs long, choose a longer window.

Bethel ChMS always uses the occurrence whose start time is closest to now. Cancelled occurrences are never offered for check-in.

Outside the window, the check-in page and the kiosk show "There's no active check-in for this event right now. Please check with the welcome team." Staff can still record attendance from the occurrence's attendance page at any time.

## Share the event QR code

Each event with public check-in has one QR code and one check-in link. People scan the code with their phone to check themselves in, kiosk mode runs on the same link, and Porter workers scan the same code to check in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the event's settings">
    Open **Events**, select the event and click the **Settings** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Public Check-in QR card">
    The **Public Check-in QR** card shows the QR code, the **Check-in URL** with a **Copy** button, and the buttons **Copy Link**, **Launch Kiosk Mode**, **Print QR** and **Regenerate Token**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share or print the code">
    Click **Copy Link** to copy the check-in link for a message or slide, or click **Print QR** to make a poster.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If public check-in is off, the card reads "Public check-in is disabled for this event. Enable it in the event settings to generate a QR code." Turn on **Public Check-in** first.

**Launch Kiosk Mode** opens the check-in page as a kiosk in a new browser tab. See [Kiosk mode](/guides/kiosk-mode).

<Note>
Porter workers scan this same QR code to check in, so keep **Public Check-in** on for events your service team checks in to. See [Check in with Porter](/guides/porter-check-in).
</Note>

### Print a QR poster

1. On the **Public Check-in QR** card, click **Print QR**.
2. In **Print QR Code**, check the **Live preview**. The **Branch**, **Event** and **Service time** are filled in for you from the event.
3. Under **Customise**, edit the **Welcome line** and **Instructions** if you want different wording.
4. Choose a **Page size**: **A4** or **A5**.
5. Click **Download PDF**, then print the file.

### Replace the QR code

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin, Editor.

Replace the code only if it has been shared somewhere it shouldn't be, or you want old printed copies to stop working.

1. On the **Public Check-in QR** card, click **Regenerate Token**.
2. In **Regenerate Check-in Token**, click **Regenerate**.

Bethel ChMS creates a new QR code and check-in link and shows "Check-in token regenerated".

<Warning>
Regenerating the token stops every printed or shared copy of the old code working straight away. People who open the old link see "Event not found". Porter workers who scan the old code are told it isn't today's check-in code. Print and display the new code before your next service.
</Warning>

## Self check-in on a phone

With public check-in on, people can check themselves in on their own phone without installing anything. They scan the event QR code with their phone's camera, or open the check-in link you shared.

1. They type their **Email or mobile number** and tap **Continue**.
2. Bethel ChMS shows "Welcome back" with their first name and surname initial. They tap **Check In**, or **Back** to start again.
3. A confirmation screen shows "Checked in" and their name. It stays on screen until they tap **Done**, so they can show it at the door.

If they are already checked in for this service, they see "Already checked in" instead.

### Household check-in

If the person belongs to a household, Bethel ChMS asks who else from their household is here. Everyone is ticked to begin with, and anyone already checked in is marked "Already checked in".

- **Check in selected** checks in the person and everyone ticked. The button shows how many people that is.
- **Just me** checks in only the person.
- **Back** returns to the start.

If everyone else in the household is already in, the screen says so and offers **Continue**. Learn more about households in [Households](/guides/households).

### First-time visitors

If Bethel ChMS can't find the email address or mobile number, the screen shows **First time here?** with the details they typed. They can tap **Register & Check In**, or **Try a different email or number**. Visitors can also tap **First time here?** on the first screen.

The registration form asks for:

- **First Name** and **Last Name** (required)
- **Email** or **Mobile Number** (at least one is required)
- **Gender** (optional): **Male**, **Female**, **Other** or **Prefer not to say**
- Any visitor custom fields your organisation has set up

They tap **Register & Check In** to create their visitor record and check in at the same time. If the email or mobile number is already on file, they're asked to check in instead.

Visitors who register with an email address are emailed straight away with a welcome message and their personal check-in QR code. They can scan that code at a kiosk next time. See [Visitors](/guides/visitors) for what happens to visitor records afterwards.

<Tip>
The phone check-in page doesn't scan QR codes or search by name. Those options are only on a [kiosk](/guides/kiosk-mode). The page reminds people that anyone on the welcome team can check them in at the door.
</Tip>

### Messages people might see

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| "Check-in is not available for this event." | **Public Check-in** is off for the event. |
| "There's no active check-in for this event right now. Please check with the welcome team." | No occurrence is inside the check-in window, or the occurrence was cancelled. |
| "Event not found" | The link is wrong, or the QR code was replaced with **Regenerate Token**. |

## Take attendance as staff

Each occurrence has its own attendance page, where staff check people in, correct mistakes and see who is missing.

### Open the attendance page

Open **Events** and select the event, then use any of these:

- Click **Take attendance** on the card for the next occurrence at the top of the event page.
- On the **Occurrences** tab, click the occurrence's row, or open its **⋯** menu and choose **Take attendance**.
- On the **Attendance** tab, click an occurrence that has already started.

You can also click **Take attendance** on a **Live now** card on the **Dashboard** or the **Events** page, or choose **Take attendance** from an event's **⋯** menu in the events list.

The page shows the event name and date, a green **Live** label while the occurrence is in progress, and how many people have checked in, split into members and visitors, with the time of the last check-in. Bethel ChMS keeps your screen awake while this page is open.

For an event linked to a group, a banner reminds you that the absent list shows group members only.

### Check people in with Record Attendance

1. Click **Record Attendance**. On a phone, the button sits in a bar at the bottom of the screen.
2. Choose **Member** or **Visitor**.
3. Search by name, email or mobile.
4. Click **Check in** next to the person.

Bethel ChMS records the check-in straight away and shows a "Checked in" card with the time, the person's name and **Undo**. The sheet stays open, so you can search for the next person. When you're finished, click **Done**. The footer shows how many people you have checked in this session.

Anyone who is already checked in shows an "In" label with the time they arrived, instead of a **Check in** button.

#### Check in the rest of a household

When you check in a member who belongs to a household, Bethel ChMS asks whether to check in the rest of the household too. Household members who aren't in yet are ticked. Anyone already checked in is shown ticked and marked "Already checked in".

- Click **Check in selected** to check in everyone ticked.
- Click **Not now** to carry on without them.

### Add a first-time visitor while taking attendance

If a visitor isn't on the list, you can create their record and check them in at once.

1. In **Record Attendance**, scroll to **Not on the list?** and click **Add visitor**.
2. Enter their **First Name** and **Last Name**. **Mobile Number** and **Email** are optional.
3. Click **Add and check in**.

The "Checked in" card shows that this was their first visit. If you entered an email address, Bethel ChMS emails the visitor their personal check-in QR code. You can add more details later in [Visitors](/guides/visitors).

### Scan QR codes

Use **Scan QR** when members bring their personal QR code, for example in the email Bethel ChMS sent them. See [Give members their QR code](#give-members-their-qr-code).

1. Click **Scan QR**. The scanner opens full screen.
2. Allow camera access if your browser asks.
3. Point the camera at the QR code.

The scanner uses the rear camera. Use the flip button to switch cameras, and the torch button in a dark room (on devices that have one). The camera stays on between scans, so you can scan one person after another.

- **A member's code** checks the member in straight away. The result card shows "Checked in", their name and **Undo**. If they're already in, you see "Already checked in" and the time they arrived.
- **A code Bethel ChMS doesn't recognise** shows "Not recognised". Codes from another branch don't match.

The **Scanned** list, under the camera or beside it on a wide screen, shows everyone you have scanned this session, newest first. When you're finished, click **Done**.

### Check in from the Absent tab

The attendance page has two tabs: **Attendees** and **Absent**. Each shows a count.

**Absent** lists active members who haven't checked in yet, and updates after each check-in. Click **Check in** next to a member to record them, or use the row menu to **Call**, **Message** or **Email** them, or **Open member**. After a check-in, Bethel ChMS shows a message with **Undo**.

**Attendees** lists everyone who has checked in, newest first. The 20 most recent show first. To see the rest, click the button under the list, for example **Show 12 earlier check-ins**.

### Undo a check-in

**Undo** appears on the "Checked in" card in **Record Attendance**, on the result card in **Scan QR**, and in the message after you check someone in from the **Absent** tab.

Undo removes only the check-ins you have just made. Anyone who checked in another way in the meantime, for example at the kiosk or with another steward, keeps their check-in, even if they were part of the same household.

### Enter attendance in bulk

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin.

Bulk entry works well when you type up a paper register after the event.

1. On the attendance page, click the **⋯** menu at the top and choose **Bulk Entry**.
2. In **Bulk Attendance Entry**, search by name if needed.
3. Tick members, or click **Select All**.
4. Click the **Check In** button. It shows how many members you selected, for example **Check In 12 Members**.

The list shows every member of the branch. Members who are already checked in can't be ticked and are marked "Already checked in". When you finish, Bethel ChMS tells you how many members were checked in and how many were skipped because they were already recorded.

### Correct or remove a record

On the **Attendees** tab, open the row menu for the person. On a phone, tap the row.

- **Edit time** opens **Edit Attendance Time**. Change the **Date** or **Time** and click **Save Changes**.
- **Remove** opens **Remove Attendance Record**. Click **Remove** to take the person off this attendance list. You can't undo this.
- **Open member** opens the member's page. It isn't shown for visitors.

### How each check-in is labelled

The **Capture Method** column shows how each person was checked in.

| Capture method | How the person was checked in |
| --- | --- |
| **Manual** | A staff member used **Record Attendance** or the **Absent** tab. |
| **QR Code** | A staff member scanned the person's QR code with **Scan QR**. |
| **Admin Entry** | A staff member used **Bulk Entry**, or ticked household members in **Record Attendance**. |
| **Self Check-in**, **Kiosk**, **Kiosk QR** | The person checked themselves in, on their own phone or at a [kiosk](/guides/kiosk-mode). **Kiosk QR** means they scanned a QR code at a kiosk. |
| **Porter** | A worker checked in with [Porter](/guides/porter-check-in), or a Porter admin checked them in. |

If someone has already been checked in another way, a Porter check-in for the same occurrence keeps the existing record.

## Give members their QR code

Each member has a personal QR code that staff can scan with **Scan QR** and members can scan at a kiosk.

To show a member's code, open their member page and use any of these:

- On a tablet or computer, click **QR Code** in the top bar.
- Tap **QR code** in the quick actions under the member's name.
- Open the **More actions** menu (⋮) in the top bar and choose **Show QR code**.

The code appears with the note "Scan this code to record attendance". Click **Full screen** to make it larger.

Org Owners and Org Admins can also click **Email code** to send the member their code.

### Email QR codes to many members

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin.

1. In the sidebar, open **People** and click **Members**.
2. Open the **⋯** menu and choose **Email QR codes**. To email particular people, tick them on the list first and use **Email QR codes** in the selection bar (it reads **QR** on a phone).
3. Choose **Selected members**, **All members** or **Filter**. With **Filter**, narrow the recipients by status, age group or group.
4. Click **Continue**.
5. Review the preview and click **Send now**.

The preview separates members who will receive an email from members who are skipped because they have no email address, or because a code was emailed to them in the last hour. You can download the skipped lists as a CSV file to follow up.

## Review attendance records

The **Attendance** page brings together attendance from every event in your branch.

In the sidebar, open **Church life** and click **Attendance**. On a tablet, use the **Attendance** shortcut in the rail. On a phone, tap **More**, then **Attendance**.

The page has two tabs, **Records** and **Member Frequency**, and starts with the last 30 days.

### Filter the list

- The date range button shows the dates you're looking at, for example **Last 30 days**. Click it to choose a preset. Under **Range**: **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**, **This month** and **Year to date**. Under **Single day**: **Today**, **Yesterday** and **Last Sunday**. On a tablet or computer you can also pick dates on the calendar. On a phone, enter **From** and **To** dates instead. Click **Apply**.
- **Name…** searches by name.
- On the **Records** tab, the chips under the toolbar filter by event. Choose **All events** or a single event.
- **Filters** opens a sheet with the same **Event** choice and the sort order.

Your filters are saved in the page link, so you can bookmark the page or share the link with a colleague.

### Records

The **Records** tab lists each check-in with **Attendee**, **Event**, **Date**, **Type** and **Capture Method**. Visitors have a **Visitor** label.

Sort by **Attendance Date**, **Event**, **Attendee** or **Capture Method** in **Filters**. On a tablet or computer, you can also click a column heading. The list shows 50 records a page.

Click **Export CSV** to download the records that match your current filters as a spreadsheet file.

You can also see one member's attendance on their member page, on the **Attendance History** tab.

## Find members who have stopped attending

The **Member Frequency** tab shows how often each member attended in the date range. Use it to spot people who have stopped coming and update their records in bulk.

The columns are **Member Name**, **Events Attended**, **Events Absent**, **Missed in a row**, **Attendance Rate** and **Last Attended**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Member Frequency">
    On the **Attendance** page, click **Member Frequency**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a date range and event">
    Choose a date range. To measure attendance against one event, choose it in **Filters**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by missed services">
    Use the chips **Any streak**, **Missed 2+ in a row**, **3+**, **4+** or **6+**. For a different number, enter it in **Missed at least X in a row** in **Filters**. This counts the member's most recent absences in a row, up to the end of the date range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check a member's history">
    Click a member to see their 7 most recent occurrences, each marked **Attended** or **Absent**. Click **Show all occurrences** for the full list, or **Open member** to go to their member page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select members">
    Tick the members you want to update. You can select up to 100 at a time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply a change">
    In the selection bar, click **Set status…** or **Set custom field…**. On a phone, these read **Status…** and **Custom field…**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

- **Set status** changes the selected members to the **Target status** you choose: **Newcomer**, **Active** or **Inactive**. Enter a **Reason** (required, up to 500 characters) and click **Apply**. Bethel ChMS tells you how many members were updated and how many already had that status.
- **Set custom field** sets a **Custom field** to the value you enter, or removes the field from the selected members if you tick **Clear value**. Enter a **Reason** and click **Apply**.

Branch Admins and Editors can change only members in their own branch.

If you change the filters while members are selected, Bethel ChMS asks **Clear your selection?** Choose **Keep selection** or **Clear and apply filter**.

<Tip>
Member Frequency is for reviewing and updating records in one go. For ongoing pastoral follow-up of members who stop attending, with absence rules, assignments, statuses, due dates and notes, use **Member care** in [Care](/guides/care).
</Tip>

## Visitors in attendance

Visitors appear in attendance lists and records alongside members, with a **Visitor** label. Each check-in adds to their visit history. To see the visitor list, follow up with visitors or convert a visitor to a member, see [Visitors](/guides/visitors).
