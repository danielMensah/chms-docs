**Change History** shows who changed a record, when they changed it and what they changed. It appears as a tab on the detail page of your key records, so you can answer questions like "who changed this member's status?" or "when was this event's time moved?" without asking around.

Bethel ChMS records the history automatically as people work. There is nothing to switch on and nothing extra to fill in.

<Info>
**Who can do this:** Anyone who can open a record can see its **Change History** tab. Org Owners and Org Admins can open records across the organisation. Other roles open records in their own branch, because each branch owns its data. See [Roles and permissions](/guides/roles-and-permissions).
</Info>

## Where you'll find it

The **Change History** tab is on the detail page of these records:

- **Members** (see [Managing members](/guides/managing-members))
- **Ministries** (see [Setting up ministries](/guides/setting-up-ministries))
- **Events** (see [Managing events](/guides/managing-events))
- **Households** (see [Households](/guides/households))
- **Groups**, or whatever your branch calls them, such as cells (see [Groups](/guides/groups))
- **Locations** (see [Locations](/guides/locations))
- **Users**, the staff accounts that sign in to Bethel ChMS (see [User management](/guides/user-management))

On households and locations, the tab label also shows how many entries the history has.

Visitor records don't have change history.

## Open change history

1. Open the record you want to check, for example a member from the **Members** list.
2. Click the **Change History** tab.

The timeline lists the newest changes first.

## Read the timeline

Each entry starts with one line saying what happened, who did it and when, for example "Updated by Sarah Jones on 6 April 2026 at 09:15". A coloured dot beside the entry shows the kind of change:

| Dot | Action | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Green | **Created** | The record was added. |
| Amber | **Updated** | One or more details were changed. |
| Red | **Deleted** | The record was deleted. |
| Blue | **Restored** | A deleted record was brought back. |

The name in the entry is the person who made the change. You may also see:

- **System**, when Bethel ChMS made the change itself rather than a signed-in person.
- **Unknown**, when Bethel ChMS can no longer find the person who made the change.

For updates, each changed field is listed underneath, with the field name, the old value crossed out, an arrow, and the new value. For example, **Last name** ~~Smith~~ → Jones.

If nothing has been recorded for a record yet, the tab reads "No changes recorded yet."

## What each record tracks

The timeline lists changes to the main details of each record. Behind-the-scenes information, such as internal reference numbers and timestamps, is left out so the history stays easy to read.

| Record | Fields listed in updates |
| --- | --- |
| **Members** | First name, Last name, Date of birth, Gender, Email, Mobile number, Address, Post code, Status |
| **Ministries** | Name, Description, Status |
| **Events** | Name, Description, Location, Start date, End date, Recurring, Recurrence pattern, Recurrence end date, Group |
| **Households** | Name |
| **Groups** | Name, Description, Active |
| **Locations** | Name, Address, Post code, Capacity, Primary location |
| **Users** | Name, Role, Scope, Branch |

Changes to other details, such as a member's nationality, tags or custom fields, aren't listed. If an update changed only details like these, its entry shows who made the change and when, with no fields underneath.

Each history covers the record's own details. For example, an event's history covers the event itself, not the attendance taken at it.

## Good to know

- No one can edit or delete entries in the timeline, including Org Owners. That is what makes it a trustworthy record for audits.
- The history covers changes made since Bethel ChMS started recording it. Edits made before then don't appear.
- Gifting entries and Activity Log entries have their own **Audit History** section on the entry's page instead of a **Change History** tab. It describes each change in a sentence, such as "changed amount from £20.00 to £25.00". See [Gifting and donations](/guides/gifting-and-donations) and [Activity log](/guides/activity-log).
