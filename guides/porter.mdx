**Porter** is the part of Bethel ChMS that your church's workers and volunteers use on their phones. Stewards, ushers, the media team and anyone else on a service rota use it to:

- **Check in** when they arrive, by scanning the event's check-in QR code. The check-in only counts when the phone is on the premises, and it shows up as attendance in Bethel ChMS. See [Check in with Porter](/guides/porter-check-in).
- **Loan and return equipment**, such as microphones, cables and cameras, by scanning the QR labels you stick on them. See [Equipment, loans and faults](/guides/porter-equipment).
- **Report faults** the moment they find them, with photos. See [Equipment, loans and faults](/guides/porter-equipment#report-a-fault).
- **See when they're serving** on the service rota, say when they can't make it and swap with a teammate. See [Porter: service rota](/guides/porter-rota).
- **Pick up and finish tasks** for a project, such as setting up for the Carol Service. See [Porter: projects and tasks](/guides/porter-projects).
- **Hear what matters to them** in the **Notifications** tab, and on their phone. See [Porter notifications and return reminders](/guides/porter-alerts).

This page has two parts:

- **For church admins:** [switching Porter on](#get-porter-switched-on), [giving people access](#give-someone-porter-access) and [setting it up](#set-up-porter-for-your-church).
- **For workers:** [installing the app, signing in and finding your way around](#for-workers-get-started-with-porter).

## Porter access levels

Porter has its own two access levels, separate from the roles people have in Bethel ChMS. The **Users** page shows them as:

- **Porter · Worker**: checks in, loans and returns equipment, and reports faults.
- **Porter · Admin**: does everything a worker does, and also sets Porter up and looks after the equipment.

On these pages, a **worker** is anyone with Porter · Worker, and a **Porter admin** is anyone with Porter · Admin.

| What they can do in Porter | Porter · Worker | Porter · Admin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Check in on arrival | Yes | Yes |
| Loan and return equipment (after checking in) | Yes | Yes |
| Report a fault, at any time | Yes | Yes |
| Browse the equipment list and open an item | Yes | Yes |
| See their own check-in and equipment history | Yes | Yes |
| See their own rota and tasks, and their **Notifications** | Yes | Yes |
| Set each location's check-in area, in **Setup** | No | Yes |
| Check workers in by hand | No | Yes |
| See everyone's history, including **Rejected attempts** | No | Yes |
| Add and edit equipment, print labels, export the equipment list | No | Yes |
| Manage equipment tags | No | Yes |
| Mark items missing or found, resolve faults, retire items, delete sets | No | Yes |
| See open faults and missing items in **Notifications**, and set return reminders | No | Yes |
| Plan projects and fill the service rota for every team | No | Yes |

Team leaders and assistant leaders with Porter access can also fill the rota and plan projects for their own teams. See [Porter: service rota](/guides/porter-rota#who-can-do-what) and [Porter: projects and tasks](/guides/porter-projects#who-can-do-what).

A Porter access level sits on the person's **member record**, not on a Bethel ChMS role. This means anyone can have it: a volunteer who never uses Bethel ChMS, or a staff member with any Bethel ChMS role. Someone with Porter only can't open Bethel ChMS. Someone with both uses one email and password for both. See [Porter access levels](/guides/roles-and-permissions#porter-access-levels).

### Porter covers your whole organisation

Your **organisation** is your church network, with all its branches, members and settings. In Bethel ChMS, each branch owns its own records. Porter works differently: it covers the whole organisation, so one team of Porter admins can look after check-in and equipment for every site.

- **Setup** lists every location in your organisation, grouped by branch.
- **Manual check-in** lists every worker in your organisation.
- The equipment list, faults and missing items, and return reminders cover all branches.

A Porter admin therefore works across every branch, whatever their Bethel ChMS role. For example, a Branch Admin who is also a Porter admin can set up check-in and manage equipment for all branches in Porter.

## Get Porter switched on

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin

Porter is off for every church until it's switched on. It isn't tied to your plan: the Bethel ChMS team switches it on for your church on request. To ask for it, email [hello@bethelware.app](mailto:hello@bethelware.app).

While Porter is off:

- The **Users** page has no Porter options.
- The Bethel ChMS sidebar has no **Porter** link.
- Anyone who opens Porter sees **Porter isn't switched on for your church**, with a message asking them to get their church admin to contact the Bethel ChMS team.

If Porter is switched off again later, everything is kept, including your equipment, check-ins and who has which access level.

## Give someone Porter access

**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, once Porter is switched on for your church

You give Porter access in Bethel ChMS, on the **Users** page. In the sidebar, open **Organisation** and click **Users**. Branch Admins can see who has Porter on the **Users** page, but can't change it.

<Info>
Every Porter user must be linked to a **member record**, because their check-ins and equipment loans go on it. If the person isn't in Bethel ChMS as a member yet, [add them as a member](/guides/managing-members) first.
</Info>

### Someone new to Bethel ChMS

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add user">
    On the **Users** page, click **Add user**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their email">
    Enter the **Email** they'll sign in with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the app">
    Under **Apps**, choose **Porter** for someone who only needs Porter, or **Both apps** if they also need Bethel ChMS. For **Both apps**, also fill in their Bethel ChMS **Name** and **Role**, and their **Branch** if the role needs one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose their Porter role">
    Under **Porter role**, choose **Worker** or **Admin**. **Worker** is selected to begin with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link their member record">
    Under **Link to member record**, click **Search members…** and type a name or email. Each result shows the member's branch and email address, and **has sign-in** if they already have a sign-in. Pick the right person.

    If the member record has a different email address from the one you entered, a warning tells you that saving replaces it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Click **Create user**. The person gets an invitation email from Bethel ChMS.

    If the member already has a sign-in (**has sign-in**), enter the email they already sign in with and choose **Porter**. Porter is linked to their existing sign-in as soon as you click **Create user**, and they keep their password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For a picture of the **Add user** sheet and its **Apps** choices, see [Send an invitation](/getting-started/invite-users#send-an-invitation).

The invitation email has an **Accept Invitation** button. The person opens it and sets a password. Someone invited to Porter only goes straight into Porter. Someone invited to **Both apps** starts in Bethel ChMS. Their check-ins and loans then go on the member record you linked.

Invitation links expire. Until the person accepts, they appear under the **Invites** chip on the **Users** page as **Invite pending**, or **Invite expired** once the link has lapsed. Click **Resend** to send a fresh link. For a **Porter**-only invitation, you can also open the **⋯** menu and choose **Cancel invite** to stop the link working. See [Manage invitations](/guides/user-management#manage-invitations).

### Someone who already uses Bethel ChMS

Give them Porter from **Edit user** rather than adding them again:

1. On the **Users** page, click **⋯** on their row and choose **Edit**.
2. Under **Apps**, tick **Porter**.
3. Choose a **Porter role** and use **Link to member record** to pick their member record. If their sign-in is already linked to a member record, it's shown and can't be changed.
4. Click **Save changes**.

Because they already have a sign-in, Porter works for them as soon as you save. No invitation is sent.

<Tip>
**Setting Porter up yourself?** Give yourself **Porter · Admin** this way, from **Edit** on your own row. Setup, equipment, the rota and projects are all done inside Porter, so you need Porter admin access to reach them.
</Tip>

### Change or remove someone's Porter access

| To | For a Bethel ChMS user | For someone with Porter only |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Change their access level** | Open **Edit**, choose the other **Porter role** and click **Save changes**. | Click **Add user**, enter the email they sign in with, choose **Porter**, pick the new **Porter role** and link the same member record, then click **Create user**. Because they already sign in, the change applies straight away. |
| **Remove Porter** | Open **Edit**, untick **Porter** and click **Save changes**. | Click **⋯** on their row and choose **Remove Porter**, then confirm with **Remove Porter**. |

Removing Porter keeps the person's member record, past check-ins and sign-in, so you can give Porter back later. Deactivating a Bethel ChMS user also stops them using Porter, because they share one sign-in.

On the **Users** page, people with Porter only appear after your Bethel ChMS users. Their role shows as **No ChMS role** with **Porter only** underneath. For more, see [Give or remove Porter access](/guides/user-management#give-or-remove-porter-access).

## Set up Porter for your church

Once Porter is switched on, work through these steps. Most of them are done inside Porter by a Porter admin.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give yourself Porter admin access">
    Give yourself, and anyone else who will run Porter, **Porter · Admin**. See [Someone who already uses Bethel ChMS](#someone-who-already-uses-bethel-chms).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your locations and events in Bethel ChMS">
    Porter uses the [locations](/guides/locations) and [events](/guides/managing-events) you already have in Bethel ChMS. Make sure **Public Check-in** is on for every event your workers check in to, because they scan the event's check-in QR code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set each location's check-in area">
    In Porter, open **Setup**, then **Locations** under **Check-in**, and set where check-in works for each location. See [Set a check-in area](/guides/porter-check-in#set-a-check-in-area).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register your equipment and print labels">
    Add your equipment in Porter's **Equipment** tab, print the QR labels and stick them on. See [Equipment, loans and faults](/guides/porter-equipment).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on notifications and set return reminders">
    Turn on notifications on your phone, and choose when workers are reminded to return equipment. See [Porter notifications and return reminders](/guides/porter-alerts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your rota teams">
    If your teams serve on a rota, add each team's positions and how many people each service needs. See [Porter: service rota](/guides/porter-rota).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give your workers access">
    Add each worker with **Porter · Worker**. See [Give someone Porter access](#give-someone-porter-access).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## For workers: get started with Porter

### Install the app

Porter is part of the Bethel ChMS app, so there's nothing to download from an app store. On your phone, open [app.bethelchms.com/porter/login](https://app.bethelchms.com/porter/login) and sign in. On browsers that can install apps, a bar at the top of Porter then invites you to **Add Porter to your home screen**. If you only use Porter, the installed app opens straight into Porter while you're signed in.

On iPhone and iPad, install the app before you turn on notifications, because notifications only work when Porter is opened from the Home Screen. For step-by-step help, see [Install the app](/getting-started/install-the-app).

### Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Porter sign-in page">
    Go to [app.bethelchms.com/porter/login](https://app.bethelchms.com/porter/login). The page is headed **Porter**, with the line **Check-in and equipment for church workers**. If you open the installed app while signed out, it shows the main Bethel ChMS sign-in page instead, which works just as well (see below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Enter your **Email** and **Password**. Tap the eye icon to show or hide your password. Let the security check finish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Tap **Sign in**. Porter opens on **Home**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you already use Bethel ChMS, sign in with the same email and password. One account works for both. You can also sign in on the main Bethel ChMS sign-in page: if you only have Porter, it takes you straight to Porter. See [Sign in](/getting-started/sign-in).

If the page says **This account doesn't have Porter access. Ask an admin at your church.**, ask a church admin to [give you Porter access](#give-someone-porter-access).

**Forgotten your password?** Reset it from the main Bethel ChMS sign-in page, which takes you through choosing a new password. See [Reset a forgotten password](/getting-started/sign-in#reset-a-forgotten-password). Then sign in with your new password.

### Find your way around Porter

Porter is the same app on every device. Only the way around it changes with the size of the screen.

- **On a phone**, the tabs run along the bottom of the screen. The bar at the top shows the page's name, a way back, **Guide** (a book icon), which opens the guide for that screen, and your initials, which open a menu with **Sign out**. Porter admins also get a gear there for **Setup**.
- **On a tablet held upright**, a narrow rail of icons runs down the left, with **Setup** among them for Porter admins, and your initials and **Sign out** at the foot.
- **On a tablet on its side or a computer**, the rail is wider and names each tab. At its foot are your name, **Back to ChMS** (if you also use Bethel ChMS) and **Sign out**. The bar along the top shows where you are, for example *Projects › Carol Service*, with the page's buttons on the right, **Guide**, and **?** for the [keyboard shortcuts](/guides/porter-projects#keyboard-shortcuts).

| Tab | Who sees it | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Home** | Everyone | What you need to do now: check in, your tasks and rota dates, and what's on loan to you. |
| **Rota** | Everyone | When you're serving, and for team leaders and Porter admins, filling the rota. See [Porter: service rota](/guides/porter-rota). |
| **Tasks** | Everyone | Your tasks under **My tasks**, and projects for those who plan them. See [Porter: projects and tasks](/guides/porter-projects). |
| **Equipment** | Everyone | Loan and return equipment, and browse the equipment list. See [Equipment, loans and faults](/guides/porter-equipment). |
| **Notifications** | Everyone | Everything Porter has sent you. Porter admins also see open faults and missing items. A number shows what's new. See [The Notifications tab](/guides/porter-alerts#the-notifications-tab). |
| **Setup** | Porter admins | Check-in areas, manual check-in, equipment tags, return reminders, rota reminders and task settings. A gear at the top on a phone. |

Your check-in and equipment history is on **Home**, under **Check-in and loan history**. If a worker opens a screen meant for Porter admins, Porter tells them it's only for Porter admins.

#### Home

**Home** shows today's date and greets you by name. It opens on a card with the one thing you need to do now:

- **On a day you're serving**, *You're serving at Sunday Service*, with your position, the time and place, and a **Check in** button.
- **Once you've checked in**, the event, the time and the location, with **My rota** if you're on the rota. It says **By an admin** if a Porter admin checked you in by hand.
- **When a team task is due and nobody has it**, the task with a **Claim** button.
- **Otherwise**, **You're not checked in**, with a **Check in** button and *Can't scan? Ask the steward to check you in*.

Under the card are:

- **Needs you**: tasks due or overdue, team tasks to claim, and swap requests from teammates.
- **Coming up**: your next tasks and serving dates.
- **With you**: the equipment on loan to you, with when you took it, each with a **Return** button. Return everything before you leave.
- **Check-in and loan history**, and **Report a fault**, for anything broken or not working.
- **Turn on notifications**, for Porter admins, and for workers while they have equipment on loan, a slot on the rota or a task. See [Porter notifications and return reminders](/guides/porter-alerts).

Porter admins also see **Notifications** on **Home** when there are open faults or missing items, and links to **Manual check-in** and **Setup**. Team leaders and Porter admins see **Not here yet** around service time. See [On the day](/guides/porter-rota#on-the-day).

## Move between Bethel ChMS and Porter

If you use both, you don't need to sign in twice.

- **To open Porter:** on a computer, click **Porter** at the bottom of the Bethel ChMS sidebar. It has a small **App** label. On a tablet or phone, tap **More** and find **Porter** under **Apps**.
- **To go back:** tap **Back to ChMS**. On a tablet on its side or a computer, it's at the foot of the rail down the left. Otherwise, Porter admins find it in **Setup** and workers find it on **Home**. It opens your Bethel ChMS **Dashboard**.

The **Porter** link only appears when Porter is switched on for your church and your own member record has a Porter access level. Treasurers and Care Managers don't see it. **Back to ChMS** only appears for people who also have a Bethel ChMS account. See [Find your way around](/getting-started/find-your-way-around#porter).

## If Porter won't open

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="You don't have access to Porter">
    You're signed in, but your account has no Porter access. The screen explains that the account can sign in to Bethel ChMS only, and asks you to get an admin at your church to give you Porter access. Tap **Sign in with another account** to sign out and return to the Porter sign-in page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Porter isn't switched on for your church">
    Porter hasn't been switched on for your organisation yet. Ask your church admin to contact the Bethel ChMS team. See [Get Porter switched on](#get-porter-switched-on).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Service paused">
    Your church's Bethel ChMS account is on hold for billing, so Porter isn't available for now. The page reads **This service is temporarily paused** and asks you to contact a church administrator. No action is needed from you, and everything comes back once billing is sorted. Your Org Owner can find out more in [Billing and subscription](/guides/billing-and-subscription#if-your-account-is-paused).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No connection">
    When your phone loses its connection, a red **No connection** bar appears at the top of Porter. Scans aren't recorded until you're back online, so reconnect to mobile data or Wi-Fi and try again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Privacy

- **Location.** Porter uses your phone's location only to check that you're on site when you check in. Your phone shares its position once for each scan. Porter keeps only how far you were from the location and how accurate your phone's position was, never where you actually were.
- **Photos.** Equipment and fault photos are stored privately. Only people signed in to your church's Porter can see them.
- **Notifications.** Turning on notifications on a device lets Porter send notifications to it. Turn them off at any time. See [Porter notifications and return reminders](/guides/porter-alerts).
