Bethel ChMS has two dashboards:

- **Dashboard** shows one branch at a time: what's happening today, who has checked in, and who your members are.
- **Organisation Dashboard** shows trends across every branch in your organisation, so you can compare branches.

A **branch** is an individual church or campus, and your **organisation** is the whole church network that contains them.

## The branch Dashboard

<Info>
**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin, Editor. Treasurers and Care Managers don't have a Dashboard. They go straight to their own pages instead.
</Info>

**Dashboard** is the first item in the sidebar. On a phone, it's the first tab in the tab bar at the bottom of the screen.

### Which branch you're looking at

The Dashboard always shows one branch.

- **Branch Admins and Editors** always see their own branch.
- **Org Owners and Org Admins** choose a branch with the branch selector. On a computer, it's at the top of the sidebar, under your organisation name. On a tablet or phone, tap the branch chip in the top bar. The first time you open Bethel ChMS, the first branch in alphabetical order is selected.

See [Choose a branch](/getting-started/find-your-way-around#choose-a-branch) for more about the branch selector.

### What's on the Dashboard

The Dashboard has five sections.

#### Upcoming events

The **Upcoming events** card gives you quick access to the QR code and kiosk for your events. Click **View all** to open the **Events** page.

Events that are happening right now appear at the top as green **Live now** cards, up to three at a time. Each card shows the event name, start time and location, and how many people have checked in so far. It has two buttons:

- **Take attendance** opens the attendance list for this occurrence, so you can mark people present. See [Recording attendance](/guides/recording-attendance).
- **QR & kiosk** opens kiosk mode for the event in a new tab, ready for people to check themselves in at the door. If public check-in is switched off for the event, it opens the event page instead. See [Kiosk mode](/guides/kiosk-mode).

Below the live cards is a list of events that haven't started yet, with each event's name, time and location, and when it starts (**Today**, or for example **In 3d** for three days' time). Click an event to open it. Click **Load more** to see more. If nothing is planned, the card reads **No upcoming events**.

#### Member status

The member status strip shows how many member records the branch has and splits them into **Active**, **Newcomer** and **Inactive**, with a count and percentage for each. Click a status to open the Member Directory report for that status. See [Reports](/guides/reports).

#### Latest check-ins

The **Latest check-ins** card lists everyone who has checked in at this branch in the last 7 days, newest first. Under the title you'll see the total, for example **48 in the last 7 days**.

- Each row shows the person's name (visitors are marked **Visitor**), the event and location, when they checked in, and how they checked in.
- Use **Search by name…** to find someone.
- Click a person to see their details.
- Click **Load more** to see older check-ins from the last 7 days.

If nobody has checked in recently, the card reads **No check-ins in the last 7 days**. Click **Live view** to open the full page for today's check-ins (see [Live view](#live-view-of-todays-check-ins)).

#### Demographics

The **Demographics** card describes your active and newcomer members. Use the switch at the top to choose **Gender**, **Age group** or **Nationality**. Each view shows a chart with a count and share for each value.

- **Gender** shows Male, Female, Other, Prefer not to say and Not set.
- **Age group** shows each of your age groups. See [Age groups](/guides/age-groups).
- **Nationality** shows the four most common nationalities. If there are more, click the extra row to see the full breakdown in Reports.

Under the gender and age group charts, a note tells you what percentage of member records have that detail filled in. The note turns amber when fewer than 70% of records have it, which is a sign the chart may not tell the whole story. Click a gender or age group to open **Members** filtered to it.

#### Members

The **Members** card lists the branch's members, showing each person's **Name**, **Gender**, **Age Group**, **Nationality** and **Status**, 50 to a page.

- Use **Search by name, email, or phone…** to find someone.
- Click **Filters** to narrow the list by **Status**, **Gender**, **Age group**, **Nationality**, **Tags** or **Custom fields**. Bethel ChMS remembers your filters for each branch on that browser.
- Org Owners, Org Admins, Branch Admins and Editors can click **Add Member** to add someone new. See [Managing members](/guides/managing-members).

## Live view of today's check-ins

**Live view** on the **Latest check-ins** card opens a full-page view of today's check-ins, titled **Latest check-ins**, with a **Live** label and a **Today** badge. Keep it open on a screen during a service to watch people arrive. New check-ins appear at the top as they happen, without refreshing the page.

At the top are three summaries:

- **Today**: the total number of check-ins today.
- **Visitors today**: how many of those were visitors.
- **By age group**: a chart of today's check-ins by age group.

The list below shows each person, the time they checked in and how long ago, the event and location, their status (**Visitor**, **Active**, **Newcomer** or **Inactive**), their age group and how they checked in. Use **Search by name…** to find someone, and **Load more** to see more. Click a person to see their details, such as gender, email and mobile (and nationality for members), with quick links to call, message or email them.

If nobody has checked in yet today, the page reads **No check-ins yet today**. To return to the Dashboard, click the back arrow at the top left of the page.

Org Owners and Org Admins can switch branch on this page with the branch selector, just as on the Dashboard.

## Your branch choice across pages

When an Org Owner or Org Admin chooses a branch, that branch stays selected as you move between pages, whether you use the sidebar, the tablet rail, the phone tab bar or the **More** menu. Bethel ChMS also remembers it the next time you open the app on that browser.

The selected branch applies to every page in the **People**, **Church life** and **Giving & outreach** groups, such as **Members**, **Households**, **Groups**, **Visitors**, **Care**, **Events**, **Attendance**, **Ministries**, **Activity Log**, **Locations** and **Gifting & Donations**, and to **Users** and **Import**.

Branch Admins, Editors, Treasurers and Care Managers always work in their own branch and don't see a branch selector.

## The Organisation Dashboard

<Info>
**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin
</Info>

In the sidebar, open the **Organisation** group and click **Organisation dashboard**. On a tablet or phone, tap **More**, then look under **Organisation**. The page is titled **Organisation Dashboard**, with **All branches** underneath, because it always covers your whole network.

{/* TODO: new screenshot: the redesigned Organisation Dashboard showing the date range control, KPI row and Branch Performance card */}

### Choose a date range

Below the title, choose the period the dashboard covers: **7 days**, **30 days**, **90 days** or **12 months**. The dates covered are shown alongside. On a phone, tap the button showing the current range, for example **Last 30 days**, choose a range in the **Date range** sheet and tap **Apply**.

The dashboard starts on 30 days. Next to the range you'll see when the figures were last updated, for example **Updated just now**.

The range applies to the attendance totals, branch performance, branch health and attendance trends. **Network Growth** always shows the last 12 months, the weekly chart on **Network Attendance** always shows the last 12 weeks, and demographics always show your current members, so none of these change with the range.

### Summary tiles

Four tiles at the top give you the headline figures:

- **Attendance**: total check-ins across all branches in the period, with the percentage change compared with the period of the same length just before it.
- **Active members**: how many active members you have, compared with last month.
- **Highest attendance** and **Lowest attendance**: the branches with the most and fewest check-ins in the period. Click one to open that branch's Dashboard.

### Organisation Dashboard cards

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Network Attendance** | The total check-ins for the period and its percentage change, plus a chart of weekly check-ins across all branches for the last 12 weeks. |
| **Network Growth** | The number of active members at the end of each month, for the last 12 months. |
| **Branch Performance** | A row for each branch with its **Active Members**, **Weekly Avg Attendance**, **30-Day Growth (%)**, **Conversion Rate (%)** and **Ministry Participation (%)**. Click **Gender M/F %** to add a column showing each branch's gender split. Click a branch to open its Dashboard. |
| **Branch Health Leaderboard** | Branches ranked by a combined score: attendance counts for 40%, growth 30%, conversion 20% and events 10%. The top branch is highlighted. Click a branch to see how it scores on each part. |
| Demographics card | Use the switch to choose **Gender**, **Age group** or **Nationality**. The card's title changes to match: **Network Gender Demographics**, **Network Age Group Demographics** or **Nationality Distribution**. For gender and age group, choose **Network overview** for the whole network or **By branch** to compare branches. Nationality shows the four most common nationalities; the full breakdown is in Reports. |
| **Attendance Trends** | Weekly check-ins for each branch as lines on a chart, starting with the five branches that had the most check-ins in the period, plus a dashed **Network Average** line. Click a branch to show or hide its line. A table underneath lists the weekly figures. |

## Choose the right dashboard

| Use this dashboard | When you need to |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | See today's live events, open the kiosk, watch check-ins come in, and look at one branch's members and demographics. |
| **Organisation Dashboard** | Compare branches and follow network-wide attendance, growth and demographics over time. |
