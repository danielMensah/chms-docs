Bethel ChMS uses six roles to control what people can see and do. Each role works at one of two levels: across your whole **organisation**, or within a single **branch**.

Your **organisation** is your church network. It contains all your branches, members and settings. A **branch** is one congregation or site within it.

If your church uses **Porter**, people can also hold a separate Porter access level. Porter is the part of Bethel ChMS that church workers and volunteers use on their phones. See [Porter access levels](#porter-access-levels).

<Note>
If you used Bethel ChMS before, the **Care Manager** role was previously called Backslider Manager, and the area it works in is now called **Care**.
</Note>

## The six roles

| Role | Works across | In short |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Org Owner** | The whole organisation | Full control, including billing, ownership and deleting the organisation |
| **Org Admin** | The whole organisation | Headquarters oversight and administration across every branch |
| **Branch Admin** | One branch | Runs one branch, including its members, events, attendance, visitors, care and users |
| **Editor** | One branch | Adds and updates records in one branch, but cannot delete them |
| **Treasurer** | One branch | **Gifting & Donations** only |
| **Care Manager** | One branch | **Members** and **Care** only |

<Tip>
On a computer, your own role is shown at the top of the sidebar, under your organisation's name.
</Tip>

### Org Owner

There is one Org Owner per organisation. The owner has full control over the organisation, including settings, branches, users, billing, currency, ownership transfer and organisation deletion.

The Org Owner is the only person who can transfer ownership to someone else. Nobody can be given the Org Owner role any other way.

### Org Admin

Org Admins have visibility across the entire organisation. They can view branch records across all branches and manage supported branch records after selecting a branch. They can manage users, branches, custom fields, tags, update campaigns, Care settings and organisation-wide reports. When Porter is switched on, they can also give people Porter access.

Think of this role as headquarters oversight and administration. Most day-to-day branch data entry still happens at the branch level.

### Branch Admin

Branch Admins have full control within their own branch. They can create, edit and delete members, events, locations, attendance records and visitors. They work Care cases, set up absence rules for their branch, and can invite other Branch Admins and Editors to their branch.

Branch Admins do not see **Update Campaigns** or **Gifting & Donations**. Update campaigns are run by Org Owners and Org Admins, and giving records are kept by Org Owners, Org Admins and Treasurers. Branch Admins also cannot give Porter access.

This is the right role for a local church administrator or branch pastor who manages their own congregation's data.

### Editor

Editors can create and edit records within their branch, including members, events, locations, attendance and visitors. They can also work Care cases in their branch: **Member care**, **Visitor care** and **Pastoral care**. They cannot delete records (apart from removing someone from an attendance list), manage users, set up absence rules or Care settings, or access update campaigns.

Assign this role to volunteers or staff who need to enter data but should not remove anything or invite other users.

### Treasurer

Treasurers are finance users who work in one branch. Their sidebar shows only **Gifting & Donations**, and that is where they land when they sign in. They can use **Gifting & Donations** and the gifting reports for their assigned branch.

Treasurers cannot access members, Care, update campaigns, events, ministries, attendance, users, branch settings or organisation settings. They cannot be assigned Care cases.

When you choose this role, the form shows: "Gifting & Donations only. Cannot access members, events, or other branch data."

Assign this role to finance team members who need donation records without wider church management access.

### Care Manager

Care Managers work in one branch and focus on following people up. Their sidebar shows only **Members** and **Care**, and they land on **Member care** when they sign in.

In their branch, Care Managers can:

- Work cases on all three Care tabs (**Member care**, **Visitor care** and **Pastoral care**): add notes, change the status, assignee and due date, and open new pastoral care cases.
- Convert a visitor in Visitor care into a member.
- View, edit and delete member records.
- Be assigned Care cases, including automatically.

Care Managers cannot add new members, open the **Visitors** page, add visitors to Visitor care by hand, set up **Absence rules** or open Care settings. If they try to open any other page, Bethel ChMS takes them back to **Member care**.

When you choose this role, the form shows: "Care & Members only. Can view, edit and delete members in their branch. Cannot configure absence rules or access other pages."

Assign this role to pastoral care team members who need to follow people up without wider access to church records. See [Care](/guides/care) for how follow-up works.

## Organisation-wide and branch roles

Roles fall into two groups based on what they can see:

- **Organisation-wide roles** (**Org Owner** and **Org Admin**) can see data across all branches. Branch-level pages let them select a branch and manage supported records in that branch.
- **Branch roles** (**Branch Admin**, **Editor**, **Treasurer** and **Care Manager**) only see their own branch. They work with the data they're responsible for and have no visibility into other branches' records.

Every branch role must be given a branch. If you choose one of these roles without a branch, the form shows "Branch is required for this role".

This split keeps each branch in charge of its own records, while headquarters keeps oversight of the whole network. Where a branch user can see something that belongs to the wider organisation, it is usually read-only to them. For example, a Branch Admin can see every absence rule in the organisation but can only change the rules for their own branch.

<Info>
Organisation-wide roles use the branch selector for branch-level pages. Check each feature guide for the exact actions available.
</Info>

## Choosing a branch

Org Owners and Org Admins choose the branch they're working in with the branch selector. Pages show one branch at a time, and Bethel ChMS remembers your choice as you move between pages.

- **On a computer:** the branch selector sits at the top of the sidebar, under your organisation's name.
- **On a tablet or phone:** tap the branch chip in the top bar of a branch-level page to open the **Branch** sheet, then pick a branch. Tablets also have the selector in the **More** menu.

Branch Admins, Editors, Treasurers and Care Managers never see the branch selector. Bethel ChMS always uses their assigned branch. See [Find your way around](/getting-started/find-your-way-around) for more on the sidebar and menus.

## What each role sees in the sidebar

Everyone except Treasurers and Care Managers sees **Dashboard** at the top of the sidebar, followed by these groups:

| Role | People | Church life | Giving & outreach | Organisation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Org Owner**, **Org Admin** | Members, Households, Groups, Visitors, Care | Events, Attendance, Ministries, Activity Log, Locations | Gifting & Donations, Update Campaigns | Organisation dashboard, Branches, Users, Reports, Automation, Import, Age Groups, Tags, Settings |
| **Branch Admin** | Members, Households, Groups, Visitors, Care | Events, Attendance, Ministries, Activity Log, Locations | None | Users, Reports, Import, Tags, Organisation Settings |
| **Editor** | Members, Households, Groups, Visitors, Care | Events, Attendance, Ministries, Activity Log, Locations | None | Organisation Settings |
| **Treasurer** | None | None | Gifting & Donations | None |
| **Care Manager** | Members, Care | None | None | None |

For Branch Admins and Editors, **Groups** appears under the name their branch uses for groups. Some items, such as **Care**, **Gifting & Donations** and **Update Campaigns**, only appear if your plan includes them. See [Feature availability by plan](/guides/feature-availability-by-plan).

When Porter is switched on and your own member record has a Porter access level, a **Porter** link also appears in the sidebar. Treasurers and Care Managers do not see this link.

### Confidential pastoral care cases

A pastoral care case can be marked **Confidential**. A confidential case is visible only to the person it's assigned to, the person who opened it, and Org Owners and Org Admins. Other users in the branch, including Branch Admins, do not see it. See [Care](/guides/care).

## Porter access levels

Porter is the part of Bethel ChMS for church workers and volunteers, used on a phone. Workers check in when they arrive for a service, loan and return equipment, and report faults. Porter has two access levels of its own, shown on the **Users** page as:

- **Porter · Worker**
- **Porter · Admin**

Porter access works differently from the six roles above:

- **It sits on the person's member record**, not on a Bethel ChMS role. Anyone can have it: a volunteer with no Bethel ChMS role at all, or a Bethel ChMS user of any role. On the **Users** page, people with Porter but no Bethel ChMS role show **No ChMS role** and **Porter only**.
- **It covers the whole organisation**, not one branch. A Porter admin works across every branch, whatever their Bethel ChMS role. For example, a Branch Admin who is also a Porter admin can set up check-in and manage equipment for all branches in Porter.
- **Only Org Owners and Org Admins can give, change or remove it**, and only once Porter is switched on for your church. Branch Admins can see who has Porter on the **Users** page, but cannot change it.

| What they can do in Porter | Worker | Admin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Check in on arrival | Yes | Yes |
| Loan and return equipment (after checking in) | Yes | Yes |
| Report a fault | Yes | Yes |
| Browse the equipment register and see their own history | Yes | Yes |
| Use **Setup** (check-in areas, equipment tags, return reminders) | No | Yes |
| Check workers in by hand | No | Yes |
| See **Alerts** | No | Yes |
| Add and edit equipment, print labels and export the register | No | Yes |
| See everyone's history and rejected check-in attempts | No | Yes |

For switching Porter on, giving access and signing in, see [Porter](/guides/porter).

## Who can invite whom

Not every role can invite every other role. Here's how the invitation chain works:

| Your role | You can invite |
| --- | --- |
| Org Owner | Org Admin, Branch Admin, Editor, Treasurer or Care Manager |
| Org Admin | Org Admin, Branch Admin, Editor, Treasurer or Care Manager |
| Branch Admin | Branch Admin or Editor in your own branch only |
| Editor | No one |
| Treasurer | No one |
| Care Manager | No one |

<Note>
Branch Admins can only invite people into their own branch. They cannot invite **Org Admin**, **Treasurer** or **Care Manager** users, and they cannot give Porter access. Nobody can invite someone as **Org Owner**. Use [ownership transfer](#transferring-ownership) instead.
</Note>

To send an invitation, see [User management](/guides/user-management).

## How role changes take effect

When you change someone's role or branch, the change is saved straight away. If the person has Bethel ChMS open at the time, ask them to sign out and sign back in, so their menu and pages match their new role.

<Note>
If your church's subscription is suspended, everyone in the organisation, whatever their role, sees a billing page instead of the usual app, and Porter shows a paused screen. See [Billing and subscription](/guides/billing-and-subscription).
</Note>

## Transferring ownership

**Who can do this:** Org Owner

Only the current Org Owner can transfer ownership. You might do this when a senior pastor hands over leadership, or when a denominational office restructures how accounts are managed.

The new owner must already be an **Org Admin**. If you don't have one yet, [edit the person's role](/guides/user-management#edit-a-user) to **Org Admin** first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open organisation settings">
    In the sidebar, open **Organisation** and click **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the transfer">
    In the **Ownership & Danger Zone** card, click **Transfer Ownership**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the new owner">
    Under **New Owner**, choose an Org Admin from the list. If you have no Org Admins, the sheet shows "You must first promote a user to Org Admin before transferring ownership."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with your password">
    Enter your password under **Confirm Your Password**. Let the security check finish. It usually completes by itself, and occasionally asks you to tick a box. Then click **Transfer Ownership**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When the transfer succeeds, you see "Ownership transferred successfully". You become an **Org Admin** and the person you chose becomes the **Org Owner**. If your password is wrong, you see "Incorrect password" and nothing changes.

<Warning>
Ownership transfer is immediate and cannot be undone by you. The new owner would need to transfer it back. Make sure you're selecting the right person.
</Warning>
