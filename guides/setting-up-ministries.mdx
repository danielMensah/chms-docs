A **ministry** in Bethel ChMS is a team, department, or service group within a branch, such as a worship team, an ushering department, or a children's ministry. Ministries are not events. They help you organise who is responsible for what. Each ministry has a **roster**, the list of people who serve in it.

<Info>
**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin, and Editor can create and edit ministries, archive them, and manage ministry rosters. Only Org Owner, Org Admin, and Branch Admin can delete ministries and link or unlink groups. Treasurers and Care Managers don't see Ministries.
</Info>

Ministries belong to one branch. Branch Admins and Editors always work in their own branch. Org Owners and Org Admins see one branch at a time, so choose the branch with the branch selector before you create or edit a ministry. See [Find your way around](/getting-started/find-your-way-around).

## Open ministries

In the sidebar, open **Church life** and click **Ministries**. On tablets and phones, tap **More**, then find **Ministries** under **Church life**.

## Find a ministry

The ministries list shows each ministry's name, how many members it has, its leaders (or **No leader**), and an **Active** or **Archived** badge. On tablets and computers, each row also shows the description under the name, and the list uses the columns **Name**, **Leader(s)**, **Members**, and **Status**. The widest screens add a **Created** column.

The member count includes each person once, even if they are a direct member and also come in through a linked group.

To narrow the list:

- Type in the search box to find a ministry by its name, its description, or a leader's name.
- Click **All**, **Active**, or **Archived** above the list. Each chip shows how many ministries it contains.
- Click **Filters** to sort the list and filter by **Status**. You can sort by **Name**, **Status**, **Member Count**, or **Created At**.

Click a ministry to open its page. If you can edit ministries, each row also has a **⋯** button with **Open ministry**, **Edit**, and **Delete**. Only Org Owner, Org Admin, and Branch Admin see **Delete**.

The footer has a **Groups** link that takes you straight to [your groups](/guides/groups).

## Create a ministry

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Ministries">
    In the sidebar, open **Church life** and click **Ministries**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new ministry">
    Click **Add Ministry** in the top bar. On phones, tap **+**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Enter a **Name** for the ministry, up to 100 characters. The name must be unique within your branch. Optionally, add a **Description** to explain the ministry's purpose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the ministry">
    Click **Create Ministry**. The ministry starts as **Active**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once the ministry exists, open it to add members or link groups.

## The ministry page

The top of the ministry page shows:

- The ministry's status and description.
- The number of members, the number of linked groups, and the date the ministry was created.
- A **Leaders** section listing every **Leader** and **Assistant Leader**. When contact details exist, you can call, message, or email them from here.

If the ministry is archived, a banner says that no new members can be added.

Below that, the page has three tabs: **Roster**, **Linked Groups**, and **Change History**. The **Roster** and **Linked Groups** tabs show a count.

The **More actions** menu (⋮) in the top bar holds **Add Member** (for active ministries), **Edit Ministry**, and, for Org Owner, Org Admin, and Branch Admin, **Delete Ministry**.

## Add a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the ministry">
    Click the ministry in the ministries list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Add Member">
    Click **Add Member** in the top bar, or open the **More actions** menu (⋮) and choose **Add Member**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search and select">
    Type part of the person's first name or last name, then select them from the results. Each result shows the person's age group and tags to help you pick the right person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a role">
    Choose a **Role**: **Member** (the default), **Leader**, or **Assistant Leader**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the joined date and confirm">
    Check the **Joined Date**, which defaults to today, and click **Add Member**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* TODO: new screenshot: the Add Member sheet with the member search, Role, and Joined Date fields */}

<Info>
You can only add people who are members of the ministry's branch. If the person is already a direct member of the ministry, Bethel ChMS tells you they are already active in this ministry.
</Info>

You cannot add members to an archived ministry. The **Add Member** button is hidden until you make the ministry active again.

## Ministry roles explained

Each person in a ministry has one of three roles:

- **Leader:** A person responsible for the ministry.
- **Assistant Leader:** Supports the leaders in coordinating the team.
- **Member:** A general participant in the ministry.

<Tip>
A ministry can have several leaders and assistant leaders. For example, a large worship ministry might have two leaders who alternate between services. Making someone a leader does not change anyone else's role. This is different from [groups](/guides/groups), which have one leader and one assistant leader each.
</Tip>

People who join through a linked group always have the **Member** role in the ministry.

## Viewing the roster

The **Roster** tab lists everyone currently serving in the ministry. Each row shows:

- The person's name and age group.
- Where they come from: **Direct member** if you added them to the ministry, or **via** and the group's name if they come in through a linked group.
- Their tags and their role.
- The date they joined, on tablets and computers.
- Contact buttons, on wider screens.

A line above the roster shows how many people are direct members and how many come in through linked groups.

Use the **Flat** or **By group** view to choose how you read the roster:

- **Flat** shows each person once. If a person is a direct member and also comes in through a linked group, the role from their direct membership is shown.
- **By group** shows one section for each linked group that has members, with the group's name and a count. Click the group's name to open it. A **Direct only** section lists people who are direct members and are not in any linked group.

## Change a member's role

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the person's options">
    On the **Roster** tab, tap or click the person's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the role">
    Choose **Change Role**, pick the new **Role**, and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Removing a member from a ministry

When you remove someone from a ministry, Bethel ChMS sets a leave date on their assignment rather than deleting it. This keeps a record of the time they served.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the person's options">
    On the **Roster** tab, tap or click the person's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove the member">
    Choose **Remove from ministry**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm removal">
    Click **Remove** in the **Remove Member** confirmation. Their direct membership ends. If they also come in through a linked group, they stay on the roster through that group.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If a person rejoins a ministry later, Bethel ChMS creates a new assignment. Their earlier time in the ministry is kept as a separate record. You can see a person's current and past ministry assignments on the **Ministry Assignments** tab of their member profile.
</Note>

## Linking groups to a ministry

A **group** is a small fellowship unit in your branch, such as a home cell. You can link groups to a ministry when they are sub-teams, cells, or fellowships that belong under it. A ministry can have many linked groups, but each group can link to only one ministry.

<Info>
**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, and Branch Admin.
</Info>

When you link a group, Bethel ChMS adds everyone currently in the group to the ministry roster. When people join or leave the group, the ministry roster updates to match. Direct ministry members stay separate, so unlinking a group does not remove people who are also direct members or who are in another linked group.

You can only link groups to an active ministry, and the group must be in the same branch as the ministry.

### Link a group from a ministry

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the ministry">
    In the sidebar, open **Church life**, click **Ministries**, and open the ministry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Linked Groups">
    Click the **Linked Groups** tab, then click **Link a group**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a group">
    Select a group from the list. Groups that are already linked to a ministry do not appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the link">
    Click **Link Group**. Bethel ChMS adds the group's members to the ministry roster and tells you how many were added.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Linked Groups** tab lists each linked group with its **Leader** and number of **Members**. Click a group's name to open it.

### Link a ministry when creating a group

You can also link a group while creating it. In **Groups**, click **Add Group**, choose the ministry under **Ministry (optional)**, then click **Create Group**. See [Groups](/guides/groups#link-a-group-to-a-ministry).

If the group is created but the link fails, Bethel ChMS keeps the group and shows a message explaining why the link failed. Editors can create groups, but only Org Owner, Org Admin, and Branch Admin can link them.

### Unlink a group

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the ministry">
    In the sidebar, open **Church life**, click **Ministries**, and open the ministry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the group">
    Click the **Linked Groups** tab and find the group you want to unlink.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview the impact">
    Click **Unlink**. Bethel ChMS shows how many people will be removed from the roster, and how many will stay because they are direct members or are in another linked group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm unlinking">
    Click **Unlink** to remove the link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Unlinking removes the group's members from the ministry roster, unless they are direct members or in another linked group. It does not remove anyone from the group itself.

### Members who come in through a group

People who join a ministry through a linked group are managed from the group, not from the ministry roster. On the ministry roster, **Change Role** and **Remove from ministry** are unavailable for these people, with a note naming the group they come in through.

To remove one of these people from the ministry, remove them from the linked group, or unlink the group. Bethel ChMS keeps the ministry roster in step with the group.

To give one of these people a different role in the ministry, such as **Leader**, add them as a direct member with **Add Member** and choose the role. The **Flat** view then shows them once, with the role from their direct membership.

## Archiving a ministry

Archiving a ministry sets its status to **Archived**. Archived ministries keep their roster and history, but you can no longer add new members or link groups. You can make an archived ministry active again at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the edit form">
    Open the ministry and click **Edit**, or open the **More actions** menu (⋮) and choose **Edit Ministry**. You can also choose **Edit** from the **⋯** button on the ministry's row in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Archive the ministry">
    Set **Status** to **Archived** and click **Save Changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To reactivate the ministry, follow the same steps and set **Status** to **Active**.

<Warning>
You cannot archive a ministry while groups are linked to it. Unlink all groups first.
</Warning>

<Tip>
Use archiving when a ministry is on pause but you want to keep its history. For example, a seasonal outreach team that only runs in December.
</Tip>

## Deleting a ministry

<Info>
**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, and Branch Admin. Editors cannot delete ministries.
</Info>

Deleting a ministry removes it from your ministries list and ends all its active member assignments.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the deletion">
    In the ministries list, click the **⋯** button on the ministry's row and choose **Delete**. Or, on the ministry page, open the **More actions** menu (⋮) and choose **Delete Ministry**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Delete**. If you deleted the ministry from its own page, Bethel ChMS takes you back to the ministries list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
You cannot delete a ministry while groups are linked to it. Unlink all groups first. There is no option in the app to restore a deleted ministry.
</Warning>

<Note>
If you just want to pause a ministry, archive it instead. Archiving keeps the ministry in your list with its roster, and you can reactivate it whenever you need to.
</Note>

## Ministries in Porter

If your church uses Porter, the part of Bethel ChMS that workers and volunteers use on their phones, your active ministries also act as departments for equipment. When you add an item to the equipment register, you can choose a ministry as its **Owning department**. The department appears on equipment labels and exports, and you can use it to filter labels for printing. See [Porter equipment](/guides/porter-equipment).

## Change history

The **Change History** tab shows changes to the ministry's **Name**, **Description**, and **Status**. See [Change history](/guides/change-history).
