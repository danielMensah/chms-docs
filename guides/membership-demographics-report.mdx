title: "Membership demographics report"
description: "Use the Membership Demographics report in Bethel ChMS to count your members by age group and gender, add composite totals and a nationality breakout, fix missing details, and export to CSV or PDF."
---

The **Membership Demographics** report counts your members by age group and gender in one table, called a cross-tab. Use it when you need board-ready totals: how many youth you have, your male and female split, how many active members attended recently, or the figures for a mid-year or end-of-year membership report.

The report uses the [age groups](/guides/age-groups) your organisation has set up. A member's age group comes from their date of birth, or is set by hand when there is no date of birth.

<Info>
**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin and Branch Admin. Editors don't have **Reports** in the sidebar, and Treasurers and Care Managers can't open this report.
</Info>

## Open the report

1. In the sidebar, open **Organisation** and click **Reports**. On a tablet held upright, tap **Reports** in the rail. On a phone, tap **More**, then **Reports**.
2. On the **Build Report** tab, under **Build a report**, click **Membership Demographics**.

The report opens as its own page, and the preview loads straight away. Click **Back to Reports** (the back arrow in the top bar) to return to the list, or click **⋯** and choose **Change report type** to open a different report.

Three buttons sit above the preview: **Filters**, **Composite rows** and **Nationality breakout**, numbered 1 to 3 on a tablet or computer. Each shows what is currently set and opens a panel where you change it. On a phone, these panels slide up from the bottom of the screen.

## Choose who is counted

Click **Filters** to decide which members appear in the cross-tab. The top of the panel shows how many members match. Changes apply as you make them.

| Filter | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Lifecycle Status** | Counts members with the statuses you tick: **Newcomer**, **Active** or **Inactive**. New reports start with **Active** only. If you untick every status, the report counts **Active** members. |
| **Branch** | Counts members from **All branches** or one branch. Branch Admins don't see this filter, because their reports always cover their own branch. |
| **Tags** | Counts members with the tags you pick. With two or more tags, choose **Match any** or **Match all**. See [Tags](/guides/tags). |
| Custom fields | Filters by your member [custom fields](/guides/organisation-settings#custom-fields). Yes/no fields offer **Is true**, **Is false** and **Is false or not set**. |
| **Attendance recency** | Turn this on to count only members who attended at least once in the last few months. Set **Months** between 1 and 24. It starts at 6. |

Click **Show** (for example, **Show 312 members**) to close the panel, or **Clear All Filters** to remove every filter, including **Attendance recency**.

## Read the cross-tab

Under **Preview**, a line starting **Active filters:** lists the status, custom field and attendance recency filters in use. The table has one row for each age group, in your organisation's age group order, and these columns:

- **Male** and **Female** always appear.
- **Other**, **Prefer not to say** and **Not specified** appear only when at least one counted member falls into them. **Not specified** is for members with no gender recorded.
- **Total** is the right-hand column, adding up each row.

Below the age groups you may see:

- An **Unknown** row, for members who have no age group. It only appears when there are such members.
- A **Composite rows** section, if you have added any. See [Add composite rows](#add-composite-rows).
- An **Overall Members** row at the bottom, with the totals for everyone counted.

If no one matches your filters, the preview reads "No members match your filters."

## Add composite rows

Composite rows add named totals that combine several age groups, without changing your age group setup. For example, you could add **Total Youth (13-35)** or **Total Adults (13+)**.

1. Click **Composite rows**.
2. Click **Add composite**.
3. Type a label in the label box, for example "Total Youth".
4. Tick the age groups to include.
5. Click **Done**.

An age group can be in more than one composite row. To remove a composite row, click the bin button next to its label.

<Note>
If a composite row includes an age group that has since been deleted, Bethel ChMS skips that age group and shows a notice in the **Composite rows** panel. The rest of the report is not affected.
</Note>

## Add a nationality breakout

A nationality breakout adds a second table that counts members of the nationalities you choose.

1. Click **Nationality breakout**.
2. Click **Select nationalities...** and use **Search nationalities...** to find the ones you want. Only nationalities recorded on your members are listed.
3. Pick each nationality you want to show. Your choices appear as chips, and you can click the cross on a chip to remove it.
4. Click **Done**.

The **Nationality breakout** table appears below the cross-tab. Members of other nationalities are counted under **Other nationalities**, and members with no nationality recorded are counted under **Not set**.

## Fix missing demographic details

When some counted members are missing an age group, gender or nationality, a banner appears above the preview: "Some members have incomplete demographic data." It shows a count for each missing detail, such as "12 missing age group", "3 missing gender" or "5 missing nationality". Members with no age group are counted in the **Unknown** row, members with no gender under **Not specified**, and members with no nationality under **Not set** in the nationality breakout.

The counts follow all of your report filters, so a member you have filtered out is not counted as missing data. You can close the banner with its cross. It comes back when the preview reloads, for example after you change a filter, or when you next open the report.

Click a count to open **Members missing age group** (or gender, or nationality). The list shows each member's name and branch, 25 at a time. From here you can fill in the gaps:

- **One member at a time:** choose a value in the **Set** box on that member's row, for example **Set gender…**. It saves straight away and the row shows **Saved**.
- **Several members at once:** tick the members, or tick the box at the top to select everyone on the page. Choose a value in the box that appears above the list, then click **Apply to** (for example, **Apply to 8**). You see a message such as "Updated 8 members".
- **Open the full profile:** click a member's name. Their profile opens in a new tab, so you keep your place in the report.

Bethel ChMS logs every change you make here, with the reason "Completeness fix from demographics report".

<Note>
Members who have a date of birth get their age group from it, so you can't set it by hand here. They are skipped: the member's row says so, and after a change to several members the message tells you how many were skipped. To fix their age group, check their date of birth, or check your [age group ranges](/guides/age-groups).
</Note>

## Export the report

The export bar stays at the bottom of the page. It has two buttons, and on a tablet or computer it also shows how many members are counted:

- **Export CSV** creates a spreadsheet file with the same numbers as the preview. The CSV always includes all five gender columns plus **Total**, even ones the preview hides, so files from different months line up. Your nationality breakout is added below the cross-tab.
- **Export PDF** creates a formatted PDF using the same filters as the preview, including the nationality breakout.

The buttons are available once the preview has loaded and at least one member is counted. Files are named with today's date, such as `membership_demographics-2026-10-17.csv`.

On a computer, the file downloads straight away. On a phone or tablet that can share files, your device's share options open instead, so you can save the file or send it by email or message. A message such as "membership_demographics-2026-10-17.pdf is ready." then appears for a few seconds with **Download**, to save another copy, and **Share** on devices that can share files.

## Save the report as a template

Save your setup to run the same report again later. The template keeps your lifecycle status, branch, tag and custom field filters, attendance recency, composite rows and nationality breakout.

1. On a tablet or computer, click **Save as template** in the top bar. On a phone, tap **⋯**, then **Save as template**.
2. Enter a **Name** and, if you like, a **Description (optional)**.
3. Click **Save**.

This report always saves a new template. If you loaded a saved demographics template and changed it, saving creates a separate copy, and the original stays as it was.

To load a saved template, open **Reports**, go to the **Templates** tab and click the template. See [Use templates](/guides/reports#use-templates).

## Use the built-in membership template

Every organisation has a system template called **Mid & End of Year Membership Report**. It counts active members across all branches (Branch Admins see their own branch) and includes two composite rows:

- **Total Youth (13-35)**
- **Total Adults (13+)**

These composite rows are set by age range rather than by named age groups, so they work with your own age groups. When you load the template, Bethel ChMS includes each of your age groups that falls entirely inside the range. If one of your age groups only partly overlaps a range, for example a 30 to 40 age group against the 13 to 35 youth range, it is left out of that composite and a notice in the **Composite rows** panel explains why.

System templates can't be changed. To make your own version, **Clone** it from the **Templates** tab, or load it, adjust it and click **Save as template**.
