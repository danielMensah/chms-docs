Now that your church is set up, invite the people who'll help manage it. A **user** is someone on your team who signs in to Bethel ChMS. Each user has a **role** that controls what they can see and do.

<Info>
**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin. Branch Admins can only invite Branch Admins and Editors to their own branch.
</Info>

## Send an invitation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Users page">
    In the sidebar, open **Organisation** and click **Users**. On a tablet held portrait, or a phone, open **More** and tap **Users** under **Organisation**.

    The **Users** page lists everyone who can sign in to your organisation, along with anyone you've invited.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Add user form">
    Click **Add user** at the top of the page. On a narrow screen this button shows only a person icon with a plus sign. The **Add user** form opens.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    | Field | Required | Notes |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | **Email** | Yes | The email address the invitation is sent to. They'll sign in with this address. |
    | **Name** | Yes | The person's name as it appears in Bethel ChMS. |
    | **Role** | Yes | **Org Owners** and **Org Admins** can choose **Org Admin**, **Branch Admin**, **Editor**, **Treasurer**, or **Care Manager**. **Branch Admins** can choose **Branch Admin** or **Editor**. |
    | **Branch** | Sometimes | Appears when you choose **Branch Admin**, **Editor**, **Treasurer**, or **Care Manager**, and is required for those roles. If you're a **Branch Admin**, it's set to your own branch and you can't change it. **Org Admins** work across every branch, so they don't need one. |

    <Frame caption="When Porter is switched on, Org Owners and Org Admins also see the Apps choice. Leave Bethel ChMS selected unless the person needs Porter.">
      <img src="/images/getting-started/invite-add-user-sheet.png" alt="The Add user panel in Bethel ChMS, with an email address entered, Bethel ChMS chosen under Apps, the name Ruth Appiah, Care Manager chosen as the role with its note about access, London Central chosen as the branch, and the Cancel and Create user buttons" width="480" />
    </Frame>

    When you choose **Treasurer** or **Care Manager**, a short note under **Role** reminds you what that role can access.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create the user">
    Click **Create user**. Bethel ChMS emails the person an invitation, and a message confirms **Invitation sent to** their email address. They appear on the **Users** page under **Invites** until they open the link in their invitation email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose the right role

Not sure which role to pick? Here's a quick summary:

- **Org Admin:** Organisation-level access across every branch. Good for senior pastors or headquarters staff who oversee the whole network.
- **Branch Admin:** Manages members, attendance, and events for one branch. Ideal for local pastors or office managers.
- **Editor:** Can add and update records within a branch, but can't change settings or invite others.
- **Treasurer:** Works only in **Gifting & Donations** for one branch, with no access to members, events, or other branch data.
- **Care Manager:** Works only in **Care** and **Members** for one branch. They follow up people who need care, and can view, edit, and delete members in their branch, but can't set up the rules that spot absences or open any other pages.

For a full breakdown of what each role can do, see [Roles and permissions](/guides/roles-and-permissions).

<Info>
**Who can invite whom:**
- **Org Owner** can invite **Org Admin**, **Branch Admin**, **Editor**, **Treasurer**, and **Care Manager**.
- **Org Admin** can invite **Org Admin**, **Branch Admin**, **Editor**, **Treasurer**, and **Care Manager**.
- **Branch Admin** can invite **Branch Admin** and **Editor** within their own branch.
- **Editor**, **Treasurer**, and **Care Manager** can't invite anyone.
</Info>

## Give Porter access

**Porter** is the Bethel ChMS phone app for service teams, such as workers and volunteers. When Porter is switched on for your church, Org Owners and Org Admins see an extra **Apps** choice below **Email** in the **Add user** form:

- **Bethel ChMS:** Access to Bethel ChMS only, as described above.
- **Porter:** Access to Porter only.
- **Both apps:** Access to Bethel ChMS and Porter.

When you include Porter, choose a **Porter role** (**Worker** or **Admin**) and use **Link to member record** to pick the person's member record. If you choose **Porter** on its own, the **Name**, **Role**, and **Branch** fields don't appear, because the person only needs Porter. One sign-in works for both apps, so someone with access to both uses the same email and password.

For how Porter works and how to set it up, see [Porter](/guides/porter).

## What the invitee sees

The invitee receives an email inviting them to Bethel ChMS, with an **Accept Invitation** button. When they click it, Bethel ChMS checks the invitation and opens a **Welcome!** page that shows:

- **Organisation:** The name of your organisation.
- **Role:** The role you gave them, such as **Branch Admin** or **Care Manager**. For a Porter-only invitation, this shows **App** with **Porter** instead.
- **Branch:** The branch they belong to, if their role has one.
- **Email:** The email address they'll sign in with.

To accept, they:

1. Choose a **Password** of at least 12 characters, with upper-case and lower-case letters and at least one number.
2. Type it again in **Confirm Password**.
3. Tick **I agree to the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy**.
4. Click **Set Password & Continue**.

They're then signed in and taken to their starting page: the **Dashboard** for most roles, **Gifting & Donations** for a Treasurer, **Care** for a Care Manager, or Porter for someone with Porter access only. Next time, they sign in at [app.bethelchms.com](https://app.bethelchms.com). See [Sign in and reset your password](/getting-started/sign-in).

If you gave someone access to both apps and their Porter access couldn't be added when they accepted, the page tells them, and they can continue into Bethel ChMS. Add their Porter access again from the **Users** page, using **Edit** on their row.

## If an invitation expires

Invitation links expire. If someone opens an old link, Bethel ChMS can't verify it, and they see a message such as **Could not verify invitation** or **This invitation has expired. Please contact your administrator for a new invite.**

To send a new link:

1. On the **Users** page, click **Invites**.
2. Find the person and click **Resend**.

Resending sends a fresh email with a new link. If you're a **Branch Admin**, you can resend invitations only to people in your own branch.

If the person isn't listed under **Invites**, they've already opened their invitation link, so they don't need a new invitation. If they set a password, they can sign in. If they didn't, or they've forgotten it, they can click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page to choose one.

## Manage invitations and users

Use the **Users** page to search for users, filter by role (Org Owners and Org Admins can also filter by branch), and switch between **All**, **Active**, **Invites**, and **Deactivated**. From each user's menu you can edit their details, resend an invitation, or deactivate their access. Branch Admins can edit or deactivate only Branch Admins and Editors in their own branch.

For the full user management workflow, see [User management](/guides/user-management).
