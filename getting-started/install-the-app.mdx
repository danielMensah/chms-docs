You can add Bethel ChMS to the home screen of your phone or tablet, or install it on your computer. It then opens from its own icon, full screen and without the browser's address bar, just like any other app. You install it from your web browser, so there is nothing to download from an app store.

The installed app is called **Bethel ChMS**. Under its icon on your home screen it shows the shorter name "Bethel".

<Info>
**Who can do this:** Anyone who signs in to Bethel ChMS or Porter
</Info>

## One app for Bethel ChMS and Porter

[Porter](/guides/porter) is the part of Bethel ChMS that your church's workers and volunteers use on their phones, for on-site check-in, equipment loans and fault reports. Bethel ChMS and Porter are one app, so you only ever install it once. When you open it, it takes you to the right place for your account.

- If you use both, open Porter from the **Porter** link in the sidebar, or from **More** under **Apps** on a tablet or phone. To come back, use **Back to ChMS** on Porter's **Home** screen (Porter admins find it on **Setup**). See [Find your way around](/getting-started/find-your-way-around#porter).
- If you only use Porter, you can install the app from any Porter page, and it opens straight into Porter.

## Install the app

Sign in at [app.bethelchms.com](https://app.bethelchms.com) on the device you want to use. Once you're signed in, on browsers that can install apps, a bar appears at the top of the screen inviting you to add Bethel ChMS to your home screen. In Porter, the same bar reads **Add Porter to your home screen**. The bar doesn't appear on the sign-in pages.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone and iPad">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Bethel ChMS in Safari">
        Go to [app.bethelchms.com](https://app.bethelchms.com) in Safari and sign in. On iPhone, the bar at the top reads **Add Bethel ChMS to your home screen: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.** If you don't see the bar, carry on with the next step.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap Share">
        Tap the **Share** button in Safari. If you can't see it, tap Safari's menu button first.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add it to your Home Screen">
        Choose **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open the app">
        Open Bethel ChMS from the new "Bethel" icon on your Home Screen. You may see a Bethel ChMS opening screen briefly while the app starts.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Bethel ChMS in Chrome">
        Go to [app.bethelchms.com](https://app.bethelchms.com) in Chrome and sign in. The bar at the top reads **Add Bethel ChMS to your home screen**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap Install">
        Tap **Install** in the bar, then confirm in the window your phone shows.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open the app">
        Open Bethel ChMS from the new "Bethel" icon on your home screen or in your app list.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Computer">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Bethel ChMS in Chrome or Edge">
        Go to [app.bethelchms.com](https://app.bethelchms.com) in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge and sign in. The bar at the top reads **Add Bethel ChMS to your home screen**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Click Install">
        Click **Install** in the bar, then confirm in the window your browser shows.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open the app">
        Bethel ChMS opens in its own window. Next time, open it from your computer's list of apps, like any other program.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
If you don't see the bar, your browser may still let you install the app from its own menu. Look for an option such as **Install** or **Add to Home Screen**. Not every browser can install apps. The bar never appears inside the installed app itself.
</Tip>

### Hide the install bar

If you'd rather not install the app now, tap the **X** at the right of the bar. The bar stays hidden in that browser for 30 days. Hiding it in Bethel ChMS also hides it in Porter, and the other way round. You can still install the app from your browser's menu in the meantime.

## Where the app opens

When you open the installed app, it takes you to your usual starting page.

| You are | The app opens on |
| --- | --- |
| Signed out | The sign-in page (**Welcome back**). Porter workers can sign in here with the same email and password, and they go straight to Porter. |
| An Org Owner, Org Admin, Branch Admin or Editor | Your **Dashboard** |
| A Treasurer | **Gifting & Donations** |
| A Care Manager | **Care**, on the **Member care** tab |
| Someone who only uses Porter | Porter's **Home** screen |

If you use both Bethel ChMS and Porter and you were last working in Porter, the app may open in Porter instead. Use **Back to ChMS** to return.

See [Sign in](/getting-started/sign-in) for help signing in.

## Porter alerts on iPhone and iPad

On iPhone and iPad, Porter alerts only work when the app has been added to the Home Screen and opened from there. On iPhone, until you do this, Porter shows a card titled **Add Porter to your Home Screen** with the same steps as above. On iPad, follow the same steps even if you don't see the card. See [Porter alerts](/guides/porter-alerts).

## Using the installed app

- **You always have the latest version.** Pages load from the internet each time, so there is nothing to update.
- **You need an internet connection.** The installed app doesn't work offline.
- **Exports use your device's share options.** On a phone or tablet, exporting a CSV or PDF opens your device's share options, so you can, for example, save the file to Files, email it or send it with AirDrop.
