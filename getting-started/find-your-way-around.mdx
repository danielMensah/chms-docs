Bethel ChMS works on computers, tablets and phones. The way you move between pages changes with the size of your screen, but every layout shows the same pages for your role.

A few terms you'll see on this page:

- Your **organisation** is your church network. It contains all your branches, members and settings.
- A **branch** is an individual church or campus within your organisation.
- Your **role** decides which pages you can open. See [Roles and permissions](/guides/roles-and-permissions).

## How the layout changes with your screen

| Screen | How you move around |
| --- | --- |
| Computer, or tablet held landscape | A [sidebar](#on-a-computer-the-sidebar) down the left of the screen. |
| Tablet held portrait | A [narrow rail of icons](#on-a-tablet-held-portrait-the-rail) down the left, with a **More** button. |
| Phone | A [tab bar](#on-a-phone-the-tab-bar) along the bottom of the screen, with a **More** button. |

If you turn a tablet on its side, Bethel ChMS switches between the rail and the sidebar to suit the new shape. The layout follows the width of the screen or window, so a narrow browser window on a computer shows the rail or the tab bar too, and a small tablet held portrait can show the phone tab bar.

## On a computer: the sidebar

{/* TODO: new screenshot: the desktop sidebar with the People group open, showing the organisation name, role, branch selector, Porter link and the account footer */}

The top of the sidebar shows your church's initials, your organisation name and your role. Roles appear here as **Organisation owner**, **Organisation admin**, **Branch admin**, **Editor**, **Treasurer** or **Care Manager**. If you are an Org Owner or Org Admin, the [branch selector](#choose-a-branch) sits just below.

Under that you'll find a **Dashboard** link and four groups of pages:

- **People**
- **Church life**
- **Giving & outreach**
- **Organisation**

Click a group to open it and see its pages. Only one group is open at a time, so opening another group closes the first. When you go to a page, its group opens for you. On the **Dashboard**, every group starts closed. Groups that have no pages for your role are not shown.

| Group | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| **People** | **Members**, **Households**, **Groups**, **Visitors**, **Care** |
| **Church life** | **Events**, **Attendance**, **Ministries**, **Activity Log**, **Locations** |
| **Giving & outreach** | **Gifting & Donations**, **Update Campaigns** |
| **Organisation** | **Organisation dashboard**, **Branches**, **Users**, **Reports**, **Automation**, **Import**, **Age Groups**, **Tags**, and **Settings** (Org Owner and Org Admin) or **Organisation Settings** (Branch Admin and Editor) |

You only see the pages your role allows. See [What each role sees](#what-each-role-sees).

<Tip>
If your branch calls its groups something else, such as cells, the sidebar uses your name instead of **Groups**, for example **Cells**. Org Owners and Org Admins always see **Groups**. See [Groups](/guides/groups).
</Tip>

### Porter

If your church has [Porter](/guides/porter) switched on and your own member record has a Porter access level, a **Porter** link with a small **App** label appears below the groups. Click it to open Porter in the same window, without signing in again. Treasurers and Care Managers never see this link.

### Your account

The bottom of the sidebar has three links:

- **Help & docs** opens this documentation in a new tab.
- **What's New** shows recent updates to Bethel ChMS. The label next to it shows the version of Bethel ChMS you are using, for example **v2.0.0**. See [What's new](/guides/whats-new).
- **Cookies & privacy** lets you change your cookie choices and links to the Cookie Policy. See [Cookie choices](#cookie-choices).

Below the links are the email address you signed in with and your role. The button with the sign-out icon next to your email signs you out. See [Sign out](#sign-out).

### Hide or show the sidebar

To give a page more room, click the **Toggle Sidebar** button (the panel icon at the far left of the top bar). The sidebar slides away, and the same button brings it back. You can also press **Ctrl+B** on Windows or **Cmd+B** on a Mac. Bethel ChMS remembers whether you left it open or hidden.

## On a tablet held portrait: the rail

A tablet held upright shows a narrow rail of icons down the left of the screen instead of the full sidebar.

- At the top is a square with your church's initials. Tap it to open the **More** drawer.
- Below it are up to eight shortcuts, chosen from **Dashboard**, **Members**, **Events**, **Attendance**, **Visitors**, **Households**, **Gifting** and **Reports**. If your role can't see some of these, your other pages fill the gaps.
- At the bottom is **More**. It is highlighted when the page you are on is not one of the shortcuts.

To fit, **Gifting & Donations** appears as **Gifting**.

### The More drawer

**More** opens a drawer from the left. At the top you'll see your organisation name and your role, and an **X** button that closes the drawer. Org Owners and Org Admins also see the branch they are viewing next to their role (for example, "Main Campus · Organisation owner"), and get the [branch selector](#choose-a-branch) here.

The drawer then lists **Dashboard** and every page you can open, under the headings **People**, **Church life**, **Giving & outreach** and **Organisation**. If you have Porter access, **Porter** appears under an **Apps** heading. At the bottom are your email, your role, a **Sign out** button and the links **What's New**, **Cookie settings** and **Cookie Policy**.

## On a phone: the tab bar

A phone shows a tab bar fixed to the bottom of the screen with four shortcuts and **More**: **Dashboard**, **Members**, **Events**, **Visitors** and **More**. A Treasurer sees **Gifting** and **More**. A Care Manager sees **Members**, **Care** and **More**.

**More** is highlighted when you are on a page that isn't in the tab bar.

### The More sheet

Tap **More** to slide up a sheet that covers most of the screen. Tap the **X** at the top to put it away. At the top you'll see your organisation name and your role, plus the branch you are viewing if you are an Org Owner or Org Admin.

The sheet shows a **Dashboard** tile, then each group (**People**, **Church life**, **Giving & outreach** and **Organisation**) as tiles you can tap. An **Apps** section holds **Porter** (if you have Porter access) and **What's New**. At the bottom are your account details with **Sign out**, **What's New**, **Cookie settings** and **Cookie Policy**.

<Note>
There is no branch selector in the phone More sheet. On a phone, use the branch chip in the top bar instead. See [Choose a branch](#choose-a-branch).
</Note>

## The top bar on every page

Every page has a bar along the top that stays in place as you scroll. From left to right, it can show:

- On a computer, the **Toggle Sidebar** button. See [Hide or show the sidebar](#hide-or-show-the-sidebar).
- A back arrow on detail pages, such as a single member or event, to return to the list.
- The page title, sometimes with a short line of extra detail underneath.
- On tablets and phones, the branch chip, for Org Owners and Org Admins on pages that show one branch at a time.
- The page's main button, such as an add button.
- A button with three dots (**More actions**) that holds the page's other actions.

On a phone, the **More actions** menu and other menus open as a sheet from the bottom of the screen. On tablets and computers they open as a drop-down list.

## Choose a branch

<Info>
**Who can do this:** Org Owner, Org Admin
</Info>

Most pages show one branch at a time. Bethel ChMS has one branch selector for the whole app, so you choose a branch once and every page follows it.

- **On a computer:** Click the branch name at the top of the sidebar, under your organisation name, and choose a branch from the list.
- **On a tablet or phone:** Tap the round chip in the top bar that shows the branch name. The **Branch** list opens with the current branch marked. Tap the branch you want. On a tablet held portrait, the selector is also in the **More** drawer.

Branches are listed in alphabetical order. When you choose a branch, the page you are on switches to that branch.

Bethel ChMS remembers your choice as you move between pages, and the next time you open the app in the same browser. The first time you use Bethel ChMS in a browser, the first branch in alphabetical order is selected.

Branch Admins, Editors, Treasurers and Care Managers don't see a branch selector. They always work in their own branch, because each branch owns its own data. Org Owners and Org Admins look after the whole organisation, so they can move between branches.

## When a page is loading

When you open another page, or use your browser's back and forward buttons, and the page takes more than a moment to load, the page area dims and shows the Bethel ChMS mark with a spinning ring and a message such as **Loading Members…**.

While this is showing, clicks on the page are paused until the new page appears, so a slow link can't be opened twice. The sidebar, the tablet rail and the phone tab bar stay visible and work as normal. If a page never arrives, for example because your connection dropped, the message clears itself after 10 seconds so you can try again.

## Banners and messages

Some messages appear as a banner above the top bar.

- **Install banner:** Invites you to add Bethel ChMS to your home screen. See [Install the app](/getting-started/install-the-app).
- **Updated terms:** When the Terms and Conditions or Privacy Policy change, a banner asks you to review them. Click **Review** (or **Review & Accept** on wider screens). The **Updated Terms** window links to the **Terms and Conditions** and **Privacy Policy**. Tick **I agree to the updated Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy** and click **Accept**.
- **Failed payment:** If your church's latest payment didn't go through, the Org Owner sees a banner with a **Manage Billing** button to update the card. You can hide the banner for now with its **X** button. See [Billing and subscription](/guides/billing-and-subscription).

### Cookie choices

The first time you visit, a small **Cookie Preferences** card appears at the bottom of the screen. Choose **Reject Non-Essential** or **Accept All**. You can keep using the page while the card is open.

To change your mind later, click **Cookies & privacy** at the bottom of the sidebar (or **Cookie settings** in the **More** menu on a tablet or phone). Strictly necessary cookies are always on. Turn the **Analytics** switch on or off, then click **Save Preferences**. To read how Bethel ChMS uses cookies, click the **Cookie Policy** link in the same window.

## Using Bethel ChMS on a touch screen

On phones and tablets, Bethel ChMS makes a few things easier to tap:

- Buttons and fields are larger, and text boxes use a size that stops your phone from zooming in while you type.
- On a phone, forms and windows slide up from the bottom of the screen, with their buttons fixed at the bottom so they stay within reach. On tablets and computers, most forms open as a panel from the right.
- Lists with filters have a **Filters** button (on a phone, just the filter icon). It opens a sheet where you choose your filters, then tap the button at the bottom to see the results (it shows how many match, for example **Show 46 members**). Tap **Clear all** to start again.
- In lists you can put in order, you don't need to drag rows. Tap **Reorder** to add up and down arrow buttons to each row, and tap **Done** when you've finished. On some lists, such as tags, you'll also find **Move up** and **Move down** in a row's menu.
- When you export a file, such as a CSV or PDF, your device's share options open, so you can, for example, save it to Files, email it or send it with AirDrop.

## Sign out

- **On a computer:** Click the sign-out icon at the bottom of the sidebar, next to your email.
- **On a tablet or phone:** Open **More** and tap **Sign out**.

To sign back in, go to [app.bethelchms.com](https://app.bethelchms.com). See [Sign in](/getting-started/sign-in).

<Note>
**If you've used Bethel ChMS before:** the sidebar used to be a single list. Pages are now grouped under **People**, **Church life**, **Giving & outreach** and **Organisation**. The organisation-wide dashboard is now called **Organisation dashboard** and sits in the **Organisation** group. **Sign out** is now a button at the bottom of the sidebar rather than an item in a menu, and pages no longer have their own branch selectors.
</Note>
