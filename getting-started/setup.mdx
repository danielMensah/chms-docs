This guide walks you through setting up a new church in Bethel ChMS. By the end, you'll have an organisation, a paid subscription, a branch, a primary location, and a full year of weekly Sunday services ready to go.

<Info>
**Who can do this:** Anyone setting up a new church. Whoever creates the organisation becomes its **Org Owner**, the highest level of access in Bethel ChMS, and is the only person who can complete the payment step.
</Info>

<Note>
If someone has already set up your church and sent you an invitation, you don't need this guide. Open the invitation email and follow [What the invitee sees](/getting-started/invite-users#what-the-invitee-sees) instead.
</Note>

## Choose how to get started

You can start in one of two ways:

- **Sign up yourself.** Go to [app.bethelchms.com](https://app.bethelchms.com) and follow the steps below. This takes a few minutes.
- **Talk to us first.** On the Bethel ChMS website, click **Get started**. Fill in the short form with your **Full name**, **Email address**, **Church or organisation** and **Number of branches**, add anything you'd like us to know, and click **Send message**. The team replies by email to help you get set up.

## Set up step by step

The setup wizard has four steps: **Create Organisation**, **Payment**, **Set Up Your First Branch** and **You're All Set**. A progress bar at the top shows where you are. On a phone it reads, for example, "Step 2 of 4" with the name of the next step. The **Back** and continue buttons stay pinned to the bottom of the screen.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [app.bethelchms.com](https://app.bethelchms.com). The **Welcome back** sign-in page opens. Next to **Don't have an account?**, click **Sign up**.

    On the **Create your account** page:

    1. Enter your **Email**.
    2. Choose a **Password**. Make it at least 12 characters long, with upper-case and lower-case letters and at least one number, so it's accepted first time. Click the eye icon in the field if you want to check what you typed.
    3. Tick **I agree to the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy**. You can open either document from the links, then use the back arrow to return.
    4. Wait for the security check above the button to finish. It usually completes by itself.
    5. Click **Sign up**.

    The page then shows **Check your email for a confirmation link.** If Bethel ChMS asks you to confirm your email address, open the link in that email by clicking **Confirm My Email**.

    Then go to [app.bethelchms.com](https://app.bethelchms.com) and, if you're asked to, sign in with your new email and password. Because you haven't set up your church yet, Bethel ChMS opens the setup wizard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your organisation">
    An **organisation** is your church network: it contains all your branches, members, and settings. If you lead a single church, your organisation is that church. If you oversee several campuses, your organisation is the umbrella that holds them all together.

    Fill in the following details:

    | Field | Required | Description |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | **Organisation Name** | Yes | The name of your church or network, at least 2 characters. |
    | **Email** | Yes | A contact email for the organisation. |
    | **Address** | No | The main address of your church or headquarters. |
    | **Post Code** | No | The postcode for the address above. |

    Click **Create & Continue**.

    If you see **Organisation name already exists**, another organisation in Bethel ChMS already uses that name. Try a slightly different name, for example by adding your town.

    <Info>
      You automatically become the **Org Owner**. You can manage everything across all branches, invite other users, and assign roles. See [Roles and permissions](/guides/roles-and-permissions) for details.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a billing period and pay">
    The **Payment** step sets up your subscription. The **Your plan** card shows the plan and its price per branch, with VAT included.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/getting-started/setup-payment-step.png" alt="The Payment step of the Bethel ChMS setup wizard, showing the four-step progress bar, the Your plan card at £80 per branch with VAT included, Monthly and Annual billing period choices, the Monthly total, and the Back and Continue to payment buttons" width="480" />
    </Frame>

    1. Under **Billing period**, choose **Monthly** (£80 a month) or **Annual** (£880 a year, marked **1 month free**).
    2. Check the **Monthly total** or **Annual total** line.
    3. Click **Continue to payment**.
    4. You're taken to Stripe, our secure payment provider. Enter your card details on Stripe's page, not in Bethel ChMS.

    After you pay, Stripe brings you back to a page that says **Confirming your payment…**. Keep this page open. It usually takes a moment, then shows **Payment confirmed!** and moves you on to the branch step. Your subscription starts as soon as payment is confirmed.

    <Note>
      If you see **This is taking longer than usual…**, your payment may still have gone through. Click **Refresh** to check again. If it keeps happening, use the **contact support** link or email [hello@bethelware.app](mailto:hello@bethelware.app).
    </Note>

    <Warning>
      Your organisation is saved as soon as you click **Create & Continue** in the previous step, so don't create it again. If you leave the Stripe page without paying, or you end up back on the **Create Organisation** step, email [hello@bethelware.app](mailto:hello@bethelware.app) and we'll help you finish setting up.
    </Warning>

    To learn more about your subscription, see [Billing and subscription](/guides/billing-and-subscription).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up your first branch">
    A **branch** is an individual church or campus within your organisation. Even if you only have one church, Bethel ChMS uses branches to organise your members, events, and attendance.

    This step has three sections:

    | Section | Field | Required | Description |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | **Branch** | **Branch Name** | Yes | The name of this church or campus, such as "Grace Chapel" or "Eastside Campus", at least 2 characters. |
    | **Branch** | **Branch Email** | No | A contact email for this branch. |
    | **Primary Location** | **Location Name** | Yes | The venue or building where this branch meets, such as "Main Hall". |
    | **Primary Location** | **Address** | No | The address of that venue. |
    | **Primary Location** | **Capacity** | No | How many people the venue holds. |
    | **Sunday Service** | **Start Time** | Yes | When your Sunday service begins. It is filled in as 10:00, so change it if your service starts at a different time. |
    | **Sunday Service** | **End Time** | Yes | When your Sunday service ends. It is filled in as 12:00 and must be after the start time. |
    | **Sunday Service** | **Description** | No | Fills in as "Weekly Sunday Service at" followed by your branch name. Change it if you like. |

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/getting-started/setup-first-branch-step.png" alt="The Set Up Your First Branch step of the Bethel ChMS setup wizard, with the Branch, Primary Location and Sunday Service sections filled in, the Sunday service set to 10:00 to 12:00, and the Back and Create & Continue buttons" width="480" />
    </Frame>

    Click **Create & Continue**.

    <Tip>
      Bethel ChMS creates your branch, its primary location, and a **Sunday Service** event that repeats every week for the next 52 weeks. You can change the service or add more events later in [Managing events](/guides/managing-events), and add more venues in [Locations](/guides/locations).
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="You're all set">
    The **You're All Set** page confirms that your workspace is ready and shows the **Organisation** and **Branch** it created. When you're ready, click **Go to Dashboard**.

    Your dashboard shows your selected branch at a glance, including member demographics, upcoming events, recent check-ins, and member lists.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If you get stuck at any point during setup, email [hello@bethelware.app](mailto:hello@bethelware.app) and we'll help you finish.
</Note>
