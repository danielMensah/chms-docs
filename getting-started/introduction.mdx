Bethel ChMS is a church management system built for churches of every size, from a single congregation to a multi-branch network. It gives pastors, office staff and branch administrators one place to manage the people and activities that keep your church running.

You use Bethel ChMS in your web browser at [app.bethelchms.com](https://app.bethelchms.com), on a computer, tablet or phone. The layout adapts to each screen, and you can [install it on your device](/getting-started/install-the-app) so it opens like any other app.

## What you can do with Bethel ChMS

- **Manage members and households:** Keep contact details up to date, link families together and organise people by branch. See [Managing members](/guides/managing-members).
- **Welcome visitors:** Record first-time guests, follow them up and turn them into members when they join. See [Visitors](/guides/visitors).
- **Care for your people:** Spot members who have stopped attending, follow up new visitors and keep track of pastoral needs. See [Care](/guides/care).
- **Track attendance and check-in:** Take attendance for each service or meeting, or let people check themselves in at the door. See [Recording attendance](/guides/recording-attendance) and [Kiosk mode](/guides/kiosk-mode).
- **Run events, groups and ministries:** Schedule services and meetings, and organise your small groups and ministry teams. See [Managing events](/guides/managing-events).
- **Keep records current:** Send members a form to check and update their own details. See [Update campaigns](/guides/update-campaigns).
- **Record giving:** Log gifts and donations and see how giving is going. See [Gifting and donations](/guides/gifting-and-donations).
- **See the bigger picture:** Use dashboards and reports to follow trends in a branch or across your whole organisation. See [Dashboards](/guides/dashboards) and [Reports](/guides/reports).
- **Support your service teams with Porter:** Let workers and volunteers check in on site, loan and return equipment by scanning labels, and report faults so your admins are alerted. See [Porter](/guides/porter).

<Note>
**What you'll need to get started:** an email address, a payment card to start your subscription (you pay securely through Stripe during setup) and a few minutes. See [Set up your church](/getting-started/setup) and [Billing and subscription](/guides/billing-and-subscription).
</Note>

## Key terms

Before you dive in, here are a few terms you'll see throughout Bethel ChMS:

- **Organisation:** Your church network. It contains all your branches, members and settings.
- **Branch:** An individual church or campus within your organisation. Even a single church has one branch.
- **Member:** A person in your church directory. Each member has a member record with their details, attendance and history.
- **Branch scope:** The branch you are viewing or managing. Org Owners and Org Admins can switch between branches with the branch selector, at the top of the sidebar on a computer or in the top bar on a tablet or phone. Everyone else always works in their own assigned branch. See [Find your way around](/getting-started/find-your-way-around#choose-a-branch).
- **Role:** The level of access a person has in Bethel ChMS: **Org Owner**, **Org Admin**, **Branch Admin**, **Editor**, **Treasurer** or **Care Manager**. See [Roles and permissions](/guides/roles-and-permissions).
- **Porter:** The part of Bethel ChMS that your church's workers and volunteers use on their phones, for on-site check-in, equipment loans and fault reports. The Bethel ChMS team switches Porter on for your church on request.
- **Porter access level:** The access a person has in Porter, either **Porter · Worker** or **Porter · Admin**. A Porter access level belongs to the person's member record and is separate from their Bethel ChMS role, so someone can use Porter without having any access to the rest of Bethel ChMS.
